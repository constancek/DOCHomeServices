# Taylor Mill, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'taylor-mill'):**
- "a hilltop residential Kenton County city south of Covington" is incomplete. Covington borders it to the north, west and south, and an eastern strip runs along the Licking River. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Mill,_Kentucky
- "mid-century and newer single-family homes" holds up (median 1975; 31.4% built 1940-59, 39.4% built 1980-99), but 16.4% of units are in buildings of 5+ units (section 2).
- "hillside grade adds demand on sump pumps": **unverified** as a local fact. Don't use.
- "every day, Sundays too" is a company claim; check stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in Kenton County, ZIP 41015. 2020 population 6,873; 6.20 sq mi. Incorporated 1956; merged with Winston Park in 1972. Borders Covington (north, west, south) and Fairview and Ryland Heights (southeast); Licking River to the east. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Mill,_Kentucky

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits: **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Taylor Mill by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector, office at Boone County Health Center, 7505 Burlington Pike, Florence. Same source.
- Electrical: the state list only says to contact the County Judge Executive or the city office. Local electrical office: **unverified**. Same source.
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: not researched; don't use.
