# Villa Hills, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'villa-hills'): ZIP 41017, Kenton County, "hilltop residential" city "above the river", "mid-century and newer single-family homes", hillside grade adding demand on sump pumps and drains.
**Errors in locations.ts:** "mid-century" overstates the age. Median year built is 1981 and only 3.5% of units predate 1960 (ACS below); "homes built from the 1960s to the 1990s" fits. The sump pump / hillside-grade claim has no source: **unverified**. No `adjacent` list (Crescent Springs, Fort Mitchell and Erlanger are nearby; borders **unverified**). "Every day, Sundays too" is an hours claim the brief keeps out of body copy.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in Kenton County along the Ohio River; 2020 census population 7,310. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Hills,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- Census geocoder places 719 Rogers Rd (city building) and 2800 Amsterdam Rd in Villa Hills city (place 2179698), tracts 672 and 640.01. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 41017 is shared with Crescent Springs, Lakeside Park, Crestview Hills, Edgewood and Fort Mitchell, so ZIP-level data mixes towns. Same geocoder results.

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits: **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Villa Hills by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector, office at the Boone County Health Center, 7505 Burlington Pike, Florence. Same source (state plumbing inspections, from _shared.md).
- Electrical: the state list only says to contact the County Judge Executive's office or the city office where work is done. Ludlow's site describes PDS as a one-stop shop that includes electrical permits, but who issues electrical permits in Villa Hills is **unverified**. https://www.ludlow.org/government/departments/code-enforcement.aspx
- Kentucky requires a permit for replacing a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: **unverified**, don't use.
