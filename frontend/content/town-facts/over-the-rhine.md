## Where it is
- Over-the-Rhine is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, just north of downtown. Its ZIP is 45202. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Census/2010/Over-the-Rhine.pdf
- The city counts four census tracts as OTR: 9, 10, 16 and 17. 2020 Census population: 5,622 (down from 6,064 in 2010). https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/OverTheRhine_2020.pdf (figures came from a search result because the city site refused connections; re-check them)

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Hamilton County tracts 9, 10, 16 and 17 added together)
Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061000900-census-tract-9-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061001000-census-tract-10-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061001600-census-tract-16-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061001700-census-tract-17-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25001, B25003, B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040)
- Housing units: 4,208. Occupied: 3,343.
- Median year built is 1938 in all four tracts. That is the ACS floor value, so read it as "1939 or earlier" rather than an exact year.
- 2,951 of 4,208 units (70.1%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 522 units (12.4%) date from 2000 or later, and 240 of those from 2020 or later.
- Units in structure: 361 single-family detached, 239 attached (rowhouse), 324 two-family, 1,078 in 3-4 unit buildings, 832 in 5-9, 436 in 10-19, 507 in 20-49, 370 in 50+, and 61 mobile homes. So 85.7% of units sit in buildings with two or more units.
- Tenure: 2,340 of 3,343 occupied units are rented (70.0%), and 1,003 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: electricity 1,589 (47.5%), utility gas 1,557 (46.6%), bottled gas 122, other 75.
- For comparison, ZIP 45202 as a whole (downtown included): 11,940 units, 73.6% rented, and 55.0% heated by electricity. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45202-45202/

## Permits
- The City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building and mechanical permits for work at OTR addresses. A mechanical permit is required to install or replace an air conditioner. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections

## Historic district
- The Over-the-Rhine Historic District was listed on the National Register on 17 May 1983 (ref. 83001985), roughly bounded by Central Parkway, Reading Road, Liberty, Sycamore and Mulberry Streets. A boundary increase was later nominated. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/362674c3-1e0a-4626-9f94-f03e6df460b5
- Exterior changes in the city's local historic districts need a Certificate of Appropriateness. The Urban Conservator reviews it, and the Historic Conservation Board hears the case. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-historic-landmarks ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/historic-conservation-board/february-5-2024-case-materials-and-staff-report/
- Unverified: the exact rule for outdoor equipment. A 2020 staff report quotes the OTR guidelines: "vents, skylights, and roof top utilities should be inconspicuously placed or screened where necessary." That wording came from a search result and was not read in the adopted guidelines. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation/historic-conservation-board/december-7-2020-staff-report-and-attachments/
