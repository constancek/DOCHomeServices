## Where it is
- Mount Washington is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood on the city's eastern edge in Hamilton County, and it borders Anderson Township. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Washington,_Cincinnati
- History: John L. Corbly laid it out in 1846, it incorporated as a village in 1867, and Cincinnati annexed it in 1911. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Neighborhood%20Plans/Mt%20Washington/2007%20Mt%20Washington%20Comprehensive%20Plan.pdf
- The city's statistical approximation uses Census Tracts 46.02, 46.03, 46.04 and 46.05, plus one block group of Tract 250.01. The 2020 Census total for that area was 15,357. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/MtWashington_2020.1.pdf (read from a search snippet, because the city site refused connections)
- Census geocoder results:
  - 1925 Sutton Ave, 6100 Campus Ln, 2000 and 6200 Beechmont Ave: Tract 46.02
  - 1500 Sutton Ave and 1600 Beacon St: Tract 46.03
  - 2350 Beechmont Ave and 6300 Corbly Rd: Tract 46.04

  All of these addresses geocode inside Cincinnati city. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45230 also covers parts of Anderson Township and California, so don't use ZIP figures as neighbourhood figures (unverified).

## Other local factors
- Terrain: Mount Washington is hilltop and hillside. In September 2018, heavy rain caused a landslide on Beechmont Hill that closed Beechmont Avenue, and Duke crews replaced a utility pole. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/beechmont-avenue-reopening-after-landslide
- WCPO's reporting names Mount Washington among the areas with backyard slides. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/living-with-landslides-documentary-shows-the-history-of-cincinnatis-hills-and-their-dangers
