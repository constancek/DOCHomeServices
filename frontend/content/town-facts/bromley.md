## 1. Where it is

## 4. Flood (changes the job)
- NWS Cincinnati gauge: flood stage 52 ft; record 80.0 ft on 1937-01-26. https://www.weather.gov/iln/1937ohioriverflood
- 1937: one third of Bromley under water by Jan 21; 70% under water at the crest; 600 residents displaced. https://www.kentonlibrary.org/genealogy/community-history/bromley-flood-1937/
- April 2025 (Ohio at 60.7 ft per NWS): mayor Mike Denham estimated 20 to 30 basements flooded; KY-8 closed; a few dozen residents evacuated. https://linknky.com/news/2025/04/07/region-hit-with-historic-flooding-what-to-know-how-to-help/
- 2018: 140 homes and businesses affected across Bromley and Ludlow; water stayed 4 to 5 days in places. https://local12.com/news/local/cleaning-up-after-high-flood-water-hits-nky-communities
- 1997 crest 64.7 ft with 133 structures in the flood zone: **unverified** (library page did not load; seen in a search excerpt only). https://www.kentonlibrary.org/genealogy/community-history/bromley-flood-1997/
