# College Hill (Cincinnati, OH 45224): verified local facts (shared by all services)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` lists Clifton as an adjacent neighbourhood. That could not be confirmed and looks unlikely. College Hill is in the city's far northwest, and Clifton is near the University of Cincinnati, several neighbourhoods south. Treat Clifton as unverified. The Northside adjacency is unverified too. The only confirmed neighbour is North College Hill to the north, which is a separate city outside Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Hill,_Cincinnati
- "Large early-1900s homes and mid-century houses" fits the data. About a third of homes predate 1940 and most of the rest were built in the 1940s to 1960s (Census, below). "Hilltop" is unverified: no source checked describes the terrain.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, in the northwest of the city, in Hamilton County. Hamilton Avenue (US-127) is the main corridor. It became a village in 1866 and was annexed by the city in 1911, 1915 and 1923. 2020 Census population: 16,039. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Hill,_Cincinnati
- The city's 2010 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation used Census Tracts 81 (part), 82.01, 82.02, 84, 111 (part) and 221.02. The 2020 tract list was not confirmed because the city site would not load. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Census/2010/CollegeHill.pdf (from a search summary, unverified)

## Permits
- College Hill is inside the City of Cincinnati, so the City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building, mechanical, plumbing and electrical permits. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)
- July normal high 86F, normal low 65-66F (1991-2020 normals). https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf ; https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- On average 21 days a year reach 90F or higher (1981-2010 normals). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
