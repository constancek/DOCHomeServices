# South Fairmount (Cincinnati, OH 45214): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives the ZIP as 45205. The Census geocoder puts addresses in the neighbourhood core in 45214 (1700, 1900, 2000 and 2100 Queen City Ave; 1700 and 1900 Harrison Ave), and Wikipedia's infobox also says 45214. Use 45214. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/address?street=1900+Queen+City+Ave&city=Cincinnati&state=OH&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&layers=Census%20Tracts&format=json ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Fairmount,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- `locations.ts` says the area was "rebuilt around the Lick Run Greenway stormwater project after a long history of valley flooding". The greenway is real (see below), but its stated purpose is cutting combined sewer overflows into Mill Creek. A "long history of valley flooding" in South Fairmount is unverified. FEMA flood zones were not checked. https://www.wvxu.org/post/south-fairmounts-lick-run-greenway-designed-help-msd-reduce-sewage-overflows
- The entry's FAQ says "on call every day, on weekdays and weekends alike", which is a 7-day claim by another name.

## Where it is
- South Fairmount is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, on the west side. The 2020 Census counted 2,181 residents. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Fairmount,_Cincinnati (to locate the source; the city's 2020 Census neighbourhood PDF could not be reached because cincinnati-oh.gov refused connections)
- Neighbours, per Wikipedia: Camp Washington, East Price Hill, Lower Price Hill, North Fairmount, Queensgate, West Price Hill and Westwood. Unverified against a city map. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Fairmount,_Cincinnati
- Lick Run once flowed between Queen City Avenue and Westwood Avenue. It was buried about 100 years ago and piped straight to Mill Creek. https://www.wvxu.org/post/south-fairmounts-lick-run-greenway-designed-help-msd-reduce-sewage-overflows
- Annexation date: unverified.

Queen City Ave from the 2200 block west falls in Tract 100.03, which is mostly Westwood (median year built 1968) and is not used here. Margins of error are large at this size. ## Other local factors
- Lick Run Greenway: a $103 million MSD project, completion marked in May 2021, built under a federal Clean Water Act consent decree to divert about a billion gallons of sewage a year from Mill Creek. Ohio EPA referred to "localized flooding and basement backups" in Mill Creek neighbourhoods. https://www.wvxu.org/post/south-fairmounts-lick-run-greenway-designed-help-msd-reduce-sewage-overflows . MSD's own page (msdgc.org) refused connections, and its overflow figure (reported elsewhere as 800 million gallons) is unverified.
- Basement backups in South Fairmount homes after the greenway opened: unverified.
- Historic districts: none found. Wikipedia says the Lunkenheimer Valve site was designated a national historic site in January 2026 (unverified; not found on NPS). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Fairmount,_Cincinnati
- FEMA flood zone status: not checked.
