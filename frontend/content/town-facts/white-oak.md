# White Oak (Hamilton County, OH 45247 / 45239): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls White Oak a "Green Township community". It is split between two townships. The Census Geocoder puts 5800 and 6000 Cheviot Rd and 3200 Jessup Rd in Green Township, but 3500 Blue Rock Rd in Colerain Township. All four addresses are in White Oak CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Oak,_Ohio
- `zip: '45247'` is only half of it. The geocoder returns 45239 for Jessup Rd and Blue Rock Rd, and Wikipedia lists both 45239 and 45247. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Oak,_Ohio
- "Largely mid-century and newer single-family homes" is about right (median build year 1968, 71.8% single-family detached). It leaves out the 19.2% of units in buildings of 5 or more units. Copy should not say "all single-family". See the housing block.

## Where it is
- White Oak is an unincorporated census-designated place (CDP) in Green and Colerain townships, Hamilton County. Area 6.21 sq mi. 2020 Census population 19,541. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Oak,_Ohio
- CDP place id 16000US3984812. Tracts 207.63 (Green Township side) and 207.64 (Colerain side, Blue Rock Rd) both geocode into it. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

- Electrical: Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI) (from _shared.md).
- HVAC/mechanical permits: unverified. Only a commercial permit aggregator said the county issues them, and that isn't an acceptable source. Don't name an office for HVAC permits.
- Zoning, Colerain side: Colerain Township does its own zoning. A zoning approval, usually a zoning certificate, has to be filed with the county building permit application. https://hamilton-co.org/i_want_to/pre_development_meeting ; https://energyzoning.org/sites/default/files/PDF/3906116616_Colerain%20Township_Hamilton_20220920.pdf
