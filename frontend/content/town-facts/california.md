# California (Cincinnati, OH 45230): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` puts California "near Riverbend and the old Coney Island grounds". Riverbend Music Center, Belterra Park and the Coney Island site are next to California but in Anderson Township, not in the neighbourhood. Don't imply they're inside it. (The Census geocoder places 6201 Kellogg Ave inside city limits, which conflicts with this. Treat the exact line as unverified.) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California,_Cincinnati
- "Older homes near Riverbend carry aging plumbing" is overstated. The block group's median year built is 1968, and only 12.2% of homes predate 1940 (see below). Mid-century housing is a more accurate description. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610275003-bg-3-tract-275-hamilton-oh/
- "Basement backups the repeat emergency" is unverified as a local statistic. NWS flood impacts do confirm basement and yard flooding in California at high river stages (see Flooding).
- The entry has no neighbour list. The real neighbours are Mount Washington, East End and Anderson Township. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California,_Cincinnati

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45230. It is the city's southeasternmost neighbourhood, where the Little Miami meets the Ohio River. Cincinnati annexed it in 1909. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California,_Cincinnati
- 2020 Census: 944 residents, 452 housing units and 323 households. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California,_Cincinnati (city profile at https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/California_2020.1.pdf refused the connection, so it is not checked)
- Kellogg Avenue is the main road. GCWW's Richard Miller Treatment Plant is at 5651 Kellogg Ave, 45230. It treats Ohio River water and supplies about 88% of GCWW's drinking water. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/water/assets/File/Tours/WaterTreatmentAndWaterHistoryMuseumTourGuidelines.pdf ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/water/water-quality-and-treatment/water-quality-reports/latest/
- The neighbourhood includes California Woods Nature Preserve. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California,_Cincinnati

## Census geography
- The Census geocoder puts 5400, 5550, 5651, 5900 and 6000 Kellogg Ave in Cincinnati city, Tract 275, Block Group 3 (390610275003). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Block Groups 1 and 2 of Tract 275 fall in Anderson Township, so don't use tract-level figures for California. (The full tract has a median year built of 1981 and is 99% single-family.) Block Group 3 is the California proxy, but its exact match to the neighbourhood boundary is unverified. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061027500-census-tract-275-hamilton-oh/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Tract 275 Block Group 3)
Margins are wide. For example, population is 750 ±183. Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610275003-bg-3-tract-275-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1968 (±26 years).
- Of 369 units, 45 (12.2%) were built in 1939 or earlier, 155 (42.0%) in 1940-1969, 116 (31.4%) in 1970-1999 and 53 (14.4%) in 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 356 single-family detached and 13 mobile homes. There are no multi-unit buildings.
- Tenure: 294 of 334 occupied units are owner-occupied (88.0%) and 40 are rented (12.0%).
- Heating fuel: 257 utility gas (76.9%), 43 electricity (12.9%), 31 propane (9.3%) and 3 fuel oil.

## Flooding (Ohio River at Cincinnati, gauge CCNO1)
All of the following comes from NWS impact statements at https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1
- Flood stages: action 40 ft, flood 52 ft, moderate 56 ft, major 65 ft.
- At 47 ft, high water reaches some yards in California. At 48-49 ft it reaches Eldorado Avenue and the land between Eldorado and the river.
- From 50-52 ft, parts of Kellogg Avenue flood from east of Delta Avenue to near Eight Mile Road.
- At 56.5 ft, "significant flooding of California occurs", with Little Miami backwater flooding basements and some first floors.
- At 59 ft, "some homes incur flooding in California" and "basement flooding of low lying homes and businesses is widespread".
- From 63-72 ft, NWS lists California with the East End among the worst-hit areas.
- Record crest: 80 ft (Jan 1937). Recent crests: 60.91 ft (7 April 2025) and 55.46 ft (19 Feb 2025), reused from riverside.md.
- FEMA flood zone status for individual lots was not checked. Unverified.
