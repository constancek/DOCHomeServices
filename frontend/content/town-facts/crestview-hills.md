# Crestview Hills, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'crestview-hills'): ZIP 41017, Kenton County city "home to Thomas More University and the Crestview Hills mall", homes beside a commercial corridor, "mid-century and newer houses".
**Errors in locations.ts:** "mid-century" is wrong. Median year built is 1984, 56.9% of units were built 1980-99 and only 4.3% before 1960. The housing is also unusual: 26.3% single-family attached (condos and townhomes) and only 43.4% detached. "Mall" as the name of the Town Center Blvd retail area is **unverified**. Thomas More University is confirmed inside the city (geocoder below). No `adjacent` list (Edgewood, Lakeside Park, Fort Mitchell nearby; borders **unverified**). "Any day of the week" is an hours claim the brief keeps out of body copy.
