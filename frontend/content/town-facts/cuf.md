# CUF (Clifton Heights, University Heights, Fairview), Cincinnati, OH: verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives ZIP 45219 only. Census geocoder matches put 2700 Stratford Ave, 2800 Digby Ave and 3101 Clifton Ave (Hebrew Union College) in 45220, and 2400 W McMicken Ave in 45214. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- `county: 'Cincinnati Neighborhoods'` is not a county. CUF is in Hamilton County. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002600-census-tract-26-hamilton-oh/
- "full of 19th- and early-1900s homes" overstates it. 42.3% of units predate 1940; 48.6% were built 1940-1999 (the 1970s alone: 1,183 units). See tables below.
- "largely converted to student rentals": 84.5% of occupied units are rented (ACS). The student share and "converted" are unverified.
- "aging galvanized lines" and "shared stacks": unverified.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighborhoods, south and west of UC's main campus. University Heights is north of Straight Street; Fairview (west) and Clifton Heights (east) are split by Ravine Street. Borders Clifton, Mount Auburn, Over-the-Rhine, Camp Washington and the UC campus. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUF,_Cincinnati (locator only)
- 2020 Census: 20,385 residents, 7,517 housing units (Wikipedia summary of the city profile; city PDF not checked, so unverified). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUF,_Cincinnati

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Hamilton County tracts 25, 26, 27, 29.01, 29.02, 30)
Tracts picked by geocoding real addresses; tract 28 (centred in Camp Washington) excluded. The city's official tract list is unverified. Tract 30 includes UC campus housing. The 7,224-unit total is close to the 2020 count of 7,517. Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002500-census-tract-25-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002600-census-tract-26-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002700-census-tract-27-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002901-census-tract-2901-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002902-census-tract-2902-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061003000-census-tract-30-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040)
- Median year built by tract: 1938 (26, 27), 1952 (29.01), 1961 (30), 1971 (29.02), 1975 (25).
- Year built, 7,224 units: 3,058 built 1939 or earlier (42.3%); 3,509 built 1940-1999 (48.6%); 657 built 2000 or later (9.1%).
- Units in structure: 1,978 single-family detached (27.4%), 86 attached, 801 two-family (11.1%), 1,045 in 3-4 unit buildings (14.5%), 537 in 5-9, 928 in 10-19, 864 in 20-49, 923 in 50 or more (12.8%), 62 mobile homes.
- Tenure: 5,231 of 6,192 occupied units are rented (84.5%); 961 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 3,342 utility gas (54.0%), 2,636 electricity (42.6%), 141 propane, 26 oil.
- By area: the old-house core is tracts 26 and 27 (Clifton Heights west and Fairview), where 62.7% and 72.7% of units predate 1940. Tract 25 and 29.02 lean to large 1970s-era buildings.

## Other local factors
- Steep terrain. A hillside cut for an apartment project at W McMicken Ave and Straight Street was followed by cracks in Straight Street (2023-24) and an engineered retaining wall. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/finding-solutions/someone-needs-to-be-accountable-cuf-residents-landslide-triggered-by-housing-construction-near-uc
