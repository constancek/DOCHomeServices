**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'terrace-park'):**
- "early-1900s homes" is wrong as a description of the village. Median year built is 1954; only 25.6% of homes were built 1939 or earlier, and 42.4% were built 1940-59 (section 2).
- "low ground near the river adds a flood and backup risk in heavy rain": unverified. No source found tying Terrace Park homes to flooding or sewer backups. Many homes use drywells or septic rather than public sewer (section 4), so "sewer backup" framing may not fit. Check parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
- "affluent", "tree-lined streets": not verified and not job-relevant; drop.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in eastern Hamilton County, between Mariemont, Indian Hill and Milford, by the Little Miami River. Incorporated 1893; 2020 population 2,355; about 1.2 sq mi. ZIP 45174. School district: Mariemont City Schools. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrace_Park,_Ohio
- Census geography: place 16000US3976428 (Terrace Park village), tract 273. ZIP 45174 and tract 273 return the same profile as the village. Geocoded via `census.mjs` from 703 Elm Ave and 100 Miami Ave.

## 3. Permits
- **Building permits** (including HVAC work) are handled by **Hamilton County Buildings & Inspections**, not the village. The village guide says HVAC work does **not** need a village Zoning Certificate but may need a Building Permit, and gives "a new furnace" as an example of a project needing a Building Permit but no Zoning Certificate. Village "Building and Zoning Overview", revised May 14, 2021: https://www.terracepark.org/_files/ugd/ea86d7_87f6d48483de4ae783d0fded7af93b33.pdf ; page: https://www.terracepark.org/building-and-zoning
- **Zoning Certificate** from the Village of Terrace Park is needed first for new structures, expansions, fences, pools and similar (same PDF). Zoning code in force since June 13, 2023 (https://www.terracepark.org/building-and-zoning).
- **Electrical** and **plumbing** permit issuer for Terrace Park: **unverified**. (_shared.md lists IBI for electrical and Hamilton County Public Health for plumbing in unincorporated areas; not confirmed for this village.)
- Permit fees and turnaround: unverified, don't state.

## 4. Sewage: drywells and septic (changes plumbing jobs)
- Parts of the village use **traditional drywells/septic** rather than public sewer. Ohio's ban on these systems put enforcement with the Hamilton County Board of Health; a village sewer alternative was ruled out over Metropolitan Sewer District capital cost; the septic agreement with the Board of Health was finalized 4/11/2022. How many homes are on septic vs MSD sewer: **unverified**. https://www.terracepark.org/sewer-feasibility-committee-minutes
- Projects may need **Sewage Treatment System approval from Hamilton County Public Health**. https://www.terracepark.org/_files/ugd/ea86d7_87f6d48483de4ae783d0fded7af93b33.pdf ; fact sheet: https://www.hamiltoncountyhealth.org/wp-content/uploads/Property-Improvement-Factsheet-2023.pdf

## 5. Historic districts
- No National Register or local historic district found for the village (the NRHP "Terrace Park Historic District" is in Marion, Iowa). The village building page mentions no design review. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrace_Park_Historic_District ; https://www.terracepark.org/building-and-zoning
- The Terrace Park Historical Society runs a voluntary plaque program for homes built in or before 1920; it carries no permit rules. https://tphistoricalsociety.org/historic-homes
