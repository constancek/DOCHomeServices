# Mount Adams (Cincinnati, OH 45202): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls the housing "tightly packed 19th-century rowhouses and townhomes". ACS counts only 68 single-family attached units out of 1,068. The bigger groups are 2-unit buildings (214), 3-4 unit buildings (189) and detached houses (260). Say "older homes, doubles and small apartment buildings" rather than "rowhouses". https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026800-census-tract-268-hamilton-oh/
- The `adjacent` list includes Over-the-Rhine. Wikipedia lists Walnut Hills (north), East Walnut Hills (northeast) and the East End (east) as neighbours, with downtown to the west. Whether OTR touches Mount Adams is unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Adams,_Cincinnati
- The intro says the hillside "puts real pressure on sewer laterals and basement drains during heavy rain". No source was found for this, so it is unverified.

## Where it is
- Mount Adams is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods. It sits on a hill just east of downtown, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45202, with Eden Park immediately north. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Adams,_Cincinnati
- City of Cincinnati 2020 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation: Census Tract 268, 1,578 residents (1,481 in 2010), 1,146 housing units, 975 households. I could not open the city PDF directly (the server refused connections), so these figures come from Wikipedia's citation of it. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/MtAdams_2020.pdf
- The Mount Adams Incline ran from 1872 to 1948. The neighbourhood began to gentrify in the late 1960s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Adams,_Cincinnati

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 268, Hamilton County)
Tract 268 is the city's statistical stand-in for Mount Adams. Margins of error are wide, often 30-100% of the estimate. Source for every figure here: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026800-census-tract-268-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1938. Of 1,068 units, 536 (50.2%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 258 (24.2%) date from 1960-1979, and 131 (12.3%) from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 260 single-family detached, 68 attached, 214 two-unit, 189 in 3-4 unit buildings, 90 in 5-9, 57 in 10-19, 92 in 20-49, 98 in 50 or more. No mobile homes.
- Tenure: 672 of 1,016 occupied units are rented (66.1%), and 344 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 592 utility gas (58.3%), 408 electricity (40.2%), 16 propane.

## Permits
- Mount Adams is inside the City of Cincinnati, so the city's Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building, mechanical, plumbing and electrical permits. B&I handled the 2017 Baum Street slope case (see below). Mechanical permit rules come from the city's permit guide, as in the Sedamsville file. I could not reopen the guide this session, so trade-by-trade requirements and fees are unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2017-05-11/city-inspector-developers-work-led-to-landslide

## Terrain and other local factors
- Steep hillside. In May 2017, excavation at 406 Baum Street sent a gravity wall into 402 and 404 Baum Street, and both homes were ordered vacated. City inspectors said the work "didn't follow safe building operations". https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2017-05-11/city-inspector-developers-work-led-to-landslide
- Mud from the hillside has also closed a lane of Martin Drive. The date is unverified. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/this-is-mother-nature-for-you-rain-in-forecast-means-more-landslides-loom-over-the-tri-state
- The city has Hillside Overlay Districts and is reviewing them under the Cincinnati Hillside Initiative. Whether Mount Adams lots fall inside an overlay is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/projects/active/cincinnati-hillside-initiative/
- National Register listings include Immaculata Church (1978) and Rookwood Pottery (1972). No Mount Adams historic district, local or National Register, was confirmed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculata_Church ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rookwood_Pottery_Company
- Narrow streets and tight parking (from `locations.ts`) are unverified. No official source was found.
