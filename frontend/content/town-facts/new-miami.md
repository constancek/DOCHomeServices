# New Miami, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls New Miami a small Great Miami River village just north of Hamilton, ZIP 45011, "an old mill community with closely spaced older homes" on "low riverside ground that backs up in heavy rain". The Census data below backs "older homes" (median year built 1960). "Mill community" fits loosely: the village grew around coke ovens and a steel mill (see section 1). The backup and low-ground claims have no source: **unverified**, don't use. The ZIP is incomplete: the village also uses 45013.

## 1. Where it is
- Village (incorporated 1929) in St. Clair Township, central Butler County, north of Hamilton. 2020 population 2,217. ZIPs 45011 and 45013. Elevation 591 ft. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Miami,_Ohio
- History: first known as Coke Otto / Otto. United Coke and Gas built coke ovens around 1905 for Hamilton Otto Coke; American Rolling Mill Co. bought the site in 1937 (ARMCO from 1948). (same source)
- Village office (Town Hall): 268 Whitaker Ave. The village runs its own Water Department; it lists "Permitting and Licensing" and property maintenance code enforcement among its services. https://villageofnewmiami.com
- Census geocoder: 158 S Riverside Dr and 268 Whitaker Ave both fall in New Miami village, tract 105, St. Clair Township (run via census.mjs).
