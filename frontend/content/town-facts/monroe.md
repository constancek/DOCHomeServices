## 1. Where it is
- City in east-central Butler County and west-central Warren County, in Lemon, Liberty and Turtlecreek townships. 2020 population 15,412 (2010: 12,442). ZIPs 45050, 45044 (part), 45073, 45099. Elevation 820 ft. Laid out in 1817 on the Dayton and Cincinnati pike. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroe,_Ohio
- Census geocoder: 233 S Main St and 100 Breaden Dr both fall in Monroe city, tract 112, Lemon Township, Butler County (run via census.mjs). A Warren County Monroe address was not geocoded.

## 3. Permits (the city, not either county)
- "The Development Department is responsible for issuing both zoning and building permits for projects in the City of Monroe." https://www.monroeohio.gov/1528/Building-in-Monroe
- The city contracts with **National Inspection Corporation (NIC)** for building plan review and inspections; NIC handles the Building Permit Application. https://www.monroeohio.gov/270/Building-and-Zoning and https://www.monroeohio.gov/191/Forms
- **Plumbing:** "The Butler County Health Department handles all plumbing permits within the city", whichever county the property is in. https://www.monroeohio.gov/270/Building-and-Zoning
- HVAC/mechanical and electrical permits: no separate form is listed and the pages don't say whether the building permit covers them. **Unverified.** Fees and timings: unverified.
