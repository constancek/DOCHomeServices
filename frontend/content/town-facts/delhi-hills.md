# Delhi Hills (Delhi Township, OH): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives the ZIP as 45233 without qualification. Delhi Township covers both 45233 and 45238. None of the 13 addresses I geocoded fell inside the CDP, so the CDP's ZIP is unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Township,_Ohio ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mid-century homes" is loose. The CDP's median build year is 1971, and 48.0% of homes date from 1960-79. Only 24.2% are from 1940-59. See the housing section.
- "Greenhouse and floral growing roots" checks out for the township (see Other).
- The claim that "hillside grade adds demand on drains in heavy rain" is unverified, and so is the claim that corrosion leaks and water heaters are the most common jobs.
- The utility mix is close to 50/50 (gas 48.1%, electric 48.5%), so copy shouldn't assume gas heat.

## Where it is
- Delhi Hills CDP is in unincorporated Delhi Township, Hamilton County. It covers 1.47 sq mi on a bluff north of the Ohio River, 7 miles west of downtown Cincinnati. FIPS 39-21518. 2020 Census population 5,022. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Hills,_Ohio
- Delhi Township has a 2020 population of 28,760. It also contains the Delshire CDP (east of Delhi Hills) and Mount Saint Joseph. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Township,_Ohio ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delshire,_Ohio
- The Census Geocoder puts 390 Greenwell Ave (45238) in the Delshire CDP. Addresses on Delhi Rd, Neeb Rd, Foley Rd, Rapid Run Rd and Anderson Ferry Rd are in the township but outside any CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Township administration is at 5031 Delhi Road, Cincinnati, OH 45238. https://www.delhi.oh.us/

## Other
- Terrain: the township has many hills and stream-cut ravines sloping to the Ohio River floodplain. The riverfront itself is in Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Township,_Ohio
- Landslides are common on steep Ohio River valley slopes around Cincinnati, and Hamilton County adopted an excavation and fill ordinance in 1990. No Delhi Hills incident was confirmed (unverified). https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b2059A
- Flood plain: the CDP sits on a bluff, so it is likely not a factor. FEMA zones were not checked (unverified).
- In the pre-WWII years the township had as many as 55 family-run greenhouses and was known as the "Floral Paradise of Ohio". This is trivia for HVAC copy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Township,_Ohio
- No historic district was found (unverified). The restored Sedam Springhouse is the only historic site noted. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Township,_Ohio
