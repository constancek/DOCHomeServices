# Cheviot, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'cheviot'): ZIP 45211, "small, densely built city on the west side, completely surrounded by Cincinnati", "grid of early-1900s homes and brick two-families along Harrison Avenue", neighbors Westwood, West Price Hill, Covedale. Problems found:
- "Completely surrounded by Cincinnati": **unverified, likely wrong.** Wikipedia confirms only that Cheviot touches Westwood's western border. I found no source saying Cincinnati encloses it. Don't use "surrounded".
- Neighbors: only **Westwood** is confirmed. West Price Hill and Covedale adjacency is unverified.
- "Early-1900s homes" is fair (44.9% built 1939 or earlier, median 1946). "Brick" two-families is unverified. ACS shows 520 two-unit homes (13.4%).
- ZIP 45211 is far bigger than the city (17,271 vs 3,893 units). Use city figures for "Cheviot".

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County. Laid out 1818 on Green Township land, village in 1901, city in 1931. About 7 miles northwest of downtown Cincinnati, 1.17 sq mi, elevation 919 ft, ZIP 45211. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheviot,_Ohio
- Borders Westwood (Cincinnati) on Westwood's west side. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westwood,_Cincinnati
- City Hall: 3814 Harrison Avenue. https://cheviot.org/
- Population 8,660; median household income $53,132; median owner-occupied home value $153,000 (ACS 2020-2024 5-yr). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3914128-cheviot-oh/

## 3. Permits
- **Building permits:** the City of Cheviot contracts with **National Inspection Corporation (NIC)**. Applications go to Cheviot City Hall, which forwards them to NIC; the fee is paid to the city. The page posts a "2026 Hamilton County Fees" document. https://cheviot.org/departments/building-zoning/
- **Unverified:** whether electrical and mechanical/HVAC permits also go through NIC (the city page names only building permits). Don't imply it covers them.
- **Plumbing permits:** Hamilton County Public Health issues plumbing permits across Hamilton County "excluding the City of Cincinnati". Cheviot is not named, so treat HCPH as **likely, unverified** for Cheviot addresses. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- Fees, processing times: unverified. Don't state.

## 4. Utility
- **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers electricity and natural gas (city page). Cheviot runs opt-out aggregation (voter-approved 2006): electric supplier Constellation, gas supplier IGS Energy. https://cheviot.org/city-natural-gas-electric-aggregation-program/
- Duke Energy Ohio residential HVAC rebates: none confirmed (see norwood.md). Don't promise rebates.

## 5. Climate (Cincinnati, CVG station)
- July normal high 86.0°F (1991-2020). https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- About 21 days a year at 90°F or above (1981-2010 normals). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Historic:** Cheviot Fieldhouse (3729 Robb Ave, 1936 WPA, Art Deco) is on the National Register (NRIS 09000170, 2009). It is a public building, not a residential district. No city historic district or design-review rule for homes found. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/09000170
- **Terrain/flood:** upland at 919 ft with no river through the city (Wikipedia, above). River flooding is not a factor. Parcel-level FEMA zones and sewer backup history: unverified; check https://msc.fema.gov by address.
