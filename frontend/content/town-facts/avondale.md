# Avondale (Cincinnati, OH 45229): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` lists only Corryville and Walnut Hills as neighbours. Wikipedia lists North Avondale, Evanston, Walnut Hills, Corryville and Clifton. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Cincinnati
- The "historic early-1900s homes" line is only partly right. Only 43.0% of units predate 1940, and the tract medians run from 1938 to 1961 (see the housing section). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006800-census-tract-68-hamilton-oh/
- ZIP 45229 also covers North Avondale and Paddock Hills, so ZIP-level data is not Avondale alone. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45229-45229/

## Where it is
- Avondale is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, ZIP 45229. It incorporated as a village in 1864 and the city annexed it in 1896. It is home to the Cincinnati Zoo and sits next to Cincinnati Children's Hospital. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Cincinnati
- The city's 2020 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation uses Census tracts 66, 68, 69 and 270 and counts 11,345 residents. The city PDF would not load, so this comes from search results quoting it: unverified until someone opens it. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Avondale_2020.pdf
- Reading Road and Burnet Avenue are the main corridors. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Cincinnati

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, tracts 66, 68, 69 and 270 combined)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006600-census-tract-66-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006800-census-tract-68-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006900-census-tract-69-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061027000-census-tract-270-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040)
- 6,756 housing units, 5,950 occupied.
- Median year built by tract: 66 is 1938, 69 is 1941, 270 is 1940, 68 is 1961.
- Era: 2,904 units (43.0%) built 1939 or earlier, 1,265 (18.7%) in 1940-59, 1,227 (18.2%) in 1960-79, 660 (9.8%) in 1980-99, and 700 (10.4%) in 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 1,564 single-family detached (23.1%), 237 attached, 683 two-family, 1,654 in 3-4 unit buildings (24.5%), 1,515 in 5-49 unit buildings and 1,103 in buildings of 50 or more (16.3%).
- Tenure: 4,530 of 5,950 occupied units are rented (76.1%). 1,420 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: electricity 2,953 (49.6%), utility gas 2,780 (46.7%), propane 24, no fuel 184.
- Tract 66 is the oldest and most rented: 59.3% pre-1940, 86.8% rented, 60.1% heat with electricity. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006600-census-tract-66-hamilton-oh/
- ZIP 45229 as a whole: median year built 1940, 69.4% rented, 46.8% electric heat. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45229-45229/

## Permits
- Avondale is inside the City of Cincinnati, so the City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building permits. Its residential permit guide and mechanical inspections pages cover mechanical work. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
- Whether the same office handles plumbing and electrical permits is unverified for Avondale (the city site could not be reached to check). Fees and timings are unverified.
