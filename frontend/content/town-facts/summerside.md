# Summerside, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'summerside'): ZIP 45245, Clermont County, Union Township community in western Clermont County with "suburban neighborhoods of mid-century and newer homes". Location confirmed. ZIP and "mid-century" need correcting (see the end).

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated census-designated place (CDP) in the western part of Union Township, Clermont County. 2020 census population 4,941. Mount Carmel borders it to the south; Ohio SR 32 forms part of the southern boundary and I-275 runs along the eastern edge. Originally "East Mount Carmel"; renamed when its post office opened in August 1887. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summerside,_Ohio (cites Census)
- Union Township has no incorporated municipalities. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Township,_Clermont_County,_Ohio
- Geocoded inside the CDP: 4500, 4593 and 4700 Summerside Rd, all ZIP **45244**, tract 414.05 (Census geocoder via census.mjs). Whether part of the CDP uses 45245: unverified (one third-party source lists both).

## 3. Permits
