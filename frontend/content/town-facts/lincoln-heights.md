- "Sits on low Mill Creek valley ground" and "real risk of backups in heavy rain": no source found tying the village to Mill Creek flooding or sewer backups. Unverified; don't use. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Heights,_Ohio (silent on flooding)
- "One of the first self-governed Black municipalities": unverified, and trivia under page-format rules.
- Copy reads as a homeowner town; 73.6% of occupied units are rented (section 2).
- ZIP 45215 is correct, but the ZIP is far larger than the village. Use village figures. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Heights,_Ohio

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, north of Cincinnati, ZIP 45215. Incorporated 1946. About 0.75 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Heights,_Ohio
- Neighbors: Evendale and Woodlawn; Lockland border unverified. Same source.
