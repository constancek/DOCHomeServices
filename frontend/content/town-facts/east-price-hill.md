# East Price Hill (Cincinnati, OH 45205): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- The Incline base was at 8th Street and Glenway Avenue, and it "climbed 350 feet". Its opening and closing dates are unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati (use only to locate a primary source)
- "Early-1900s homes" undersells the age. Tracts 94, 95 and 96 have a median year built of 1938, while tracts 92 and 93 date from the 1960s (see Housing). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061009400-census-tract-94-hamilton-oh/
- The claim that "hillside grade pushes on basement drains" is unverified. No source ties East Price Hill's slope to basement drainage.

## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood, one of three that make up Price Hill along with West Price Hill and Lower Price Hill. Hamilton County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati
- The city's 2020 statistical approximation uses Census Tracts 92, 93, 94, 95 and 96 and counts 15,241 residents. Read via search index only (city server refused connection). https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/EastPriceHill_2020.pdf
- ZIP 45205. ZIP and neighbourhood boundaries don't match. Whether 45205 also takes in part of West Price Hill is unverified. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45205-45205/
- Neighbours: West Price Hill to the west, Lower Price Hill to the east and Sedamsville to the south and downslope. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Sedamsville_2020.1.pdf

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year)
Tracts 92-96 combined (the city's neighbourhood approximation). Source: https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B25035,B25034,B25024,B25003,B25040&geo_ids=14000US39061009200,14000US39061009300,14000US39061009400,14000US39061009500,14000US39061009600
- Median year built by tract: 92 = 1967, 93 = 1963, 94 = 1938, 95 = 1938, 96 = 1938. 
- 2,933 of 7,317 units (40.1%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 1,203 date from the 1960s. Only 372 (5.1%) date from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 2,880 single-family detached, 259 attached, 672 two-family, 1,065 in 3-4 unit buildings, 448 in 5-9 unit buildings, 1,993 in buildings of 10 or more units.
- Tenure: 3,993 of 6,321 occupied units are rented (63.2%) and 2,328 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 3,855 utility gas (61.0%), 2,311 electricity (36.6%), 110 propane.

ZIP 45205, same survey: 7,964 units, median year built 1946, 3,611 (45.3%) built 1939 or earlier, 4,425 of 7,009 households renting (63.1%), 2,165 heating with electricity (30.9%), 4,626 with gas. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45205-45205/

## Permits
- City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections. Heating, ventilating and air conditioning, plumbing and electrical work each need a separate permit. Most HVAC, plumbing and electrical permits must be pulled by the contractor. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/
- The city outsources electrical permits and inspections to Inspections Bureau, Inc. (IBI). Same source. Fees, phones and timings unverified (page is under an "old-" URL).

## Utility
- Duke Energy Ohio supplies electricity and natural gas across the Cincinnati area. https://news.duke-energy.com/releases/duke-energy-ohio-kentucky-help-communities-across-the-region-prepare-sites-to-boost-economic-development
- Sewer service comes from MSD of Greater Cincinnati. Combined sewers in older neighbourhoods can back up into basements in heavy rain. MSD runs a sewer backup programme; report within 48 hours (per local news). https://msdgc.org/msd-news/articles/sewer-backup-5-things-you-need-to-know-to-prevent-and-report/ ; https://www.axios.com/local/cincinnati/2026/07/21/basement-flood-advice-ohio-metropolitan-sewer-district-greater-cincinnati
- Whether East Price Hill specifically runs on combined sewers is unverified.

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)
- July normal high 86F (1991-2020). https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- On average 21 days a year reach 90F or higher (1981-2010). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s

## Other local factors
- Warsaw Avenue Historic District, 3104-3220 Warsaw Ave (between Hawthorne and Purcell). National Register listing 24 Aug 2020. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2025%20-%20Special/Historic%20Designations/NRHP-Districts-8.12.25.pdf
- City Council also made it a local historic district in February 2020: 16 buildings, with guidelines for exterior changes and a review step for demolition. https://www.citybeat.com/news/cincinnatis-newest-local-historic-district-will-preserve-east-price-hills-past-and-could-fuel-its-future-12159328 ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-historic-designation/ (exterior work there goes through a Certificate of Appropriateness)
- Landslides: Elberon Avenue between Mt. Hope and Purcell has closed for landslides more than once, and the city has proposed a $600,000 retaining wall. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/dote/news/elberon-avenue-closed-between-mt-hope-and-purcell-due-to-landslide ; https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/east-price-hill/city-manager-proposes-600-000-fix-for-east-price-hills-landslide-prone-elberon-avenue
- The Incline District is a neighbourhood branding area, not a historic district. https://soapboxmedia.com/0405inclinedistricteastpricehill/
- FEMA flood zone status not checked; make no flood claims.
