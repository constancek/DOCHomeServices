# Dillonvale (Sycamore Township, OH 45236): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls Dillonvale "near Kenwood" and "on the east side near Kenwood". The addresses I geocoded put Deer Park city right next to it (4100 E Galbraith Rd and 8000 Blue Ash Rd) and Rossmoyne CDP on the other side (8400 Plainfield Rd and 8300 Blue Ash Rd). Kenwood CDP starts at Kenwood Rd (8000 Kenwood Rd geocodes to Kenwood CDP), and whether it actually touches Dillonvale is unverified. "Next to Deer Park" is safer than "near Kenwood". https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mostly mid-century homes" checks out: the median build year is 1956 and 73.2% of homes date from 1940-59 (see Housing). Dillonvale began as a 1940s subdivision built by Dillon Builders. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dillonvale,_Hamilton_County,_Ohio
- The claims that "supply lines corrode and water heaters fail" here are unverified (they're a generic age-of-home claim, not a local fact).

## Where it is
- Dillonvale is a census-designated place (CDP) in unincorporated Sycamore Township, Hamilton County. Area 0.89 sq mi, all land. 2020 Census: 3,436 residents, 1,638 housing units. Elevation 755 ft. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dillonvale,_Hamilton_County,_Ohio
- 3900 and 3800 E Galbraith Rd geocode to Dillonvale CDP, Sycamore Township, ZIP 45236, in tracts 237.01 and 236. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits (from _shared.md, plus township check)
- Building permits come from Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections, which issues them for the unincorporated townships. Electrical permits and inspections go through Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). Confirmed by search snippet only; the county page wasn't opened this session. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- Sycamore Township reviews zoning first. Its FAQ says most submittals it reviews for zoning also need a permit from the Hamilton County Building Department, and the applicant takes the township-approved drawings to the county. Small sheds (200 sq ft and under) and residential fences are exempt. The page returned 403, so this comes from the search snippet. https://sycamoretownship.org/faq-items/do-i-need-to-apply-for-a-building-permit-with-hamilton-county-also/
- Plumbing permits: Hamilton County Public Health (from _shared.md). https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- The office that issues HVAC/mechanical permits: unverified. Only third-party permit-service sites say the county issues them, and those sites don't count as a source.
