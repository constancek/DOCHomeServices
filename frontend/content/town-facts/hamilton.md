Note: hamilton-oh.gov did not resolve from our machine during research (DNS failure), so city-site wording below comes from search-result snippets of those pages, not a direct fetch.

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Hamilton the Butler County seat, ZIP 45011, straddling the Great Miami River, with German Village and Dayton Lane historic districts of 19th-century homes, and low ground near the river adding flood and backup risk. ## 1. Where it is
- City (not a township), seat of Butler County, OH, on the Great Miami River. ACS 2024 5-year population 63,468. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3933012-hamilton-oh/
- City government at 345 High St., Hamilton, OH 45011. https://www.bidexpress.com/businesses/19640/home?page=5
- ZIP 45011 is confirmed in locations.ts. Parts of the city also use 45013 and 45015: unverified.

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2024 5-year, 2020-2024; Census Reporter, geo 16000US3933012)
https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3933012-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25040, B25003 via api.censusreporter.org)
- 26,922 housing units. **Median year built: 1956.**
- Built 1939 or earlier: 7,368 (27.4%). 1940-49: 2,688 (10.0%). 1950-59: 5,471 (20.3%). So **57.7% were built before 1960**.
- 1960-79: 5,449 (20.2%). 1980-99: 2,800 (10.4%). 2000 or later: 3,146 (11.7%).
- 69.1% single-family detached, 3.8% attached, 11.5% in 2-4 unit buildings, 14.7% in 5+ unit buildings, 220 mobile homes.
- Tenure: 54.3% owner-occupied, 45.7% renter-occupied (24,858 occupied units). Median owner home value $165,200.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 61.0% (15,155), **electricity 35.2% (8,760)**, propane 1.7%, fuel oil 0.8%.

## 3. Permits
- The City of Hamilton issues its own permits. Its Permits, Certificates & Registrations page lists Building, Electric, Gas, Plumbing and **HVAC / Refrigeration** permit applications. Questions go to building@hamilton-oh.gov or (513) 785-7360 (do not put the number in copy). https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/permits-certificates-registrations
- A City of Hamilton Contractor Registration is required for any Electric, HVAC, Plumbing, Gas, Hydronics/Wet Heat or Sewer work that needs a permit. Only the Master Registration holder or their authorized representative may apply, and the permit must be pulled before work starts. https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/building-contractor-registration
- The city's hiring-a-contractor page lists "Water heater, furnace or A/C replacements" among permit types. https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/building-hiring-a-contractor
- The office's exact name (Building Department / Building Division) and its address are unverified (one fragment said 130 High St.). Fees and timelines: unverified. Third-party figures (e.g. $233.54) are Hamilton County's, not the city's. Don't use them.
- Don't confuse with Hamilton County (Cincinnati). Its Buildings + Inspections office does not cover the City of Hamilton. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi

## 4. Utilities (city-owned, not Duke)
- **Electric: City of Hamilton municipal utility.** The Utility Operations Department runs a "fully integrated municipal electric system providing generation, transmission, and distribution", plus water and wastewater. https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/utility-operations
- The city says it meets about 56% of its energy needs from renewables including the Greenup, Meldahl and Hamilton hydroelectric projects. Meldahl is a 105 MW plant on the Ohio River. https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/utilities-about and https://www.journal-news.com/news/hamilton-hydroelectric-power-plant-ohio-river-largest-nears-completion/MZEwijYqTMIf0ehxn1jwhK
- Residential rate schedule (effective 2019) had a fixed monthly charge rising from $15.50 (2019) to $23.50 (2023) plus about $0.13/kWh. Current rates are unverified. https://gis.hamilton-oh.gov/webdocs/Dept%20Utility%20Bus%20Services/Website%20Documents/Electric%20Rate%20Schedules%20-%20Effective%202-1-19.pdf
- About 29,300-30,000 electric customers (2013 news / bond page). Dated. https://www.bondlink.com/city-of-hamilton-ohio-investor-relations-oh/about/i5562
- **Natural gas: also city-owned**, run since 1890, the largest municipal gas utility in Ohio, about 23,000 customers per the city. https://www.hamilton-oh.gov/utilities-about and https://www.ohioauditor.gov/auditsearch/Reports/2010/City_of_Hamilton_Gas_System_09-Butler.pdf
- So Duke Energy rebates and Duke billing do not apply inside the city. Any city HVAC or efficiency rebate is unverified.

## 6. Other local notes
- **Historic districts:** any exterior work in a City of Hamilton Historic Area, or on a property listed by ordinance, needs a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Architectural Design Review Board. Like-for-like work can get a free administrative COA, usually within two business days. Districts include German Village, Dayton Lane and Rossville. https://hamilton-oh.gov/adrb and https://gis.hamilton-oh.gov/webdocs/Dept%20Planning/ADRB/Living-in-a-Historic-Home-Brochure.pdf . Whether a new outdoor condenser or wall penetration counts as "exterior work" needing a COA is unverified.
- **Flood:** the 1913 flood hit Hamilton hard. The Great Miami crested at 34.6 ft. https://mrcc.purdue.edu/1913Flood/communities/hamilton . The Miami Conservancy District (1914) built the dams and levees that now protect the city. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Miami_River . FEMA's preliminary Butler County panel shows Zone AE/AH along the river and areas of reduced hazard behind an accredited levee. https://prd-floodmaps.msc.fema.gov/prelim/PrelimData/Ohio/Butler%20County/prelim_issue_date-2024-07-10/FIRM/39017C0302G.pdf . Which streets sit in a flood zone, and the city's floodplain administrator: unverified.
