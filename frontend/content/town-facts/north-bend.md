## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** (1845) in Miami Township, Hamilton County, on the Ohio River where it bends north. ZIP 45052. About 1.15 sq mi. Population 835 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bend,_Ohio
- Village office: 21 Taylor Ave. https://www.northbendohio.org/
