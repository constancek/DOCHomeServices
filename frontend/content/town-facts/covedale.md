# Covedale (Cincinnati, OH 45238): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls Covedale a community "on the edge of the city near Green Township". The name actually covers two places, and the larger one is in Green Township. Cincinnati annexed most of the old village of Covedale in 1930. The part it didn't annex is the Covedale CDP, which is unincorporated Green Township. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covedale,_Ohio (to locate the source) ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- `adjacent` lists Cheviot, which is probably wrong. The Census Geocoder puts Bridgetown CDP to the north of the CDP, Mack CDP to the west, Delhi Township to the south and the City of Cincinnati to the east. None of the points I checked fell in Cheviot. The Cheviot entry lists Covedale as a neighbour, so it has the same problem. Whether Westwood touches Covedale is unverified: the city land to the north-east is in tracts 109 and 102.01. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Early-1900s through mid-century" fits the city side (median build year 1938) but not the CDP (median 1959, only 11.3% from before 1940). See the housing section.
- The claims about clay laterals and roots are unverified.

## Where it is
- **Covedale CDP:** unincorporated Green Township, Hamilton County. Area 2.83 sq mi, ZIP 45238. 2020 Census population 6,472. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covedale,_Ohio ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3919008-covedale-oh/
- 5200 Cleves Warsaw Pike geocodes to Covedale CDP, Green Township, 45238. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- **City side:** Glenway Ave between the 5000 and 5300 blocks, and 1100 Covedale Ave, are inside the City of Cincinnati, ZIP 45238. They fall in tracts 100.05, 107 and 99.01. From the 5800 block, Glenway is in tract 102.01, and at the 6000 block the ZIP is 45211. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- On the city's 2010 list, tract 107 is part of the West Price Hill statistical neighbourhood. Covedale is not one of the city's statistical neighbourhoods. Wikipedia's figure of "about 15,000" residents for city Covedale is unverified. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Census/2010/West-Price-Hill.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covedale,_Cincinnati

## Housing (ACS 2020-2024 5-year)
**Covedale CDP** (2,766 units, population 6,500). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3919008-covedale-oh/
- Median year built 1959. 313 units (11.3%) were built in 1939 or earlier, 289 in the 1940s, 915 (33.1%) in the 1950s and 591 in the 1960s, so 1,795 (64.9%) date from 1940-1969. 377 date from the 1970s and only 86 (3.1%) from 2000 or later.
- 2,639 units (95.4%) are single-family detached.
- Of 2,640 occupied units, 2,409 (91.3%) are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 2,183 (82.7%), electricity 368 (13.9%), propane 28.

**Tract 107, city side** (810 units). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061010700-census-tract-107-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built 1938, with 510 units (63.0%) from 1939 or earlier.
- 533 single-family detached, 136 two-family and 110 in 3-4 unit buildings.
- Of 795 occupied units, 440 (55.3%) are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: gas 600 (75.5%), electricity 174 (21.9%).

## Permits
- **City side:** City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections. Plumbing, electrical and HVAC each need a separate permit. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/
- **CDP (Green Township):**
  - Building permits come from Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections. Electrical permits and inspections go through Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). The county page returned 403 this session, so this is confirmed by search snippet only. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
  - Plumbing permits come from Hamilton County Public Health, which covers the county "excluding the City of Cincinnati". A homeowner permit is available to owner-occupants of a single-family home. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
  - The office that issues HVAC/mechanical permits in the CDP is unverified.
  - Zoning outside north-east Green Township is handled by Hamilton County, and a zoning certificate has to come before the building permit. Whether Covedale falls outside the north-east zoning area is unverified (it's at the township's south-east edge). https://greentwp.org/northeast-green-township-zoning
- Fees and timings are unverified.

## Utilities
- **City side:** Duke Energy Ohio. https://www.duke-energy.com/home (unverified for this session)
- **Green Township:** Duke Energy delivers power and bills residents. Under the township's aggregation, Dynegy supplied power through May 2025. https://www.dynegy.com/-/media/Green-Township-Opt-out-Letter-2023.pdf ; https://greentwp.org/gas-and-electric-aggregation
