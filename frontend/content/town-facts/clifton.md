## Where it is
- Clifton is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, around Clifton Avenue north of Dixmyth Avenue, about three miles north of downtown. It incorporated as a village in 1850 and Cincinnati annexed it in 1893. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton,_Cincinnati
- ZIP 45220. Note that Wikipedia lists 45316, which is wrong. ZCTA 45220 is much bigger than Clifton (7,350 housing units), so don't use ZIP figures as Clifton figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45220-45220/
- The city's 2020 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation covers tracts 70, 71 and 72, with 8,408 residents in the 2020 Census. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/resources/census/2020 ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Clifton_2020.pdf
- The Ludlow Avenue business district was the city's first "Main Street neighborhood". Burnet Woods covers 89 acres. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton,_Cincinnati

Source: https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25034&geo_ids=14000US39061007000,14000US39061007100,14000US39061007200 (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003 and B25040, same geo_ids). These are estimates with margins of error, and the tracts only approximate the neighbourhood boundary.
- Median year built is 1938 in each of the three tracts. Of 4,500 units, 2,799 (62.2%) were built in 1939 or earlier. Only 160 (3.6%) date from 2000 or later, and none from 2020 or later.
- Units in structure: 1,510 single-family detached (33.6%), 54 attached, 359 two-family, 424 in 3-4 unit buildings, 525 in 5-9 unit buildings, and 1,628 (36.2%) in buildings of 10 or more units. There are no mobile homes.

- Heating fuel: 2,603 utility gas (63.8%), 1,281 electricity (31.4%), 12 propane, no fuel oil.
- Median owner-occupied home value: $524,800 (tract 70), $469,500 (tract 71) and $291,800 (tract 72). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061007000-census-tract-70-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061007100-census-tract-71-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061007200-census-tract-72-hamilton-oh/

## Permits (City of Cincinnati, Department of Buildings & Inspections)
- A B&I mechanical permit is needed to install or replace an air conditioner or furnace. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
- B&I requires a plumbing permit for all new and replacement plumbing. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/inspections/plumbing-inspections/
- The city contracts Inspections Bureau, Inc. (IBI) to review and inspect electrical permits. Where applications are filed is unverified. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections
- Permits are issued only to properly licensed contractors. Fees and timings are unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/

## Other local factors
- The Clifton Avenue Historic District (National Register, 1978) is an irregular strip along Clifton Avenue, with 38 contributing buildings and significant dates of 1843 and 1925. National Register listing alone does not trigger city design review. Local designation is unverified. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/78002074 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_Avenue_Historic_District
- The "Gaslight District" name comes from the city's street gas lamps, which were listed on the National Register in 1978. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_Street_Gas_Lamps
- Parts of Clifton fall in the city's Hillside Overlay District, and the draft neighbourhood plan steers building away from landslide-prone areas. Which streets are affected is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2025%20-%20Plans/Clifton/Clifton-Plan-Goals,-Policies,-and-Actions-(11-4-2025).pdf
