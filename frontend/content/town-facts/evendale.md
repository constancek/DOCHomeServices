## 3. Permits (Village of Evendale)
- The village **Building Department** (Building, Planning and Zoning) issues building permits. The village's FAQ: "You need a permit for roof, fence, HVAC, demolition, remodel." Residential HVAC jobs don't need construction drawings; a site plan is required (a Google Earth photo is accepted). Apply online through the village portal or in person at the Administration Office. https://www.evendaleohio.org/247/Building
- HVAC uses the village's **Mechanical, Fire Suppression, and Storage Tank Permit Application** ("for installation of HVAC ... in residential or commercial structures"). https://www.evendaleohio.org/271/Applications
- **Plumbing** permits: Hamilton County Health Department, Plumbing Department. **Electrical**: The Inspection Bureau, Inc. **Sanitary sewer**: Metropolitan Sewer District. Same source.
- Contractors must file the Evendale Income Tax New Business Questionnaire. Same source.
- The village states review takes about 2 working days for standard residential permits. https://www.evendaleohio.org/247/Building (fees: schedule exists, amounts not checked; don't quote).

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Mill Creek** runs through the village; NWS has a gauge "Mill Creek at Evendale" (EVNO1). Flood stages: action 9 ft, minor 12 ft, moderate 14 ft, major 16.5 ft. At 12 ft NWS describes lowland flooding of the Mill Creek floodplain. https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/evno1
- FEMA flood elevations are published for Mill Creek and Sharon Creek in the Evendale area. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2004-01-06/pdf/04-157.pdf
- Which residential streets sit in the floodplain: unverified; check parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
