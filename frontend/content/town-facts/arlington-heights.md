# Arlington Heights, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'arlington-heights'):**
- "Small Mill Creek valley village" is loose. The defining fact is that the village sits almost entirely inside the wide median of I-75 (Mill Creek Expressway). I-75 follows Mill Creek's path through this corridor, but no source puts the village on the creek or in its flood plain. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_Heights,_Ohio ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_75_in_Ohio
- "Low valley ground raises the risk of backups in heavy rain": unverified, no source. Don't use.
- "Aging plumbing in the older housing": unverified. The homes are old (64.7% built 1939 or earlier, section 2), but nothing documents the pipe.
- "Compact grid of older homes": the age is right. "Grid" is unverified.
- "Help usually arrives the same day" and "We respond every day" are company claims. Use them only as `stats.md` states them.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County. About 0.26-0.27 sq mi, all land (the article gives both figures). Population 823 (2020 census). A northeastern suburb of Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_Heights,_Ohio
- ZIP 45215. The ZIP covers far more than the village (it also serves Wyoming and others), so use village figures, not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45215-45215/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_Heights,_Ohio
- Village offices: Town Hall, 601 Elliott Avenue. https://ahohio.org
