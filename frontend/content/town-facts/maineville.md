# Maineville, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Maineville "a growing town in southern Warren County, with a small historic core surrounded by newer subdivisions and rural homes", with "the occasional well or septic issue". The Census data backs "newer subdivisions": median year built 2004, 54.5% built 2000 or later. Only 7.9% predate 1960, so the older core is small. **Unverified:** a "historic core" (no historic district found), and well/septic homes (none found inside the village; rural homes around it are outside village limits). "Town" should be **village**.
- Note: Maineville's 45039 ZIP also covers Landen (see landen.md), so "Maineville" mailing addresses are often not in the village.

## 1. Where it is
- Village in Warren County (county seat Lebanon), incorporated 23 March 1850, named for settlers from Maine. ZIP 45039. Elevation 797 ft. 2020 population: Wikipedia gives both 1,405 and 1,468 (inconsistent; don't use). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maineville,_Ohio
- The village says it was founded in 1815 by about 100 families from Maine. https://www.mainevilleoh.com/about-mainville
- Township: Hamilton Township, Warren County. Census geocoder: 100 W Foster Maineville Rd and 8017 State Rte 48 both fall in Maineville village, tract 322.03 (run via census.mjs).
