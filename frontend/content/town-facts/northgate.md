# Northgate (Colerain Township, OH 45251): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` builds the intro and relevance around "the Northgate Mall corridor". The enclosed mall has effectively closed: interior tenants had to leave by September 14, 2026, and the township says the site has not been sold yet. The township bought the former Sears and had it demolished in June 2025. Drop the mall as a landmark, since it dates the page. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northgate_Mall_(Ohio) ; https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/i-team/mall-makeovers-northgate-mall-loan-default-complicates-its-redevelopment-plan
- "Mid-century and newer homes" is loose. The median build year is 1969, and 54.5% of units date from 1960-79, so the stock is mostly 1960s-70s. Only 9.8% date from 1940-59. See the housing section.
- "Water heaters, supply lines, sump pumps are the emergencies we are called for most" is a company claim with no source here. Unverified.
- ZIP 45251 is correct for the addresses I checked. Whether parts of the CDP use other ZIPs is unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Where it is
- Northgate is a census-designated place in unincorporated Colerain Township, Hamilton County. It covers 2.55 sq mi, and its 2020 population was 7,277. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northgate,_Ohio
- 9501 Colerain Ave and 2800 and 3500 Springdale Rd all geocode to Northgate CDP, Colerain Township, ZIP 45251, in tracts 207.01 and 205.05. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Neighbours by geocode: 8900 Cheviot Rd, and Colerain Ave at the 9000 block, fall in Groesbeck CDP. 9200 Pippin Rd is in Northbrook CDP (ZIP 45231). 3100 Jessup Rd is in White Oak CDP, Green Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits
- **Zoning:** Colerain Township's Planning and Zoning Division issues zoning certificates, through the township's online portal. https://www.colerain.org/197/Development
- Hamilton County Planning + Development says projects in Colerain Township need zoning approval from the township, and most building permits need that approval before the county Permit Counter accepts them. This was confirmed by search snippet only. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/buildings_and_inspections
- Whether a like-for-like AC or furnace swap needs a township zoning certificate is unverified. The guide PDF could not be read. https://www.colerain.org/DocumentCenter/View/4885/Zoning-Certificates
- **Building permits:** Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections (from _shared.md). The county site returned 403 this session. The township Fire Department's Inspections Bureau handles fire code and fire permits, and refers to the "Hamilton County Building Department" for building work. https://www.colerain.org/377/Inspections-Bureau
- **HVAC/mechanical permits:** the issuing office is unverified. Only a third-party permit site says the county issues them, and that isn't an allowed source.
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). Both from _shared.md. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- Fees and timings are unverified.
