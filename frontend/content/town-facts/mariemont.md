## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in eastern Hamilton County on the north bank of the Little Miami River, about 10 miles east of downtown Cincinnati. US 50 (Wooster Pike) runs through it. Planned by John Nolen for Mary Emery; groundbreaking April 23, 1923. 2020 census population 3,518; about 0.9 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariemont,_Ohio and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariemont_Historic_District
- Developed within Columbia Township in 1922; incorporated as a village in 1941. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Township,_Hamilton_County,_Ohio
- ZIP 45227 is much bigger (9,617 housing units vs 1,477) and also covers Madisonville, Fairfax and parts of Columbia Township. Use village figures for "Mariemont". https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45227-45227/

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Mariemont village 16000US3947600)
Source: tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3947600-mariemont-oh/
- Median year built: **1945** (ZIP 45227: 1951).
- Of 1,477 units: **544 (36.8%) built 1939 or earlier**, 388 (26.3%) in the 1940s, 231 (15.6%) in the 1950s, 74 in the 1960s, 45 in the 1970s. **1,282 (86.8%) built before 1980.** 179 built 2000 or later (142 in 2010-2019), none 2020 or later.
- Home types: 885 (59.9%) single-family detached, 126 (8.5%) attached, 21 two-unit, 156 in 3-4 unit buildings, 80 in 5-9, 209 (14.2%) in buildings of 10 or more units. No mobile homes.
- Tenure: 1,019 of 1,366 occupied units owner-occupied (**74.6%**).
- Heating fuel (occupied units): utility gas 1,068 (78.2%), **electricity 261 (19.1%)**, bottled gas 13, fuel oil 14, other 10. ZIP 45227 electricity share: 31.4%. ACS does not split heat pumps from electric resistance heat.

## 3. Permits (Village of Mariemont)
- The **Village of Mariemont Building Department** issues the Zoning and Certificate of Plan Approval for building work and, on a separate application, for **HVAC work**. The village building inspector does the inspections. https://mariemont.org/departments/building-department/building-department-faq/
- **Electrical**: plan review and inspections by **The Inspection Bureau, Inc.** Same source.
- **Plumbing**: **Hamilton County Public Health** (Board of Health of Hamilton County Health District). Same source.
- Ordinary repairs under $500 are exempt from a permit, per the same FAQ. Fees and turnaround times: unverified, don't state.

## 4. Historic district rules
- Listed on the National Register 1979 (319 acres, ref. 79001862); designated a **National Historic Landmark March 29, 2007** (525 acres). https://www.nps.gov/articles/2006-2007-national-historic-landmark-designations.htm and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariemont_Historic_District
- The village has **7 local historic districts**. Any exterior change there, including paint color, needs a **Certificate of Appropriateness** from the Architectural Review Board, which meets the third Monday of each month. https://mariemont.org/departments/building-department/building-department-faq/
- Whether the ARB reviews outdoor equipment such as AC condensers or vents: **unverified**. Don't claim it.
