# Columbia-Tusculum (Cincinnati, OH 45226): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls the whole neighbourhood "low ground near the Ohio River". It sits where the Little Miami meets the Ohio but also climbs the Tusculum hills, so flood risk is street-specific. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia-Tusculum,_Cincinnati
- "Original plumbing well past its service life" is an inference, not sourced fact.
- The `adjacent` list (Mount Lookout, Mount Washington) is unverified. No government map was reachable to confirm borders.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, Hamilton County. Columbia was founded in 1788 at the Little Miami River, a month before Losantiville. Cincinnati annexed Columbia in 1871 and Tusculum in 1875. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia-Tusculum,_Cincinnati
- City planning's 2020 neighbourhood profile puts the population at 1,523 and builds the neighbourhood from Census Tract 47.03, Block Group 2. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/ColumbiaTusculum_2020.pdf (PDF unreachable; figures from search snippet, BG match likely, not certain)
- ZIP 45226.

## Permits

## Historic district
- The city has a local Columbia Tusculum Historic District with conservation guidelines. Exterior changes generally need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic Conservation Board on top of the building permit; ordinary maintenance with no exterior change is exempt. Whether a condenser, vent or meter relocation counts as an exterior change: unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation-old/local-conservation-guidelines/columbia-tusculum-historic-district/
- National Register listings came through a Columbia-Tusculum multiple resource area, with individual properties listed 24 August 1979 (e.g. Norwell Residence, 506 Tusculum Ave). https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64000615_text ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwell_Residence

## Flooding and terrain
- MSD says the Delta Avenue area is very low-lying and saw both overland flooding and sewer backups in a July flash flood (year unverified). A resident put his losses at $25,000 or more. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/we-need-answers-columbia-tusculum-residents-frustrated-by-repeated-flooding-lack-of-solutions
- The "bowl" between Hoge Street and Delta Avenue is the low point; Crawfish Creek ran there until it was piped in the early 1900s. A 48-inch stormwater bypass from Lawler Avenue toward Columbia Parkway was proposed for June 2018; completion unverified. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/columbia-tusculum/city-has-plan-to-address-delta-avenue-area-flooding
