# South Lebanon, OH: verified local facts

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls South Lebanon "a growing town on the Little Miami River in Warren County, with a historic core and fast-expanding newer subdivisions". The Census data below backs the growth (65.9% of homes built 2000 or later, median 2007). It is now a **city**, not a village (Census and Wikipedia both say city; 2020 population 6,384). "Low riverside ground" backups: the NWS gauge notes below confirm riverfront basement flooding, so that part holds.

## 1. Where it is
- City in central Warren County, in parts of Union and Hamilton townships. ZIP 45065. 2020 population 6,384. Originally called Deerfield; post office renamed South Lebanon in 1871. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon,_Ohio
- Census geocoder: 10 N High St and 99 N High St both match South Lebanon city (place 16000US3973446), tract 39165032100, Union Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=10%20N%20High%20St%2C%20South%20Lebanon%2C%20OH%2045065&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&format=json . (100 Mound St with 45065 matched Lebanon city instead, so check addresses.)

## 6. Flooding (NWS gauge SLBO1, Little Miami River near South Lebanon)
https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/SLBO1 (data via https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/SLBO1)
- Flood categories: action 15 ft, minor 19 ft, moderate 25 ft, major 32 ft.
- At 23 ft "some homes near the river incur basement flooding in South Lebanon"; at 25-26 ft basement flooding of several feet in riverfront homes along South Main, South High and King Avenue; 33 ft is roughly the FEMA 1 percent flood.
- The city has a Flood Damage Ordinance 2025-03; floodplain maps referenced to a FEMA FIRM revised October 18, 1994. https://southlebanonohio.org/zoning/
