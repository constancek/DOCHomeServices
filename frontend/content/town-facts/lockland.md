# Lockland, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors / unsupported claims in locations.ts (slug 'lockland'):**
- "Closely spaced older worker housing" / "tightly packed homes": unverified, no source. Census does confirm old stock (47.9% built 1939 or earlier, section 2), but 23.4% of units are in 5+ unit buildings, so it is not all small houses.
- "Low valley ground brings a real risk of backups in heavy rain": unverified. No source found tying Lockland homes to Mill Creek flood zones or sewer backups (section 6).
- "Built up around mills and industry": partly supported. The town grew at the first Miami and Erie Canal locks north of Cincinnati, with a water-powered gristmill, and Stearns and Foster ran a factory there from the 1880s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockland,_Ohio
- "Aging plumbing prone to leaks": unverified, no source.
- ZIP 45215 is correct but the ZIP is far larger than the village (Census 45215 profile, section 2). Use village figures.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, Mayor/Council government. About 1.22 sq mi, all land; elevation 554 ft. North of Cincinnati. Population 3,514 (2020 census). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockland,_Ohio
- About 10 miles north of downtown Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mill_%26_Dunn_Historic_District
- I-75 (Mill Creek Expressway) runs through the old canal bed in Lockland. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockland,_Ohio
- Neighbors: Wyoming (railroad line forms the border) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyoming,_Ohio ; Reading lies to the east (a Reading report says Reading is bounded by Lockland to the west) https://www.hamilton-co.org//UserFiles/DBFiles/server_3788196/201903/15375547/reading_report_final_draft_07_31_2017.pdf . Other borders unverified.
- Village hall (municipal building): 101 N Cooper Ave (geocoded by Census; directory listing at https://www.citydirectory.us/village-lockland.html). Official village website: unverified (locklandoh.org and lockland.com both failed to resolve).

## 3. Permits
- Lockland is **not** on Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections' list of contract jurisdictions (Amberley Village, Elmwood Place, Indian Hill, North Bend, Terrace Park, Wyoming), and zoning approval comes from the local jurisdiction. https://hamilton-co.org/i_want_to/building_permit and https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- Lockland's zoning code requires a permit from the village **Code Enforcement Officer** before a structure is erected, altered or changes use (third-party code copy; may be outdated). https://www.zoneomics.com/code/lockland-OH/chapter_4
