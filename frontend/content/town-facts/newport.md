## 1. Where it is
- Newport is an incorporated city in Campbell County, Kentucky, with its own building department, separate from the county's. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf
- Population 13,967 (ACS 2020-2024 5-year). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2155884-newport-ky/

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Newport city, place 16000US2155884)
Source: Census tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2155884-newport-ky/
- Median year built: **1947**.
- Of 7,828 housing units, **3,637 (46.5%) were built in 1939 or earlier**. 4,694 (60.0%) were built before 1960. 868 (11.1%) were built 2010 or later.
- Home types: 3,908 (49.9%) single-family detached, 111 attached. 1,378 (17.6%) are in 2-4 unit buildings, and 2,315 (29.6%) are in buildings of 5 or more units.
- Occupied units: 6,965. Owner-occupied 3,325 (47.7%), renter-occupied 3,640 (52.3%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 3,967 (57.0%), **electricity 2,832 (40.7%)**, propane 101 (1.5%).
- ZCTA 41071 (same tables, https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41071-41071/): median year built 1959, 4,206 of 10,895 units (38.6%) built 1939 or earlier, 46.5% single-family detached, 47.3% renter-occupied, 46.7% heat with electricity.

## 3. Permits
- HVAC: the state's local HVAC jurisdiction list names the **City of Newport** (Chief Building Official) as its own local HVAC jurisdiction, separate from Campbell County. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Updated%20Local%20HVAC.pdf
- The City of Newport **Building Department** (998 Monmouth Street) issues building permits. Its online portal lists a 1-2 Family HVAC Application, a Commercial HVAC Application and an Electrical Permit Application. https://newportky.gov/227/Building-Permits
- The city says most building permits are processed within fourteen business days. Don't use the timing in copy. Same source.
- Plumbing: the state list names a state plumbing inspector for Campbell County, based at the Boone County Health Center in Florence. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf
- Kentucky requires a permit for replacing a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/

## 4. Historic districts
- Local historic districts: Buena Vista, East Row and York Street, with a Monmouth Street overlay in progress. Owners need a Certificate of Appropriateness (COA) from the city's Historic Preservation office for many exterior alterations before work starts. COAs are free. https://newportky.gov/220
- Whether outdoor condensers or other mechanical equipment need a COA: unverified (the city page doesn't say).
- East Row is on the National Register of Historic Places (refs 80001493 and 85001678, per Wikipedia; NPS listing not checked). Styles include Italianate, Second Empire, Queen Anne and American Foursquare (unverified). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Row_Historic_District

## 5. Flood plain
- Newport sits behind a floodwall that runs from Wilder down the Licking and up the Ohio to Bellevue. SD1 operates it. Newport has three flood pump stations, installed in the 1950s. https://www.wvxu.org/podcast/oki-wanna-know/2023-08-16/pipes-jutting-out-newport-levee
- The city closed its Columbia Street floodgate when the Ohio rose toward a crest of about 56 feet (March 2021). https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/campbell-county/newport-community/floodwall-gate-closes-as-ohio-river-level-rises
- Basement backups during high water: unverified.

## 6. Utilities
- Electric and gas: **Duke Energy Kentucky** serves Campbell County for both (county-level source, not address-level). https://psc.ky.gov/pscecf/2016-00168/adele.frisch@duke-energy.com/05112016103346/Application,Petition,_Exhibit_1,2a,2b.pdf
- Water: Northern Kentucky Water District, with water main replacement work underway in Newport (Phase 3 scheduled September 2025 to August 2027). https://www.nkywater.org/193/Engineering-Construction
- Sewer and flood pumps: Sanitation District No. 1 (SD1). https://sd1.org/445/Flood-Levee-Protection-System

## 7. Climate
- The official Cincinnati station is CVG in Boone County. NWS lists the CVG July normal high as 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- Average 22 days a year at 90°F or above, calculated from 1991-2020 CVG daily data (not a published normal). https://data.rcc-acis.org
