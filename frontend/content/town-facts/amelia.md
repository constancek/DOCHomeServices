# Amelia, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'amelia'): ZIP 45102, Clermont County, "community along the Ohio Pike corridor", "established neighborhoods of mid-century and newer homes". It never says village, which is correct now, but the housing age is off; see the end.

## 1. Where it is (current status)
- **No longer a village.** Voters approved dissolving the Village of Amelia in November 2019 (68% yes); it was dissolved when the Board of Elections certified the result. About 65% of the former village went to **Pierce Township** and 35% to **Batavia Township**. It was the most populous Ohio village ever dissolved and the first split between two townships. https://wcpo.com/news/local-news/clermont-county/amelia/amelia-dissolves-sending-residents-to-batavia-and-pierce-township ; https://www.fox19.com/2019/11/26/village-amelia-is-officially-no-more
- The State Auditor oversaw the wind-down into 2020. https://bataviatownship.org/PDF/admin/041020-SummaryStateAuditorMtg.pdf
- Census now treats Amelia as a **CDP** (16000US3901742). Geocoded: 44 W Main St and 100 W Main St = Pierce Township; 1 E Main St = Batavia Township; all in the Amelia CDP, ZIP 45102. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- So the township for an address depends on which side it falls; check it per job.
