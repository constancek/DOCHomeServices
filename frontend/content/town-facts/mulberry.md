# Mulberry, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'mulberry'):**
- "suburban neighborhoods of mid-century and newer homes" is wrong. Median year built is 1988; 73.2% of units were built 1980 or later. Only 39.0% are single-family detached; 17.4% are attached and 40.7% are in 5+ unit buildings (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3953270/
- "Milford area of western Clermont County": it is an unincorporated CDP in Miami Township, northwestern Clermont County, with I-275 as its western edge. Permits go to the county, not Milford (section 3). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulberry,_Ohio
- ZIP 45150 is shared; use CDP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45150-45150/
- "We respond any day of the week" is a company claim; check stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated **census-designated place** in Miami Township, Clermont County. 1.60 sq mi, all land; population 3,459 (2020). ZIP 45150. Mount Repose borders it to the east; I-275 (exit 57, SR 28) forms its western edge. Laid out in 1818 as "Newberry"; post office closed 1971. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulberry,_Ohio

## 3. Permits (unincorporated Miami Township, Clermont County)
- **Clermont County Permit Central / Building Inspection Department**, 2275 Bauer Road, issues building, electrical and mechanical permits on one application. "Heating Systems" and "Cooling Systems" are on its list of projects that need a permit; electrical and mechanical work gets rough and final inspections. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/frequently-asked-questions ; http://building.clermontcountyohio.gov/Inspections.aspx
- County monthly reports list residential "HVAC Replacement" permits by township. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2026/PERMITS%20DETAILED%20APRIL%202026.pdf

## 4. Utilities
- Electric: **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers power; Miami Township runs an opt-out electric aggregation program (supplier only). Rates change; don't quote. https://www.miamitwpoh.gov/PDF/PopDocs/050620-Aggregation.pdf
- Water: Greater Cincinnati Water Works lists Miami Township (Clermont) as outside its service area. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/water/about/outside-our-service-area . Which utility serves an address: unverified.
