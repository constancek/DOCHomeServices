# Cleves, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'cleves'):**
- "Older homes" overstates the age. The median home was built in 1972, and 42.4% date from 1980 or later (19.8% from 2000 or later). 26.0% predate 1940 (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3916028/
- "Older homes on low ground prone to flooding" applies to the whole village in the copy. NWS names only flooding "along Route 50 in Cleves" at a 32 ft Great Miami stage (section 6). Which streets flood: unverified.
- Cleves doesn't touch the Ohio: North Bend separates it from the river along its southern border. "Near where the Great Miami meets the Ohio" is fine. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleves,_Ohio
- "Aging plumbing" and backwater-valve advice: no source found (unverified).

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Great Miami River at Miamitown** gauge (MIAO1, the nearest NWS gauge upstream): minor flood 16 ft, moderate 24 ft, major 30 ft. NWS impacts: at 32 ft (about the FEMA 1 percent flood) "flooding along Route 50 in Cleves"; at 38 ft "particularly hard hit are low areas along the river from New Baltimore to Cleves." Highest listed crest 33.4 ft (January 29, 1959); recent 23.26 ft (April 6, 2025). https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/MIAO1
- **Ohio River at Cincinnati** (CCNO1): minor 52 ft, moderate 56 ft, major 65 ft. NWS notes backwater flooding up the Great Miami at major stages and, at 70 ft, "downstream of Cincinnati, most property between Route 50 and the river is flooded." https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1
- Parcel flood zones: unverified; check https://msc.fema.gov
- **Historic:** no historic district. The Cincinnati and Whitewater Canal Tunnel (National Register, May 25, 2001) runs parallel to Miami Ave at Wamsley Ave, shared with North Bend. Not a residential district; no local design review found. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Hamilton_County,_Ohio
