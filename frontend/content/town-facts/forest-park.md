# Forest Park, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'forest-park'):**
- "planned from the 1960s" is wrong. Warner-Kanter began it in the 1950s; the first homes opened in March 1956. It became a village in 1961 and a city in 1968. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Park,_Ohio
- "neighborhoods of mid-century single-family homes" overstates it. The median home dates from 1969 and 58.5% were built 1960-79, but 26.7% of units are in buildings of 5+ units and 45.2% of households rent (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3927706/
- "On call every day" / "weekends included" are company claims; check against stats.md (not a town fact).

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County, about 6.46 sq mi. Population 20,189 (2020 census), the second-largest city in the county. ZIP 45240. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Park,_Ohio
- Borders Fairfield and Springdale, the village of Greenhills, Springfield and Colerain townships, and Butler County. Same source.
- Built on land first set aside in 1935 for the federal Greenbelt town (Greenhills). Same source.

## 3. Permits (City of Forest Park)
- The city **Building Department** issues building permits. Its permit list includes "new or replacement furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps", and the code requires a separate permit application for each structure, HVAC, fire protection and sign. https://www.forestpark.org/government/building/building_permit_application.php ; https://www.forestpark.org/government/building/index.php
- HVAC installations (new or replacement equipment) are inspected by the Forest Park Building Department. https://www.forestpark.org/government/building/inspection.php
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Health Department, Plumbing Inspection Division. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). https://www.forestpark.org/business/business_permitting_information.php
- The city adopts the 2019 Residential Code of Ohio. Applications go through the city's OpenGov portal. https://www.forestpark.org/government/building/building_permit_application.php
- Fees ($295 minimum is written for new-home construction), timings and what a straight replacement needs: unverified. Don't quote.
