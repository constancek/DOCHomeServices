# Newtonsville, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'newtonsville'): ZIP 45158, "small rural village in northeastern Clermont County", older homes, "many rural properties run on wells and septic". See the end.

## 1. Where it is
- **Former village, dissolved.** Residents voted 53 to 46 on November 5, 2019 to dissolve; dissolution took effect November 25, 2019. Now an unincorporated community in **Wayne Township**. Platted 1838; 2010 population 392. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtonsville,_Ohio ; https://www.cincinnati.com/story/news/2019/11/06/election-2019-amelia-and-newtonsville-voted-not-exist/4175623002/
- No Census place exists for it now (ACS 2024 has no data for former place 16000US3955664; script check).
- Geocoded: 50, 100, 200 and 300 Main St = tract 401.02 block group 1; 280 Pin Oak St and 323 W Main St = block group 2. All Wayne Township (06000US3902582110). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Pin Oak St appears in county "Newtonsville sewer changeover" permits, so the old village sits in these block groups, which reach well beyond it.

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Septic replaced by county sewer in the old village.** A 2011 survey of 171 septic systems found about 40% failing or at risk; Ohio EPA ordered a sanitary sewer in November 2019. The county plant is sized for 57,000 gallons a day; project cost $12,391,610. https://www.clermontsun.com/2019/12/31/newtonsville-sewer-project-resolution-expected-in-january
- The 2025 county report lists 13 "Newtonsville sewer changeover" permits in Wayne Township (e.g. 280 Pin Oak St, 433 Newtonsville Rd), so homes are now tying in. Completion date and whether connection is mandatory: unverified. Report above.
- Wells outside the sewer area: unverified.
