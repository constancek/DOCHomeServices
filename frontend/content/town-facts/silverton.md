# Silverton, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'silverton'):**
- Calls Silverton "a small city". It is a **village**: a city from 1961, it reverted to village status when the 2010 census put it under 5,000 people. The village's own forms say "Village of Silverton". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silverton,_Ohio ; https://silvertonohio.us/download/zoning/Silverton-Bldg-Zoning-Application.pdf
- "Early- and mid-1900s homes" is only half right. Just 10.6% of homes predate 1940; the median home was built in 1959 and 41.7% date from 1940-59 (section 2). Say mid-century, not early-1900s.
- "Older homes" framing skips that 58.6% of occupied units are rented and only 49.9% are single-family detached; 46.1% are in buildings of 3 or more units (section 2).
- "Corrosion leaks and failing water heaters are routine": unverified, no source.
- No `adjacent` entry. Neighbors are unverified (see section 1).

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County (village 1904, city 1961 to 2010, village again after the 2010 census). Charter, council-manager government. 1.11 sq mi, all land. Population 4,908 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silverton,_Ohio
- ZIP 45236. The ZIP is much bigger than the village (18,539 housing units vs 2,911), so use village figures, not ZIP figures. Census block output from census.mjs, sources in section 2.
- Village Hall: 6943 Montgomery Rd. https://silvertonohio.us/download/zoning/Silverton-Bldg-Zoning-Application.pdf
- Shares a joint fire district with Deer Park, Golf Manor and parts of Columbia Township. Which places actually border Silverton: unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silverton,_Ohio

## 3. Permits (Village of Silverton)
- The village FAQ lists **new or replacement furnaces and air conditioners** among the work that needs a permit. Building and Zoning Permit applications come from Village Hall or the site's Documents & Forms section and go to the Clerk's Office with a deposit. https://silvertonohio.us/faq/ ; https://silvertonohio.us/building-and-zoning-permits/
- The village contracts with **National Inspection Corporation (NIC)** to enforce its building and electrical codes (page dated August 2018). https://silvertonohio.us/building-and-zoning-permits/
- The village site also posts a Hamilton County Planning & Development 2021 fee schedule with HVAC lines, so the county may handle some permits now. Whether NIC or Hamilton County currently issues HVAC permits: **unverified**. https://silvertonohio.us/download/zoning/2021-Hamilton-Co-Fee-Schedule.pdf
