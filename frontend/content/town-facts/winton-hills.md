## Where it is

- 2020 Census: Wikipedia gives both 5,684 and 5,648 residents and 2,392 housing units against 2,646 households, so its figures don't add up. The city's 2020 profile would settle it but cincinnati-oh.gov refused connections. Unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winton_Hills,_Cincinnati

## Who owns the housing
- Winton Terrace: built 1940-41 by CMHA with the PWA; 92 two-storey red-brick Colonial Revival buildings with 750 units on about 44 acres; most units are two-storey townhouse-style with basements. Listed on the National Register as the Winton Terrace Historic District (nomination ref. SG100008657; listing date 10 March 2023 per a city list seen only in a search summary). https://ohiohistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/SG100008657-Winton-Terrace-Historic-District-Hamilton-County.pdf
- Findlater Gardens: CMHA public housing added in 1958. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winton_Hills,_Cincinnati
- Unit counts conflict. A CMHA 2024 RFP addendum (seen only in a search summary; cintimha.com redirected to its homepage) lists 653 at Findlater Gardens and 608 at Winton Terrace, against the nomination's 750. Unverified. https://cintimha.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/sol-2024-1067-addendum-1.pdf
- Winton Terrace Phase 1 (185 units, 29 buildings) is converting from public housing to project-based vouchers for substantial interior and exterior rehab, per a city environmental notice dated 3 Sept 2024 (search summary only; city site unreachable). Whether HVAC is in scope is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/community-development/assets/Combined-FONSI-NOI-RROF-Winton-Terrace.pdf
- What this means: in CMHA units the housing authority or its property manager handles repairs through work orders, not the tenant. https://cintimha.com/ (site menu: "How do I submit a work order (PH)?")

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 80, Hamilton County)
Tract 80 is 99.96% inside Cincinnati. Margins of error are wide (population 4,681 +/- 883). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061008000-census-tract-80-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1955. Of 2,696 units, 786 are coded 1939 or earlier, 846 from 1940-1959, 798 from 1960-1989, 137 from the 1990s and 129 from 2000 or later. The ACS "1939 or earlier" count likely includes Winton Terrace (1940-41); treat era splits loosely.
- Units in structure: 338 single-family detached, 608 attached, 414 two-family, 159 in 3-4 units, 312 in 5-9, 865 in buildings of 10+.
- Tenure: 2,464 of 2,540 occupied units rented (97.0%); 76 owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 1,484 electricity (58.4%), 970 utility gas (38.2%), 21 propane.
- Block group 1 (Winton Terrace and Findlater Gardens addresses): 1,237 occupied units, all rented; 734 gas, 503 electric heat. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610080001-bg-1-tract-80-hamilton-oh/
