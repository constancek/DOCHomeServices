## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County (village 1851, city 1932). 2.89 sq mi, all land. An inner suburb north of Cincinnati. Population 10,600 (2020 census). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading,_Ohio
- Mill Creek forms the western border with Lockland and Arlington Heights; the 1901 Benson Street Bridge crosses it at the Lockland line. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading,_Ohio
- ZIP 45215 is much bigger than the city (13,648 housing units vs 5,312). Use city figures, not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45215/

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Mill Creek flooding:** the city says Mill Creek flooding "has plagued the city of Reading since its founding," and revised FEMA maps put many Reading households and businesses in the 100-year floodplain, raising flood insurance premiums. A 2016-17 plan studied lowering the base flood elevation. https://www.readingohio.org/page/floodplain-remediation-plan
- Relevance: equipment in basements of floodplain homes (furnaces, water heaters, panels) is exposed to flood water. Which streets are in the floodplain and how many homes: unverified (county report returned 403). Check parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
- Upstream gauge: Mill Creek at Evendale (NOAA EVNO1); 16.8 ft roughly matches a 1% (100-year) flood. https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/evno1
- **Historic districts:** none found. No Reading entries in the Hamilton County National Register list; no local historic district on the city site. Treat as none, unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Hamilton_County,_Ohio
