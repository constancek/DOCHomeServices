# Montgomery, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'montgomery'):**
- `adjacent` lists Madeira. Madeira does not border Montgomery (Kenwood, in Sycamore Township, sits between). Actual neighbors are Blue Ash, Indian Hill, Sycamore Township and Symmes Township (Census TIGERweb boundary query). https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/tigerWMS_Current/MapServer
- "preserved historic district of 19th-century buildings" overstates it. The only National Register district is the Universalist Church Historic District (3 buildings). The city's "Heritage District" is a shopping and dining area along Montgomery Road, and the Landmarks Commission only makes recommendations (see section 5).
- "neighborhoods of established and newer homes" is misleading. Median year built is 1976 and only 13.6% of homes date from 2000 or later (section 2).

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in **Hamilton County** (village 1910, city 1971), settled 1795. 5.3 sq mi. Grew by annexing land from Symmes Township from 1973. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery,_Ohio
- ZIP **45242**, which it shares with Blue Ash and parts of Sycamore and Symmes townships. Use city figures, not ZIP figures. (same source)
- Montgomery Road (US 22/OH 3) is the main street. I-71 and I-275 meet near the city. https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/about/
- Population 10,855; median household income $150,995; median owner-occupied home value $545,100 (ACS 2020-2024 5-yr). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3951716/

## 3. Permits (City of Montgomery)
- The **City of Montgomery Community Development Department** issues building and zoning permits. The city's permit list says a building permit is required for "new or replacement heating, ventilation, air conditioning equipment or ducts, and gas lines" and for new wood-burning stoves or fireplaces. https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/required-permits/
- The city contracts building, electrical and plumbing plan review and inspections to outside agencies (agency not named). https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/2024-community-development-annual-report/
- **Plumbing permits** are issued by the **Hamilton County General Health District** (plumbing division), not the city. https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/required-permits/
- Sewer: Hamilton County Metropolitan Sewer District. Water: Greater Cincinnati Water Works. (same source)
- **Unverified:** fees, turnaround times and which outside agency does the inspections. Don't state them.

## 4. Utilities
- **Duke Energy** supplies gas and electric, per the city's own permit page. https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/required-permits/
- Duke Energy Ohio residential HVAC rebates: none current (see norwood.md). City aggregation program: unverified.

## 5. Historic and local factors
- National Register: **Universalist Church Historic District** (listed Dec 2, 1970; 3 buildings, Federal/Classical Revival). Also listed: Blair House, Montgomery Saltbox Houses, Wilder-Swaim House, Yost Tavern. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universalist_Church_Historic_District
- The **Landmarks Commission** (est. 1976, seven volunteers) has identified 32 local landmarks and makes recommendations to Planning Commission and City Council. https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/landmarks-commission/ and https://www.montgomeryohio.gov/historic-landmarks/
- **Unverified:** whether exterior equipment (condensers, vents, meters) on a landmark or in the Heritage District needs design review. Don't claim it.
- Flood risk: nothing town-specific found. Unverified; check FEMA by address. https://msc.fema.gov
