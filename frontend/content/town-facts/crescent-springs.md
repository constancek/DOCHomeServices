# Crescent Springs, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'crescent-springs'): ZIP 41017, "suburban Kenton County city, with established neighborhoods of mid-century and newer homes".
**Errors in locations.ts:** "mid-century" is wrong. Median year built is 1993, only 5.4% of units predate 1960, and 65.6% were built 1980 or later. The entry also implies a town of houses, but only 36.0% of units are detached houses, 49.8% are in 5+ unit buildings and 42.0% of households rent. 61.8% heat with electricity, the highest of the five ZIP 41017 cities checked. No `adjacent` list (Villa Hills, Erlanger, Fort Mitchell nearby; borders **unverified**). "Any day of the week" is an hours claim the brief keeps out of body copy.
