# Fort Thomas, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'fort-thomas'): ZIP 41075, a city built around a former U.S. Army post, "stately early-1900s homes" above the river, original plumbing.
- **Error/overstatement:** ACS does not support "early-1900s homes" as typical: median year built is 1955 and only 32.1% predate 1940 (section 2). "Mid-century and older" is accurate.
- **Adjacent list is incomplete:** it lists only Newport and Bellevue. Fort Thomas also borders Highland Heights, Southgate, Dayton and Woodlawn, and faces Cincinnati across the Ohio River (from map reading; unverified, don't use in copy without checking).
- "Original plumbing" in these homes: unverified.

## 1. Where it is
- Fort Thomas is an incorporated city in Campbell County, Kentucky. City offices: General Services Department, 130 N. Fort Thomas Avenue, Fort Thomas, KY 41075. https://fortthomasky.org/departments/general-services/
- ZIP 41075 covers almost exactly the city (ACS: 6,753 units in ZCTA 41075 vs 6,764 in the city). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41075-41075/

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Fort Thomas city, place 16000US2128594)
Source: tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2128594-fort-thomas-ky/
- Population 17,169. Housing units 6,764.
- Median year built: **1955**.
- Built 1939 or earlier: 2,169 (32.1%). 1940s: 622 (9.2%). 1950s: 1,231 (18.2%). Built before 1960 in total: 4,022 (59.5%). Built 2010 or later: 227 (3.4%).
- Home types: 4,652 (68.8%) single-family detached, 179 attached. 709 (10.5%) in 2-4 unit buildings, 1,220 (18.0%) in buildings of 5 or more units.
- Occupied units 6,568: owner-occupied 4,676 (71.2%), renter-occupied 1,892 (28.8%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 4,418 (67.3%), **electricity 2,062 (31.4%)**, propane 49 (0.7%), fuel oil 0, other 17, none 22.

## 3. Permits
- **HVAC permits** for one and two family dwellings are issued by the **City of Fort Thomas General Services Department**. The form covers replacements and asks for heat gain/loss figures. Fee printed on form: $105 first system, $50 each additional (don't use in copy). https://fortthomasky.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/HVAC-CONSTRUCTION-PERMIT-APPLICATION-ONE-TWO-FAMILY-2025.pdf
- **Building permits**: City of Fort Thomas General Services ("building permits" in its mission statement; Building Inspector/Zoning Administrator on staff). https://fortthomasky.org/departments/general-services/
- **Electrical permits** are issued by the city (General Services), but the form says to contact a **state electrical inspector** for the inspection. https://fortthomasky.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ELECTRICAL-PERMIT-APP-2025.pdf
- The city's permit forms require contractors to hold a current Fort Thomas occupational license. Same two forms.
- The state inspector list for Campbell County names the City of Fort Thomas inspector for single-family dwellings. Plumbing inspections are listed under a state plumbing inspector. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf

## 4. Historic and design review
- The **Fort Thomas Military Reservation District** is on the National Register (ref. 86001103), roughly bounded by Pearson, Alexander and Cochran Aves., River Rd. and S. Fort Thomas Ave.; significance period 1875-1949. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/86001103
- The city's Design Review Board issues Certificates of Appropriateness for exterior work in the Midway and Central districts (form lists "Midway", "Central District", "Other"). Whether mechanical equipment needs one: unverified. https://fortthomasky.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/CERTIFICATE-OF-APPROPRIATENESS-REVISED-Design-Review-Board.pdf

## 5. Terrain
- Northern Kentucky has unstable hillside slopes from clay-rich soils over Kope Formation shale, worse in heavy rain. Regional, not Fort Thomas specific. https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1032/pdf/Kentucky.pdf
- Specific Fort Thomas landslides or flood plain exposure: unverified.

## 6. Utilities
- Electric: **Duke Energy Kentucky**. Duke's 2017 rate-case notice lists Fort Thomas. https://psc.ky.gov/pscecf/2017-00321/adele.frisch@duke-energy.com/10162017011142/CAMPBELL.pdf
- Natural gas: Duke Energy Kentucky's gas tariff covers Campbell County (unverified for Fort Thomas by name). https://psc.ky.gov/tariffs/Natural%20Gas/Local%20Distribution%20Companies/Duke%20Energy%20Kentucky/Cancelled%20Tariff%20Pages/2019/Cover%20Page/03-29.pdf
- Water: Northern Kentucky Water District runs a Fort Thomas treatment plant (retail supplier citywide unverified). https://wris.ky.gov/Portal/DWPrjData/WX21117208

## 7. Climate
- Official station is CVG in Boone County, about 15 miles west (distance approximate). NWS CVG July normal high: 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
