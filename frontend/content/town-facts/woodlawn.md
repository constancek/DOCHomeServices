# Woodlawn, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'woodlawn'):**
- "Older homes here also run on aging plumbing" and "the older homes run on aging plumbing" contradict the Census: the village's median home was built in 1987, 60.2% of units were built 1980 or later, and only 7.1% predate 1940 (section 2). Drop the "older homes" framing.
- "Low Mill Creek valley ground raises the risk of backups in heavy rain": no source found placing Woodlawn homes in a Mill Creek flood plain or tying it to sewer backups. Unverified; check parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
- "A mix of residential streets and industrial corridors": unverified (no government source found).
- It is written as a village of homes, but 49.3% of units sit in buildings of 5+ units and 66.6% of households rent (section 2). Owner-occupied single-family homes are the minority.
