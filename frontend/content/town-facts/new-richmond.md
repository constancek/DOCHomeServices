# New Richmond, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'new-richmond'): ZIP 45157, Clermont County, historic Ohio River village, abolitionist roots, "lined with 19th-century homes along the riverfront", "century-old homes carry original plumbing", high water and backups. Flood risk is confirmed below. The housing-age framing overstates it; see the end.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Ohio Township (the Beckjord power station site is in Pierce Township). Established 1814; merged with the village of Susanna in 1828. 2020 population 2,727. James G. Birney published the abolitionist paper *The Philanthropist* here until 1836. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Richmond,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 102 Willow St, 212 Front St and 300 Front St, New Richmond 45157 = New Richmond village (16000US3955384), tract 416, Ohio Township. ZIP 45157 also covers rural Ohio and Monroe township land outside the village. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Ohio River flooding, verified (NWS gauge CCNO1, Ohio River at Cincinnati; no gauge at New Richmond).** Action 40 ft, minor 52, moderate 56, major 65. NWS impact notes: at 53 ft New Richmond "also experiences flooding"; at 55 ft backwater up Twelvemile Creek floods low areas of northwest New Richmond; at 58 ft "Homes in New Richmond from Market Street to the river flood" and basements and main levels take water; at 63-64 ft "Much of the Village of New Richmond experiences major flooding"; 70 ft is just above the FEMA 1% flood. Record 80.0 ft (January 1937). Recent crests: 60.91 ft (April 7, 2025), 55.46 ft (February 19, 2025). https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1 (data via https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1)
- The 1937 flood destroyed 250 of the village's 415 homes; later floods 1964, 1997 and 2018. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Richmond,_Ohio
- **Historic district: none found.** National Register lists only single buildings here (Ross-Gowdy House; the Water Works, razed 2012). A local village design-review rule: unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Clermont_County,_Ohio
