## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated city in Hamilton County, named "The Village of Indian Hill" (a city since passing 5,000 people). About 18.7 sq mi; 2020 census population 6,087. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Hill,_Ohio
- Census geocoder places addresses on Drake Rd, Given Rd and 9100 Shawnee Run Rd in the city (place 16000US3976582), mailing ZIP 45243 (shared with Madeira; 7550 Shawnee Run Rd geocodes to Madeira). Source: census.mjs geocode output.

## 4. Utilities
- Water: **Indian Hill Water Works**, city-owned, serving Indian Hill, Terrace Park, Camp Dennison, about half of Madeira and parts of Montgomery, Milford, Symmes and Columbia townships. Groundwater from wells on the Little Miami aquifer, **softened** at the plant (7100 Glendale Milford Rd), with emergency connections to Greater Cincinnati Water Works. https://indianhill.gov/departments/water-works (search excerpt; page blocked direct fetch)
- Sewer: MSD of Greater Cincinnati where sewered; unsewered Hamilton County properties use septic or home aeration systems. Share of Indian Hill on septic: **unverified**. https://msdgc.org/projects/sewer-assessments/
