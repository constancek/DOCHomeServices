## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County (village 1874, city 1951, council-manager government). About 2.9 sq mi, all land. About 12 miles north of downtown Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyoming,_Ohio
- Population 8,723 (ACS 2020-2024 5-yr); median household income $165,188; median owner home value $430,000. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3986730-wyoming-oh/
- ZIP 45215 is much bigger than the city (13,648 housing units vs 3,095). Use city figures, not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45215-45215/

## 3. Permits (City of Wyoming)
- The city's **Community Development Department** issues residential building permits and zoning certificates for 1-3 family homes. The city's own list of work needing "a zoning certificate and/or building permit from the City" includes installing or replacing a furnace or air conditioner. https://wyomingohio.gov/159/Permit-Information
- **Electrical permits:** Inspection Bureau Incorporated (named on the city page). Same source.
- **Plumbing permits:** Hamilton County Public Health. Same source.
- **Commercial** (4+ dwelling units, non-residential): Hamilton County Building Department issues building permits on the city's behalf. Same source.
- Unverified: fees, processing times, and whether a like-for-like equipment swap is handled as a zoning certificate or a full building permit.

## 4. Electric utility
- **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers power and bills. The city runs an opt-out electric aggregation program (approved by voters November 2019, 83.1%; supplier contract via Energy Alliances, April 2020) and a natural gas aggregation program. https://wyomingohio.gov/360/Energy-Aggregation-Program and https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/ohio-customer-choice/aggregation
- Current aggregation supplier and rate: unverified. Rebates: none confirmed; don't promise any.
