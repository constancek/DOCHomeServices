## 4. Historic district (local)
- Dayton has a **local** historic district (created 2011) along Sixth Avenue, from O'Fallon Avenue into Main Street. Exterior alterations need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Board of Architectural Review; interiors are exempt. Whether outdoor condensers need one: **unverified**. https://linknky.com/news/2024/02/16/dayton-historic-preservation-guidelines/
- National Register: Dayton City Hall, Dayton High School, Doyle Country Club and Terrace Gardens are individually listed; no National Register district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Campbell_County,_Kentucky

## 5. Flood
- NWS gauge Ohio River at Cincinnati (CCNO1): action stage 40 ft, flood 52, moderate 56, major 65. Record crest 80 ft (Jan 1937). Recent crests: 60.91 ft (Apr 7, 2025), 55.46 ft (Feb 19, 2025). https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1
- NWS impact notes: at 56-59 ft, KY Route 8 floods in low areas; at 70 ft, "widespread inundation occurs in unprotected areas between Route 8 and the river." Same source.
- The 1937 flood affected 80% of the city; the floodwall was completed in late 1981. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Kentucky
- SD1 runs two Dayton pump stations: Main Street (starts at 47.00 ft) and McKinney Street (49.20 ft). The city maintains the levees, floodwalls and road closures. https://sd1.org/445/Flood-Levee-Protection-System
