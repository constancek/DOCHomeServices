# West End (Cincinnati, OH 45203 / 45214): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives only ZIP 45203. The West End is split between 45203 and 45214, and 45203 also covers parts of the CBD and Lower Price Hill. https://www.city-data.com/neighborhood/West-End-Cincinnati-OH.html (secondary; confirm on a USPS/Census ZCTA map)
- "Low ground near the Mill Creek basin raises the risk of sewer backups" is unverified. Mill Creek runs west of the neighbourhood, past Queensgate. No FEMA or MSD source tying West End addresses to Mill Creek flooding was found. Don't use it.
- "Decades-old galvanized and cast-iron plumbing" is unverified as a West End fact. It's a general inference from the build years below.
- The "historic Italianate rowhouses" line holds up. Italianate dominates Betts-Longworth, and 1-unit attached is the most common single-home type in tract 264 (see below).

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, just west of downtown and Over-the-Rhine, in Hamilton County. 2020 Census: 6,824 residents, using tracts 2, 264, 269 and block group 1 of tract 265. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/WestEnd_2020.pdf
- Mid-20th-century clearance for highways and the Queensgate industrial park cut the population from 67,520 (1950) to 17,068 (1970). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- TQL Stadium (1501 Central Parkway) is in the West End. https://www.citybeat.com/arts/fc-cincinnatis-west-end-stadium-gets-a-new-name-12264766/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year; tracts 2, 264, 269 plus tract 265 BG 1, combined)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061000200-census-tract-2-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026400-census-tract-264-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026900-census-tract-269-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610265001-block-group-1-tract-265-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040)
- 3,878 housing units. Median year built varies a lot by tract: 1938 (tract 269), 1965 (tract 2), 1977 (265 BG 1), 1991 (tract 264).
- By era: 1,489 units (38.4%) built 1939 or earlier; 956 (24.7%) built 1960-1979; 437 (11.3%) built 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 424 detached single-family, 660 attached single-family (rowhouse/townhouse), 330 two-unit, 502 in 3-4 unit buildings, 732 in 5-9 unit, 388 in 10-19, 305 in 20-49, 537 in 50+.
- Tenure: 2,724 of 3,129 occupied units are rented (87.1%); 405 owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 1,500 utility gas (47.9%), 1,386 electricity (44.3%), 49 fuel oil, the rest other or none.
- ZIP 45203 as a whole: median year built 1978, 413 gas vs 599 electric heat. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45203-45203/

## Permits (City of Cincinnati)
- HVAC: Department of Buildings & Inspections mechanical permit for installing or replacing heating, ventilating and air conditioning equipment. Window AC units are exempt. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections/ ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-permit-forms-applications/application-forms/all-forms-handouts-checklists-alphabetical-list/hvac-informational-handout/
- Plumbing: B&I Plumbing Section, including repair, alteration and private-property sewers. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/plumbing-inspections/
- Electrical: the city contracts review and inspection to Inspections Bureau, Inc. (IBI). https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/faq/
- Water-heater-only permit requirement, fees and timings: unverified.

## Utilities
- Electric and natural gas: Duke Energy Ohio (City of Cincinnati). https://www.duke-energy.com/home
- Sewer: MSD of Greater Cincinnati. Owners own the private building sewer up to the public connection, including under sidewalks. MSD says about 85% of backups aren't caused by the public sewer. Report within 48 hours to be eligible. https://msdgc.org/programs/sewer-backup-program/about-the-program/not-eligible-private-sewer-backups/ ; https://msdgc.org/programs/sewer-backup-program/

## Other local factors
- Dayton Street Historic District (National Register 1973; Bank, Linn, Poplar Sts and Winchell Ave), 1850-1890 mansions. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/national-register-of-historic-places/nr-districts/
- Betts-Longworth Historic District (local guidelines adopted 1982; National Register 1983; Ezzard Charles Dr, Central Ave, Mound and Old Court Sts), mostly late-19th-century Italianate. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-conservation-guidelines/betts-longworth-historic-district/
- In local districts, exterior changes need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic Conservation Board. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/certificate-of-appropriateness/ Whether a rooftop or yard condenser counts as a reviewable exterior change is unverified. Dayton Street's local-district status was only confirmed through a 2016 Historic Conservation Board staff report. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation-board-archived-cases/june-27-2016-staff-reports-and-attachments/
- FEMA flood zone status: not checked, unverified.
