# Turpin Hills (Anderson Township, OH 45244): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says Turpin Hills has "mid-century and newer homes". Most homes were built from 1960 to 1999. The median build year is 1977, and only 6.8% date from 1940-59 (see Housing), so the homes are mostly 1960s-1990s, not mid-century. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3977840/
- The claim that worn supply lines and failing sump pumps "account for most emergency plumbing" is unverified and has no source.
- ZIP 45244 is confirmed for 2650 Bartels Rd. Whether parts of the CDP fall in 45230 or 45255 is unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Where it is
- Turpin Hills is a census-designated place in unincorporated Anderson Township, Hamilton County. It covers about 2.97 sq mi of land, and its 2020 Census population was 5,162. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turpin_Hills,_Ohio
- 2650 Bartels Rd (45244) geocodes to Turpin Hills CDP, tract 249.03, Anderson Township. The 7650 Forest Rd and 8000 Beechmont Ave addresses fall in Forestville CDP, so they are not in Turpin Hills. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Anderson Township is bounded by the Little Miami and Ohio Rivers. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/community/about

## Permits
- **Zoning first:** the Anderson Township Planning & Zoning Department issues zoning certificates. For most improvements it needs two copies of a site plan showing setbacks to the property lines. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/Portals/andersontownship/Documents/Forms/2022%20Forms/Q%20_%20A%20Planning%20_%20Zoning%202022.pdf
- **Building permits:** the township says that, apart from fences and smaller detached structures, most improvements also need a building permit from Hamilton County (it names "Hamilton County Planning & Development"; the county office is Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections, from _shared.md). Same source as above; https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health (from _shared.md). https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI) (from _shared.md). https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **HVAC/mechanical:** unverified. Only a third-party permit site says the county issues mechanical permits for Anderson Township, which isn't good enough. Whether a condenser swap needs a township zoning certificate is also unverified.

## Other
- Historic: the 1796 Miller-Leuser Log House on Clough Pike has a state historical marker placing it in Turpin Hills. It's a single site, not a district. No historic district was found (unverified). https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=133284
- Flood: Clough Creek has mapped floodplain along Clough Pike (a 2024 township zoning case put nine lots in the floodplain). Whether that site is inside the CDP is unverified. Most of the CDP sits at about 646 ft, on hill ground above the Little Miami, so flooding is likely not a factor for most homes, but FEMA zones were not checked. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/Portals/andersontownship/Documents/Committees/Zoning/Materials/2024%20Materials/Case%204-2024%20PUD%20Clough%20Creek%20Sub.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turpin_Hills,_Ohio
