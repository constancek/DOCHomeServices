## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated **census-designated place** in western Whitewater Township, Hamilton County, on the west bank of the Great Miami River at Harrison Pike and State Route 128. ZIP 45041. About 1.37 sq mi. Population 1,256 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miamitown,_Ohio
- 58.5% of households are one person, and 28.6% are people 65+ living alone. Same source.
