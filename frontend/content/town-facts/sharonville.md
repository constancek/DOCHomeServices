# Sharonville, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'sharonville'):**
- "Mid-century homes" overstates the age. The median home was built in 1974; only 16.1% date from 1940-59, and 1980-99 (35.8%) and 1960-79 (34.8%) are the biggest building periods (section 2).
- `county` gives Hamilton County only. The city is mostly in Hamilton County but extends into Butler County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharonville,_Ohio
- "North-side city" reads like a Cincinnati neighborhood. Sharonville is its own city, with its own permit office (section 3). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharonville,_Ohio
- ZIP is given as 45241 only. Some addresses inside the city mail as 45246 (the Census geocoder matched 11355 Chester Rd as "Cincinnati, OH 45246" inside Sharonville city, place 16000US3971892). ZIP list beyond these two: unverified.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city**, largely in Hamilton County with a part in Butler County. Village 1911, city 1962. About 9.9 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharonville,_Ohio

## 3. Permits (City of Sharonville)
- The city's **Department of Community Development** issues City of Sharonville building and zoning permits. Its list of projects needing a permit includes "Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC)" and electrical work. Apply with the Application for Building/Electrical/Zoning Approval plus two sets of plans (or email permits@cityofsharonville.com). https://sharonville.org/m/faq?cat=22
- **Electrical permits:** issued by the City of Sharonville. **Plumbing permits:** issued and inspected by Hamilton County Public Health. https://www.sharonville.org/DocumentCenter/View/4576/Residential-Permitting-Guide-2026 (via search summary; the PDF was too large to open directly, so treat the wording as unverified).
- The same guide reportedly names National Inspection Corporation for plan review and inspection: unverified which trades it covers.
- Unverified: fees, timings, and whether a like-for-like HVAC swap needs the two sets of plans.

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Floodplain:** the city says "approximately 20% or more of properties in Sharonville are located in the FEMA designated floodplain." It administers a Flood Damage Reduction Ordinance and "processes and approves Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) permits for work in the designated floodplain." Mill Creek runs through the city. https://sharonville.org/239/HAMCO-Storm-Water-District-FEMA-Flood-Pl
- Whether an SFHA permit applies to replacing outdoor HVAC equipment at a floodplain address: unverified. Check parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
- A roughly $3.7 million project replaced the downtown Main Street culvert on a Mill Creek tributary with an open channel; the city expects it to take most downtown properties out of the 100-year floodplain, but FEMA had not remapped as of July 2024. https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2024-07-12/sharonville-safer-flooding-mill-creek-project (news event: mention at most once in copy)
- Historic districts: none found. Twelve Mile House (1842) is an individual National Register listing, not a district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_Mile_House . No city historic review rule found.
