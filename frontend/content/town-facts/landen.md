## 1. Where it is
- Census-designated place (not a city or village) in Deerfield Township, Warren County, around Columbia Rd and Montgomery Rd in the southeast of the township, surrounding Twenty Mile Stand, near Fosters. 2020 population 6,995. Elevation 817 ft. Named after settler Theodophilus James Landen. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landen,_Ohio
- Landen Lake is man-made, formed by damming Simpson's Creek as a flood-retention area ("Landen Farm Drainage Lake"). (same source; dredging detail is uncited there, don't use)
- Census geocoder: 8580 Landen Cove, 8779 Landen Dr and 8553 Island Pines Dr, all Maineville OH 45039, fall in Landen CDP, Deerfield Township (tracts 320.03 and 320.11). 7401 Landen Falls Dr (apartments) is just outside the CDP. (run via census.mjs)
