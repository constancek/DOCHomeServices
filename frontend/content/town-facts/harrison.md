**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'harrison'):**
- "Older homes" leads the copy, but Harrison is mostly new. The median home was built in 1992, and 42.4% date from 2000 or later; only 6.6% predate 1940 (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3933838/
- Heating is mostly electric: 64.5% of occupied units heat with electricity (heat pumps or furnaces, split unknown), only 34.0% with gas. Copy that assumes gas furnaces misses that. Same source.
- 18.9% of units are in 5+ unit buildings and 109 are mobile homes. Same source.
- "Low ground near the Whitewater adds a backup risk": the Whitewater River flood risk is real (section 6), but which homes are affected is unverified.
- "On call every day" / "any day of the week" are company claims; check stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- **City** (incorporated 1850, a city since 1981) in Harrison Township, Hamilton County, bordering West Harrison, Indiana. ZIP 45030. About 5 sq mi (sources disagree: 4.96 or 5.37). Population 12,563 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison,_Ohio
- An F4 tornado hit parts of the city on June 2, 1990. Same source.
- City offices: 300 George Street. https://www.harrisonohio.gov/

## 3. Permits (City of Harrison)
- **Building permits and inspections:** issued by the City of Harrison through Cloudpermit. **Zoning permits:** also the city. https://www.harrisonohio.gov/158/Permits
- **HVAC:** the city's Department of Buildings and Inspections handles HVAC permits. Its HVAC sheet: "Permits are required for all new installations, replacement units ... duct rework and any related mechanical devices." Not required for window AC units or duct extensions in additions to one- or two-family homes. For one- and two-family homes, duct layouts and ventilation amounts aren't required. HVAC inspections need 24 hours' notice. Permits expire if no inspection is requested within one year. The sheet is old (it cites the CABO code), so current details are unverified. https://www.harrisonohio.gov/DocumentCenter/View/355/Mechanical--HVAC-Instructions-PDF
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health, Plumbing Division. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). https://www.harrisonohio.gov/158/Permits
- Fees and timings: unverified, don't quote.
