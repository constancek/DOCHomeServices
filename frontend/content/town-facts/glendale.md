## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, a northern suburb of Cincinnati. 2020 census population 2,298. ZIP 45246. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale,_Ohio
- Developed in 1851 when the Cincinnati, Hamilton and Dayton Railway began running; civil engineer Robert Phillips laid out 200 of the 600 acres bought by founders George Crawford and Henry Clark, borrowing from rural cemetery design. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale_Historic_District_(Glendale,_Ohio)

## 4. Historic district rules
- Glendale Historic District: National Register July 20, 1976 (boundary increase December 18, 2018); **National Historic Landmark May 5, 1977**; 392 acres, roughly Coral and Washington Avenues (north), Springfield Pike/SR 4 (west), Oak Street (south), South Troy Avenue (east). Most houses Italianate or Second Empire. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale_Historic_District_(Glendale,_Ohio)
- Local historic district under village zoning code chapter 154 (Ord. 1993-52, November 4, 1993). The **Glendale Planning and Historic Preservation Commission (GPHPC)** reviews work; "a certificate of appropriateness is required for any exterior change to the building or its property." https://www.zoneomics.com/code/glendale-OH/chapter_5 and https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/glendale/latest/glendale_oh/0-0-0-32123
