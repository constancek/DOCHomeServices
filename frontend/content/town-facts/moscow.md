## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** on the Ohio River in **Washington Township**; platted 1816. 2020 population 155. The Zimmer coal plant sits partly inside village limits. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 30 Wells St (council chambers) and 100 Water St, Moscow 45153 = Moscow village (16000US3952416), tract 420.02, Washington Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://villageofmoscow.org/
- ZIP 45153 covers rural Washington Township too; use village figures for "Moscow".

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Ohio River flooding, verified (NWS gauge MELO1, Ohio River at Meldahl Dam, upstream; no gauge at Moscow).** Action 49 ft, minor 51, moderate 58, major 64. Impact notes: at 63 ft "much of Moscow becomes flooded"; at 64 ft several feet of water reach Moscow, "roughly the FEMA 1 percent flood"; at 66 ft "The Villages of Moscow and Neville are completely flooded." Record 76 ft (January 1937). Recent crests: 56.28 ft (April 8, 2025), 54.64 ft (March 4, 2021). https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/MELO1 (data via https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/MELO1)
- The village lists river flooding and the March 2012 tornado among its adversities; the tornado destroyed over 80% of the town. Mention at most once. https://villageofmoscow.org/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Ohio
- A "River Wall Phase 3" permit was issued at 108 Water St in 2025 (county report above). What it protects: unverified.
- **Historic district: none.** No Moscow entries on the National Register list. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Clermont_County,_Ohio
- Parcel flood zones: https://msc.fema.gov (street-level: unverified).
