# Bellevue, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'bellevue'): ZIP 41073, historic Fairfield Avenue business district, late-1800s homes, riverfront flood risk. The ACS data and NRHP listings below back the old-housing and Fairfield Avenue parts, and April 2025 news backs river flooding. Galvanized and cast-iron pipe is unverified.

**Errors in locations.ts:**
- `adjacent` lists Covington. Bellevue does not border Covington (Newport sits between them). The real neighbors are Newport (west), Dayton (east) and Fort Thomas (south). Dayton is missing. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellevue,_Kentucky
- Covington's own `adjacent` list includes Bellevue, which is wrong for the same reason.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city, mayor-council government, in Campbell County, on the Ohio River across from Cincinnati's Mount Adams. 2020 population 5,548; 0.90 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellevue,_Kentucky
- ZIP 41073 lies 100% in Campbell County, and its ACS figures match the city's exactly. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41073-41073/

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Bellevue city, place 16000US2105446)
Source: Census tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2105446-bellevue-ky/ (Census Reporter API, release acs2024_5yr)
- Median year built: **1938**, which falls in the "1939 or earlier" bucket (Campbell County: 1972).
- Of 2,983 units, **1,900 (63.7%) were built 1939 or earlier**. 2,498 (83.7%) were built before 1960. Only 42 (1.4%) were built 2010 or later.
- Home types: 2,023 (67.8%) single-family detached, 62 attached. 459 (15.4%) in 2-4 unit buildings, 396 (13.3%) in 5+ unit buildings, 43 mobile homes.
- Occupied units 2,745: owner 1,923 (70.1%), renter 822 (29.9%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 2,239 (81.6%), **electricity 416 (15.2%)**, propane 59 (2.1%), no fuel 31. County: electricity 43.2%. ACS does not split heat pumps from other electric heat.

## 3. Permits
- Bellevue uses the **Campbell County Planning, Zoning and Building Inspection Department** for building permits, 1098 Monmouth St, Suite 343, Newport. https://bellevueky.org/city-services/city-zoning/
- The state inspector list names Campbell County as the local building and local HVAC office for Bellevue. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- The county is the intake for building, electric and HVAC permits; the city kept "most residential building inspections" (2018 agreement, local news; current split unverified). https://nkytribune.com/?p=151350
- Plumbing inspections: state DHBC plumbing inspector. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: not researched; don't use.

## 4. Historic districts
- Two local districts: Fairfield Avenue and Taylor's Daughters. A Certificate of Appropriateness is required for "most proposed modifications" to exteriors. https://bellevueky.org/about/historic-preservation/
- Whether outdoor condensers or other mechanical equipment need a COA: **unverified** (the city page doesn't say).
- Taylor's Daughters Historic District is NRHP-listed (NRIS 88000101), roughly bounded by O'Fallon, Locust, Retreat, Clark, Chen and Fairfield avenues. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/88000101

## 5. Flood
- April 2025: flooding near Bellevue Beach Park; Frank Benke Way underwater; the mayor estimated about fifty houses affected (local news; article fetch failed, from search summary, so **unverified**). https://linknky.com/news/2025/04/07/region-hit-with-historic-flooding-what-to-know-how-to-help/
- SD1's levee and pump system covers Covington, Newport and Dayton; Bellevue is not named. Whether Bellevue has any floodwall: **unverified**. https://sd1.org/445/Flood-Levee-Protection-System

## 6. Utilities
- Electric and gas: **Duke Energy Kentucky**. The PSC let Duke bid on Bellevue's electric and gas franchise in 2021 (Case 2021-00284). https://psc.ky.gov/Case/ViewCaseFilings/2021-00284
- Sewer provider: not confirmed (likely SD1, **unverified**).

## 7. Climate
- Official station CVG (Boone County). NWS lists the CVG July normal high as 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- About 22 days a year at 90°F or above, calculated from 1991-2020 CVG daily data (not a published normal). https://data.rcc-acis.org
