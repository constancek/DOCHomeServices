# Hebron, KY: verified facts for the AC repair page

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'hebron'; ac-repair-parts/part5.ts): ZIP 41048, Boone County, near CVG, newer subdivisions plus semi-rural homes, open sunny lots. The ACS data below supports the "newer subdivisions" claim. The "semi-rural / wells and septic" claim is in the existing copy and I did not verify it.

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP) in northern Boone County. It became a CDP in the 2010 census. 2020 census population: 6,195 (2010: 5,929). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebron,_Kentucky (cites Census) and https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/hebroncdpkentucky
- ZIP 41048 covers far more ground than the CDP: about 16.5k residents (third-party figure, unverified). Use "ZIP 41048" for the area, not for population.
- Roads: I-275 forms the northern edge. KY 20 (Petersburg Rd) and KY 237 (North Bend Rd) meet near the center. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebron,_Kentucky
- CVG airport is about 3 miles east of the center and uses a Hebron mailing address, but the Census Bureau does not count it inside the CDP. Large distribution centers nearby: Amazon, DHL eCommerce, Wayfair. (same source)
- History: the post office opened in 1858 and took its name from Hebron Lutheran Church. Six sites are on the National Register of Historic Places, including the Hebron Deposit Bank. (same source)

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, ZCTA 41048)
Source: Census tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003 and B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41048-41048/
- Median year built: **2002** (Boone County overall: 1996).
- Of 5,665 housing units, **2,540 (44.8%) were built 2000-2009**, 1,201 were built 1990-1999, 682 in 2010-2019 and 230 in 2020 or later. **82.1% were built in 1990 or later.** Only 497 (8.8%) are pre-1980.
- 5,246 (92.6%) are single-family detached. Townhomes and condos are rare (76 attached, about 310 in 3+ unit buildings, 33 mobile homes).
- Owner-occupied: 4,846 of 5,533 occupied units (87.6%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 3,416 (61.7%), **electricity 1,942 (35.1%)**, propane 94 (1.7%), fuel oil 63 (1.1%).

## 3. What that means for AC (inference from the data above)
- Most homes went up between 1990 and 2009, so builder-installed AC systems are now about 17-36 years old. The 2000s wave (45% of homes) is at or past the usual replacement age.
- Roughly a third of homes heat with electricity. That points to many heat pumps, which cool and heat with the same outdoor unit, so a summer failure also means no heat in winter. ACS does not separate heat pumps from electric furnaces, so don't state a heat-pump percentage.
- These are mostly detached homes on subdivision lots, so a ground-level outdoor condenser is the normal setup. Rooftop or closet-unit condo systems are uncommon.
- A small propane or fuel-oil share (about 3%) suggests some rural homes off the gas main.

## 4. Permits
- Hebron is unincorporated, so the **Boone County Building Department** handles permits. HVAC and HVAC-replacement permits are filed through the county's online portal. Residential HVAC/replacement fee: $105, plus $50 per additional unit (confirm before publishing). Contact: 859-334-2218, bcbd@boonecountyky.org. https://boonecountyky.org/departments/building_inspection/hvac.php
- Kentucky issues HVAC permits to a licensed master HVAC contractor, with a homeowner exception. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/100/

## 5. Electric utility
- Boone County is split between **Duke Energy Kentucky** and **Owen Electric Cooperative**. The provider depends on the address, and PSC filings show the two swapping individual Boone County lots. I could not confirm which utility serves which part of Hebron. https://psc.ky.gov/Case/ViewCaseFilings/2023-00283 and https://psc.ky.gov/pscecf/2023-00020/psc@owenelectric.com/01252023112219/2023-00020_Application_Redacted.pdf
- Owen Electric rebates: $500 for an ENERGY STAR air-source heat pump, $1,000 for a cold-climate or geothermal unit (when replacing resistance heat or an older heat pump). https://owenelectric.com/energy-efficiency-rebates
- Duke Energy HVAC replacement rebate: $300 for AC, $500 or more for heat pumps, minimum 15.2 SEER2, and the condenser and air handler must be replaced together. Unverified for Kentucky: the page did not load from here. https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/home-energy-improvement/hvac-replacement

## 6. Climate
- Cincinnati's official weather station is at CVG, about 3 miles from Hebron's center, so the "Cincinnati" heat figures were recorded right next door.
- CVG July average high: **85.7°F**, and an average of **22 days per year at 90°F or above**. I calculated both from 1991-2020 CVG daily data (NOAA RCC-ACIS, https://data.rcc-acis.org, station CVG). They are not published normals. Days at 90°F ranged from 1 (1992) to 54 (2012). NWS Wilmington lists the CVG July normal high as 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- Humidity: not verified, leave it out.
