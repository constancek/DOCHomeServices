## 1. Where it is
- Village in Harlan Township, Warren County, Ohio. ZIP 45162. 2020 population 129 (154 in 2010). Area 0.16 sq mi. Laid out in 1852 as New Columbia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleasant_Plain,_Ohio
- Census place id 16000US3963576 (Pleasant Plain village), tract 39165032400, Harlan Township. Confirmed by Census coordinate lookup at 39.2783, -84.1122: https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/coordinates?x=-84.1122&y=39.2783&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&format=json . Street addresses in the village did not geocode; 8400 State Route 132 (Pleasant Plain mailing address) falls in Butlerville village, so the 45162 ZIP covers more than the village.

## 3. What that means (inference from the data above)
- Almost no piped gas: homes heat with electricity or fuel oil. Electric heat points to heat pumps or electric furnaces; fuel-oil furnaces are common. The sample is only 67 units, so margins are wide. Source: Census block above.
- Nearly every home predates 1960, so ductwork, wiring and plumbing were retrofitted into old houses. Trade inference.
