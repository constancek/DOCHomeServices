# Mount Auburn (Cincinnati, OH 45219): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Check in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says Mount Auburn is "one of Cincinnati's oldest hilltop neighborhoods" just north of downtown. Supported: it was annexed in 1849 and the Main Street Incline climbed 312 vertical feet from Main and Mulberry to the hilltop. https://home.nps.gov/wiho/learn/historyculture/historic-mount-auburn-avenue.htm
- "Victorian homes now split into apartments": partly supported by Census (2-4 unit buildings and a 67% rental rate, below). "Original cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines" is unverified as local fact.
- The `adjacent` list (Over-the-Rhine, Corryville, Walnut Hills) is unverified. Check a city neighbourhood map before using it.

## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood on the hill north of downtown and Over-the-Rhine, Hamilton County. First called Keys' Hill, platted in 1837, annexed to the city in 1849. https://home.nps.gov/wiho/learn/historyculture/historic-mount-auburn-avenue.htm ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Auburn_Historic_District (used only to find sources)
- ZIP 45219. That ZIP also covers other uptown areas, so ZIP-level data is not Mount Auburn-specific. Whether parts of the neighbourhood use 45202 or 45206 is unverified. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45219-45219/
- 2020 Census: 5,070 residents across tracts 18, 22 and 23 (1,427, 2,256 and 1,387), according to the city's planning profile. The city server refused a direct fetch, so this figure comes from the search snippet. Treat it as unverified until re-checked. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/MtAuburn_2020.pdf

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tracts 18, 22 and 23, Hamilton County)
These are the tracts the city uses for Mount Auburn. Tract edges may not match the neighbourhood boundary exactly. Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061001800-census-tract-18-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002200-census-tract-22-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002300-census-tract-23-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25003, B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040)
- Median year built: 1938 in tracts 18 and 22. Tract 23 has a median of 2011, because 457 of its 975 units date from 2010-2019.
- Era across all three tracts: 1,771 of 3,128 units (56.6%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 655 units (20.9%) date from 2010 or later.
- Units in structure: 910 single-family detached, 343 single-family attached, 449 two-family, 458 in 3-4 unit buildings, and 510 in buildings of 50 or more units (385 of those in tract 23).
- Tenure: 1,772 of 2,650 occupied units are rented (66.9%) and 878 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 1,430 utility gas (54.0%) and 1,123 electricity (42.4%). Electric heat is much higher in tract 23 (492 of 844) than in tracts 18 and 22 (268 of 697, and 363 of 1,109).
- Styles: Federal, Greek Revival, Italianate, Second Empire and Romanesque Revival houses from the 19th century. The Gorham Worth House is described as the oldest standing frame house in Cincinnati. https://home.nps.gov/wiho/learn/historyculture/historic-mount-auburn-avenue.htm

## Permits
- Work at a Mount Auburn address falls under the City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections. A mechanical permit is needed to install or replace an air conditioner. Unpermitted work costs an investigation fee equal to the permit fee. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections

## Other local factors
- Prospect Hill Historic District, bounded by Liberty, Sycamore, Boal and Highland, is a city local historic district (guidelines adopted 15 April 1981). The Historic Conservation Board reviews exterior changes, and applications need a certificate of appropriateness. Whether condensers or other mechanical equipment trigger review is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation/local-conservation-guidelines/prospect-hill-historic-district/ ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation/historic-conservation-board/january-22-2018-staff-report-and-attachments/
- Mount Auburn Historic District (National Register, 1973) covers both sides of Auburn Avenue from Ringgold Street to Howard Taft Road. National Register listing does not by itself trigger city review. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Auburn_Historic_District (used only to find sources)
- Hillside instability: in 2013 City Council approved $640,000 for a retaining wall on a Mount Auburn hillside near Alma Street that had been slipping for over four years. A rain-triggered slide also put mud on Seitz Street (date unverified). https://building-cincinnati.com/2013/08/640k-will-fix-long-standing-mt-auburn.html ; https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/this-is-mother-nature-for-you-rain-in-forecast-means-more-landslides-loom-over-the-tri-state
