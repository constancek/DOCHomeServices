# Aurora, IN: verified local facts

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Aurora "a historic Ohio River town in Dearborn County, home to the Hillforest Victorian mansion, with a riverfront downtown of 19th-century homes and buildings", and says "those century-old homes carry original plumbing" and "high water and backups [are] a recurring risk". Hillforest is real but is Italian Renaissance style (1855), not "Victorian" in any source we found. Aurora is a **city**, not a town. "Original plumbing" is unsourced; don't use. The flood risk holds (NWS below). 38.8% of homes predate 1940, so "century-old" fits part of the stock, not all of it.

## 1. Where it is
- City in Dearborn County, Indiana, across Center, Lawrenceburg and Washington townships. ZIP 47001. 2020 population 3,479. Elevation 515 ft. Platted 1819. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora,_Indiana
- Hillforest, built 1855 for Thomas Gaff, Italian Renaissance style by Isaiah Rogers, National Historic Landmark 1992. Same source.
- Census geocoder: 235 Main St and 300 2nd St both match Aurora city (place 16000US1802782), tract 18029080500, Center Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=235%20Main%20St%2C%20Aurora%2C%20IN%2047001&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&format=json

- Old houses plus many two- to four-unit buildings (21.9%): retrofitted ducts, ductless units and shared mechanical spaces are likely. Trade inference.

## 4. Permits
- Improvement Location Permits: City of Aurora Plan Commission, staffed by Dearborn County Planning & Zoning, 215B West High St, Suite 300, Lawrenceburg, 812-537-8821. A Building Permit "may also be required". https://aurora.in.us/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ilp-minor.pdf
- Building permits: the city's site plan packet says owners "must obtain... a Building Permit through the Dearborn County Building Department". https://www.dearborncounty.org/egov/documents/1623871424_67883.pdf . The county building department is at 165 Mary St, Lawrenceburg. https://www.dearborncounty.org/department/index.php?structureid=15
- HVAC/mechanical permits: unverified. Say "building permits" only.

## 5. Historic (local district)
- Aurora Downtown Historic District created by city ordinance 1993-18. Owners need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Aurora Historic Preservation Commission before work other than painting or routine maintenance; then Dearborn County issues the building permit. https://aurora.in.us/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/cert-approp-2018.pdf (search snippet). Whether a condenser counts: unverified.
- Also National Register: Downtown Aurora Historic District, 1994, about 100 acres, 272 contributing buildings. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Aurora_Historic_District

## 6. Flood (NWS Ohio River at Cincinnati, CCNO1)
https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1
- Minor 52 ft, moderate 56, major 65. At 60 ft, flooding of "riverfront buildings in Aurora"; at 63 ft Aurora has moderate flooding; at 69 ft Aurora is listed among "areas with no flood protection" with the most severe inundation.
- No levee found for Aurora. Zoning forms ask about Special Flood Hazard Areas. https://www.dearborncounty.org/egov/documents/1623871424_67883.pdf
