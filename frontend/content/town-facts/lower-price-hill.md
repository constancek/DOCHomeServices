# Lower Price Hill (Cincinnati, OH 45204): verified local facts

Note: cincinnati-oh.gov refused connections during this research (Oct 2026), so the city's 2020 neighbourhood census sheet and Buildings & Inspections pages could not be re-opened. Items that depend on them are flagged.

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` lists Sedamsville as adjacent. Unverified, and probably wrong: Sedamsville sits farther west along River Road, and Wikipedia names Sedamsville and Riverside as neighbours of Price Hill as a whole, not Lower Price Hill. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati
- `locations.ts` says "mid-1800s rowhouses" and "Italianate brick rowhouses". The National Register record lists Classical Revival, Italianate and Queen Anne styles, and says many homes date from the 1850s. "Brick" is unverified. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/85407b15-acec-4cb8-bbb7-7b5df940bd82 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Price_Hill_Historic_District
- `locations.ts` says sewer backups "climb when the creek and river run high". No source found tying basement backups to river stage here. Unverified.

## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood, one of the three that make up Price Hill (with East and West Price Hill), in the river valley at the foot of the hill. Hamilton County, ZIP 45204. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati
- Mill Creek is the police boundary with Queensgate. South Fairmount also borders it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Fairmount,_Cincinnati
- Main streets: State Avenue, West Eighth Street, Storrs Street; Oyler Park and the Frank Foster Library are here. https://uacvoice.org/2021/07/lower-price-hill-struggles-transitions-and-victories-in-one-of-cincinnatis-key-appalachian-neighborhoods-by-mike-templeton/
- 2020 Census: 434 housing units, 369 households (Wikipedia, citing the city sheet; city sheet not opened, so treat as unverified). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 263, Hamilton County)
The Census geocoder places 2121 Hatmaker St, 1910 W 8th St and 2131 W 8th St in Tract 263. The tract covers 1.9 sq mi, more than the neighbourhood (it likely takes in industrial land), so treat it as a close proxy. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026300-census-tract-263-hamilton-oh/
- Population 1,245; 498 housing units, 404 occupied.
- Median year built: 1938. 323 of 498 units (64.9%) were built in 1939 or earlier; 49 in the 1940s; 39 in 2000-2009; none since 2010.
- Units in structure: 56 single-family detached, 89 single-family attached, 95 two-family, 164 in 3-4 unit buildings, 47 in 5-9, 47 in 10-49. No mobile homes.
- Tenure: 334 of 404 occupied units rented (82.7%), 70 owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 192 electricity (47.5%), 186 utility gas (46.0%), 26 fuel oil/kerosene (6.4%), no propane.

## Historic district
- Lower Price Hill Historic District, National Register, listed 15 Nov 1988 (NRIS 88002536). Roughly bounded by W. 8th, State, Burns and English Streets; 196 contributing buildings, mostly single and multiple dwellings. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/85407b15-acec-4cb8-bbb7-7b5df940bd82 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Price_Hill_Historic_District
- National Register listing alone does not trigger city design review. Local historic designation is unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Price_Hill_Historic_District

That Duke serves every Lower Price Hill address is unverified (it covers the City of Cincinnati generally). https://news.duke-energy.com/releases/duke-energy-restores-more-than-171-000-outages-across-greater-cincinnati-repairs-continue-as-company-works-to-determine-restoration-times

## Flooding
- The Mill Creek Barrier Dam (Army Corps, 1948, built after the 1937 flood) stops the Ohio backing up the Mill Creek valley; eight pumps move Mill Creek through it during high water. https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2018-02-23/barrier-dam-aging-but-still-protects-mill-creek ; https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/barrier-dam-protects-mill-creek-valley-from-floods
- Whether Lower Price Hill homes sit behind that protection or in a FEMA flood zone: unverified. Check FEMA's map per address. https://msc.fema.gov/portal/search

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)
- July normal high 86F, low 65-66F (1991-2020). https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- Average 21 days a year at 90F or higher (1981-2010). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
- January data not checked. No verified neighbourhood-level heat or humidity figure.
