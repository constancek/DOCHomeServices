## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County, Ohio, incorporated 1912. About 0.85-0.87 sq mi, all land. 2020 population 5,432; 2,745 housing units in the 2020 count. ZIP 45236. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer_Park,_Ohio
- City office: 7777 Blue Ash Rd, Deer Park OH 45236. https://deerpark-oh.gov/media/user/departments/bldg%20permit%20process%20eff%202024.pdf

## 3. Permits (City of Deer Park)
- The **City of Deer Park** issues building/zoning permits. Applications go to the Deer Park City Office before work starts; the city contracts a Certified Building Official for plan review and inspections, residential and commercial. Fee schedule is adopted from the Hamilton County Building Department. https://deerpark-oh.gov/media/user/departments/bldg%20permit%20process%20eff%202024.pdf
- The city's Building/Zoning Permit Application lists **HVAC** and **Repair/Replacement: Furnace / Air Conditioner** as improvement types, so furnace and AC replacement go through this city permit. https://www.deerpark-oh.gov/media/user/departments/Building%20%20Zoning%20Application%20revised%20April%202022%20(6).pdf
- **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI) handles electrical permits and inspections. Same process PDF.
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Plumbing Division (Hamilton County Public Health). Same process PDF.
- Unverified, don't use: fee amounts, review times (the PDF says about 5 business days for plan review, which applies to drawings, not necessarily a like-for-like swap), the official's name in copy, phone numbers.

## 4. Electric utility
- **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers power and bills. The city runs an opt-out electric aggregation program approved by voters on November 6, 2012, plus a gas aggregation program; consultant Energy Alliances. https://deerpark-oh.gov/residents/energy-aggregation and https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/ohio-customer-choice/aggregation
