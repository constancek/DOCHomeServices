# Mason, OH: verified local facts (AC repair pilot)

## What the site already says
- locations.ts and ac-repair-parts/part2.ts both describe Mason as a "fast-growing Warren County city... near Kings Island, with large subdivisions of homes built mostly from the 1990s onward alongside an older town center". The Census data below backs this up. The AC copy also says "condensers and capacitors installed in the 1990s and 2000s" are now failing, which fits the data. The upstairs/bonus-room zoning FAQ is a reasonable trade claim, but no source covers it.

## 1. Where it is
- City in southwestern Warren County. 2020 population 34,792. ZIP 45040, area code 513, about 22 miles north of downtown Cincinnati, elevation 794 ft. Mason withdrew from Deerfield Township in 1997 by forming a paper "Mason Township". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason,_Ohio
- History: William Mason platted the village as "Palmyra" in 1815 and it was renamed Mason in 1835. Main Street was paved in 1923. (same source)
- Roads: I-71, Tylersville Rd (site of the WLW-AM tower, built 1928), Mason-Montgomery Rd (City Municipal Building at 6000 Mason-Montgomery Rd). Sources: Wikipedia above, and the city floodplain notice: https://classifieds.daytondailynews.com/ads/public-notices/legal-notice/the-city-of-mason-floodplain-administratorin-accordance-522819
- Landmarks: Kings Island and Great Wolf Lodge are "immediately south of [Kings Mills] in Mason". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kings_Mills,_Ohio . The Lindner Family Tennis Center is associated with Mason, but whether it sits inside city limits is unverified. Deerfield Towne Center is just outside city limits (local guide only, unverified).
- Terrain: rolling suburban ground. Muddy Creek runs through the city (Quinn Park area) and the city has a short border on the Little Miami River. The city has its own Floodplain Administrator. https://www.imaginemason.org/download/Legislation%20and%20Minutes/2017/legislation/Ord_2017-132.pdf
- Neighbours: Deerfield Township is next door per the 1997 split. West Chester (Butler County) to the west is unverified by a primary source.

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2024 5-year, 2020-2024; Census Reporter, geo 16000US3948188)
https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3948188-mason-oh/
- 13,866 housing units. **Median year built: 1996.**
- Built 1990-1999: 3,992 (28.8%). Built 2000-2009: 3,198 (23.1%). Built 2010 or later: 2,296 (16.6%). Built 1980-89: 1,358 (9.8%). Built before 1980: 3,022 (21.8%), including 232 from 1939 or earlier.
- So 52% of homes were built 1990-2009.
- 69.2% are single-family detached and 17.6% single-family attached (townhomes/condos). 80.1% are owner-occupied. Median owner home value is $426,200.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 60.3%, **electricity 36.6%** (4,937 of 13,502 occupied units).

## 3. What that means for AC (inference from the data above)
- About 7,190 homes date from 1990-2009. Original builder-grade condensers and air handlers from that era have now been in service for 17 to 36 years, so any of them still running are well into replacement age.
- 36.6% of homes heat with electricity. That points to a lot of heat pumps or electric furnaces with AC, so a "no cool" call can be a heat pump that also heats the house. Heat pump share is not measured directly.
- 2,436 attached units (townhomes/condos) usually have tight closet or attic air handlers. This is a trade inference.

## 4. Permits
- The City of Mason Engineering & Building Department "administers most permits for work being performed in the City". Phone 513-229-8520. Applications go through the city's online permit portal. https://www.imaginemason.org/services/engineering-building-planning/ and https://www.imaginemason.org/services/engineering-building-planning/building-documents/ (both returned 403 to our fetch, so the wording comes from search snippets)

## 6. Climate (NOAA 1991-2020 normals, CVG station USW00093814)
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&stations=USW00093814&format=json&dataTypes=MLY-TMAX-NORMAL,MLY-TMIN-NORMAL,MLY-TMAX-AVGNDS-GRTH090
- July normal high is 86.0F and normal low is 65.9F. August is 85.2F / 64.6F.
- On average there are **22.2 days a year at 90F or above**: 7.9 in July, 7.0 in August, 3.7 in June, 3.1 in September.
- July 2020 hit 90F or above 16 times at CVG. NWS ILN: https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climate_summary/ClimateReport_July2020.pdf
- CVG is in Kentucky, about 30 miles from Mason. We have no Mason-specific station data, and humidity figures are unverified.

## 7. Other local notes
- Floodplain: the Muddy Creek floodway was revised in 2019 near Quinn Park (Broadview Ct to Donna Jean Blvd). Outdoor units on parcels in the floodplain may need a floodplain permit. Source: the city notice above. How this applies to condensers is unverified.
- HOAs: probably common in subdivisions built 1990 or later, but we found no source. Unverified.
