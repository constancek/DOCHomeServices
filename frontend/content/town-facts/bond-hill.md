# Bond Hill (Cincinnati, OH 45237): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls the housing "early-1900s and mid-century single-family homes". The era is right (44.5% pre-1940, 28.7% 1940-1959, see below), but only 52.6% of units are single-family detached. Tract 64 has 685 units in two-family and 3-4 unit buildings, so "single-family" undersells the doubles and small multi-unit stock. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006400-census-tract-64-hamilton-oh/
- `locations.ts` has no neighbour list for Bond Hill, so nothing to correct there. The city's 2020 map shows Roselawn, Paddock Hills and Carthage around it. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/BondHill_2020.pdf (exact borders unverified, PDF not readable during this research)
- Its FAQ says "every day, including Saturdays and Sundays", which drifts close to the brief's no-"7-day" rule.

## Where it is
- Bond Hill is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45237. Founded in 1870 as a railroad suburb, incorporated as a village in 1886, annexed by Cincinnati in 1903. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_Hill,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- The city's Statistical Neighborhood Approximation uses Census Tracts 63 and 64. 2020 Census: 7,002 residents (3,865 in Tract 63, 3,137 in Tract 64). https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/BondHill_2020.pdf
- Census geocoder check: 4700, 4900 and 5300 Reading Rd and 1700 Dale Rd fall in Tract 63; 5100 Paddock Rd, 1501 Yarmouth Ave and 1650 California Ave fall in Tract 64. 1800 Seymour Ave falls in Tract 271, which is outside the city's Bond Hill approximation. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress
- ZIP 45237 is much bigger than Bond Hill (20,903 residents, 10,809 units, median year built 1956), so don't use ZIP figures as Bond Hill figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45237-45237/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Tracts 63 + 64 combined)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006300-census-tract-63-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006400-census-tract-64-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25001, B25003, B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040)
- Housing units: 3,525 (1,727 in Tract 63, 1,798 in Tract 64).
- Median year built: 1942 in Tract 63, 1945 in Tract 64.
- By era:
  - 1939 or earlier: 1,569 (44.5%)
  - 1940-1959: 1,011 (28.7%)
  - 1960-1979: 403 (11.4%)
  - 1980-1999: 66 (1.9%)
  - 2000 or later: 476 (13.5%). Tract 63 reports 194 units from 2010-2019; treat that single figure as possibly sampling noise (unverified).
- Units in structure:
  - single-family detached: 1,855 (52.6%)
  - single-family attached: 366
  - two-family: 480
  - 3-4 units: 419
  - 5 or more units: 395 (11.2%)
- Tenure: 1,759 of 3,191 occupied units are owner-occupied (55.1%), 1,432 rented (44.9%). Tract 64 is majority rented (927 of 1,649).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 2,255 (70.7%), electricity 876 (27.5%), propane 17.

## Other local factors
- Bond Hill Bella Vista Historic District: a local (city) historic district, approved by City Council 9-0 on 16 Oct 2019 as Cincinnati's 25th. Bella Vista Street, east of Reading Road, 17 homes, mostly Tudor Revival, built 1926-1936. https://historicbellavista.com/2019/10/16/10-16-19-cincinnati-city-council-votes-unanimously-to-approve-designation-of-the-bella-vista-historic-district/ ; https://local12.com/cincinnati-refined/the-bella-vista-historic-district
