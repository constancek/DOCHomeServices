## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` puts `county: 'Cincinnati Neighborhoods'`. The county is Hamilton County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linwood,_Cincinnati
- "Backups in older homes during high water" and "aging pipe along Eastern Avenue" are unverified inferences. No source names Linwood streets in Ohio River or Little Miami flood coverage.
- The Linwood entry has no `adjacent` list. Borders are unverified: a real-estate listing says Mt. Lookout, Hyde Park and Fairfax, which is not an official source. - "Older homes" is correct: about 82% predate 1940 (see below).

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, on the east side, Hamilton County. It was a village in Spencer Township from 1874, sat on the Little Miami Railroad, and the city annexed it in 1893. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linwood,_Cincinnati
- 2020 Census: 705 residents, 393 housing units, 206 households (51.0% one-person). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linwood,_Cincinnati ; city profile (unreachable, figures from search snippet): https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Linwood_2020.pdf
- City planning builds Linwood from Census Tract 47.03, Block Group 1. The Census geocoder places 4600, 4700 and 4900 Eastern Ave, 5082 Wooster Pike and 262 Wilmer Ave (Lunken Airport terminal) in that block group. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45226 (geocoder matches above).

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Tract 47.03, Block Group 1)
Block Group 1, Tract 47.03. Small sample, wide margins of error (total units 301 +/-106). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610047031-block-group-1-census-tract-4703-hamilton-oh/

- Units in structure: 146 single-family detached, 36 attached, 90 two-family, 24 in 5-9 unit buildings, 5 in 10-19.
- Tenure: 135 of 246 occupied units rented (54.9%), 111 owner-occupied.

Tract 47.03 as a whole (also covers Columbia-Tusculum, so a looser proxy): median year built 1942; 25.4% heat with electricity. Already verified in `columbia-tusculum.md`. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061004703-census-tract-4703-hamilton-oh/

## Flooding and terrain
- Lunken Airport sits on Little Miami valley flats, ringed by a levee; the 1937 flood overtopped it. FEMA told the city in 2007 the levee needed raising 1.5 to 3 feet. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunken_Airport ; https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2014-02-24/lunken-flood-levee-was-not-a-quiet-decision
- Ohio River floods closed Kellogg Ave between Wilmer and Delta in 2025. Kellogg is south of Linwood's homes; whether any Linwood homes flooded is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/cityofcincinnati/news/city-extends-road-closures-activates-shelter-in-response-to-flooding/ ; https://www.fox19.com/2025/02/18/list-several-roads-closed-around-tri-state-due-high-water
