# Independence, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'independence'):**
- "As Kenton County's seat" / "the Kenton County seat": Independence is one of **two** county seats; it shares the status with Covington. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence,_Kentucky
- "historic core surrounded by newer subdivisions" holds up: median year built 1999, 49.0% built 2000 or later (section 2), and a National Register district along Madison and McCullum Pikes (section 5).
- "one of the faster-growing cities": population rose 15.8% from 2010 (24,757) to 2020 (28,676). Same source.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in Kenton County, ZIP 41051. 2020 population 28,676; 17.80 sq mi. Borders Elsmere (northwest), Erlanger and Covington (north), Ryland Heights (northeast) and Boone County (west). Banklick Creek is the largest stream. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence,_Kentucky

## 3. Permits
- **The City of Independence is its own local building and HVAC inspector** for residential, storage or utility buildings up to 3 stories or 20,000 sq ft (and small commercial). All other Independence work goes to Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS). https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- The city's monthly permit reports list "HVAC-replacement" and "HVAC-new SFR" permits (fees shown there; don't quote). https://www.cityofindependence.org/DocumentCenter/View/1211/Permit-Report-06-2026---June-PDF
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector (Boone County Health Center, Florence). Electrical: local office **unverified**. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/

## 5. Historic districts
- Independence Historic District, portions of Madison and McCullum Pikes, National Register listed October 25, 2024 (from Wikipedia's list; NPS record not found, so **unverified**). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Kenton_County,_Kentucky
- A National Register listing alone doesn't restrict private owners. Local historic overlay: none found, **unverified**.
- Individually listed: Metcalfe-Stephens House (5241 Madison Pike) and Yeager-Mohr Farmstead (5002 Madison Pike). Same source.

## 6. Terrain / flood
