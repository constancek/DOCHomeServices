## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County. It is essentially one apartment complex, mostly Section 8 housing. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villages_at_Roll_Hill,_Cincinnati
- Property address: 3710 President Drive, Cincinnati, OH 45225. The listing describes it as 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom garden apartments and townhomes. https://apartments.cincinnati-oh.gov/listings/3710-president-drive-cincinnati-ohio-45225
- The Census geocoder places 3710 President Dr in ZIP 45225, Census Tract 85.02, Block Group 1 (GEOID 390610085021). The tract has only this one block group. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=3710+President+Dr%2C+Cincinnati%2C+OH+45225&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=ACS2024_Current&format=json
- 2020 Census: 1,918 residents, 744 housing units, and 66.0% of residents under 18. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villages_at_Roll_Hill,_Cincinnati (city profile: https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/RollHill_2020.pdf, connection refused, not re-checked)

## History
- It was built in the 1960s and named Fay Apartments. Wikipedia calls it a public housing project, while a search snippet says it opened in 1962 as market-rate housing with over 1,025 units. The exact year and the original unit count are unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villages_at_Roll_Hill,_Cincinnati
- The renovation cost $36 million and ran from 2010 to 2012. It demolished 17 buildings and cut the unit count from 893 to 703. Funding was a $32 million HUD loan plus a $3.2 million City HOME loan. It was billed as the largest green renovation of affordable housing in the country and was renamed in December 2012. Specific energy or HVAC upgrades are unverified (the sites didn't load). https://www.urbancincy.com/2010/10/fay-apartments-to-be-renamed-the-villages-of-roll-hill-undergo-36m-renovation/ ; https://www.urbancincy.com/2012/12/officials-celebrate-completion-of-nations-largest-green-renovation-of-affordable-housing/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 85.02)
The tract counts 924 units against 744 for the neighbourhood in 2020, and it includes pre-1940 and detached homes, so it covers housing outside the complex. Margins of error are wide. Source for every figure: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061008502-census-tract-8502-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1964 (+/-4). The largest group is 270 units (29.2%) built 1960-1969. Another 180 units (19.5%) date from 2000-2019, which may reflect the rebuild. 77 units (8.3%) predate 1940.
- Units in structure: 131 single-family detached, 320 attached (townhouse rows), 192 in 3-4 unit buildings, 153 in 5-9, 108 in 10-19, 20 in 50+.
- Tenure: 773 of 895 occupied units are rented (86.4%) and 122 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 467 utility gas (52.2%), 337 electricity (37.7%), 91 propane (10.2%).
- Inference: with 86% renting, management usually authorises equipment work.
