# Lawrenceburg, IN: verified local facts

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Lawrenceburg "a historic Ohio River city... known for its distilling heritage and a downtown of 19th-century buildings protected by a riverfront levee", and says "century-old buildings carry aging plumbing" and "historic riverfront homes". The levee and the National Register downtown are real (below). But the housing stock is not mostly old: median year built is **1983**, only 15.6% predates 1940, 34.8% is 2000 or later, and 36.7% of units are in 5+ unit buildings. "Historic riverfront homes" overstates it.

## 1. Where it is
- City and county seat of Dearborn County, Indiana, in Lawrenceburg Township. ZIP 47025. 2020 population 5,129. Known as "Whiskey City"; MGP Indiana (formerly Seagram's) distills there. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrenceburg,_Indiana
- Census geocoder: 230 Walnut St and 300 W High St both match Lawrenceburg city (place 16000US1842462), tract 18029080302. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=230%20Walnut%20St%2C%20Lawrenceburg%2C%20IN%2047025&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&format=json . Some 47025 addresses (500 Ludlow St, 510 Ridge Ave) are in Greendale, so ZIP alone doesn't mean the city.

## 4. Permits
- City of Lawrenceburg Zoning Department, City Administration Building, 230 Walnut St, 812-532-3561: call for a pre-application meeting to learn "what permits are required"; city runs a Zoning Permit Portal. https://thinklawrenceburg.com/zoning-department/
- Building permits: **unverified**. A permit aggregator says the Dearborn County Building Commissioner (165 Mary St, Lawrenceburg) handles them (https://indianapermits.com/indiana/dearborn-county/city-of-lawrenceburg/), but the city's contract list includes a 2013 interlocal with the City of Greendale "for the provision of building inspector services" (https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/lawrenceburgin/latest/lawrenceburg_in/0-0-0-8494, 403; search snippet). HVAC/mechanical permit office: unverified. Don't name one.

## 5. Utility
- Electric: Lawrenceburg Municipal Utilities (city-owned electric, water and wastewater; electric department since 1901), wholesale power from IMPA. https://www.impa.com/MediaLibraries/IMPA2017/MPN-Newsletters/Vol%2028,%20Iss%202/Lawrenceburg_Vol28_Iss2.pdf ; withdrawn from IURC jurisdiction (2009): https://www.in.gov/iurc/files/Electric_Energy-Division-Municipal-Utilities-Withdrawn.pdf
- Not Duke Energy. Rebates: unverified.

## 6. Flood protection
- Ohio River levees protect the city; they failed in 1884, 1913 and 1937. https://usgenwebsites.org/INDearborn/Pages/flood.shtml
- The Lawrenceburg Conservancy District operates the levee floodgates (floodgate closure notice on the city zoning page). https://thinklawrenceburg.com/zoning-department/
- NWS Ohio River at Cincinnati (CCNO1): minor 52 ft, moderate 56, major 65; at 71-72 ft "major flooding" is listed for Lawrenceburg and Aurora. https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1
- City has a Floodplain Management Administrator and code Chapter 152, Flood Hazard Areas. https://thinklawrenceburg.com/zoning-department/

## 7. Historic
- Downtown Lawrenceburg Historic District, National Register 1984 (#84001009), 65 acres, 257 contributing buildings. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Lawrenceburg_Historic_District
- The city also has a local Historic Preservation Commission and code Chapter 157, Historic Preservation, so local review exists. Which properties it covers and whether a condenser needs review: unverified. https://thinklawrenceburg.com/zoning-department/

## 8. Climate (from _shared.md)
- July normal high 86F, low 65-66F (1991-2020, CVG station in Kentucky; no local station). https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- About 21-22 days a year at 90F or higher. https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
