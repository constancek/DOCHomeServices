# Pendleton (Cincinnati, OH 45202): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says Pendleton's buildings date from the "mid-1800s". The Census only says about 80% of units were built in 1939 or earlier (see below). Soapbox describes the area as "Italianate 19th Century town homes, carriage houses and dense, historic tenement buildings", but no source here gives "mid-1800s" for the stock as a whole. https://soapboxmedia.com/?p=12850
- "Brittle cast-iron stacks" and "old risers" that reach "several units" are unverified as Pendleton-specific facts. They are general inferences about old multi-unit buildings, not local data.
- The FAQ says Pendleton is "one of the fastest areas for us to reach". That is a company claim. Use it only if `stats.md` supports it.
- The entry has no `adjacent` list. Neighbours are Over-the-Rhine (west), the Central Business District (south) and Mount Auburn (north). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendleton,_Cincinnati (to locate the source; its boundary line is marked "citation needed")

## Where it is
- Pendleton is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods. It is a small triangle bounded by Liberty Street, Reading Road/Central Parkway and Sycamore Street, east of Over-the-Rhine and north of downtown, in Hamilton County. It has been a separate neighbourhood from OTR since 1987. https://soapboxmedia.com/?p=12850 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendleton,_Cincinnati
- ZIP 45202. The Census geocoder matched 1310 Pendleton St, 1200 and 1325 Broadway St and 500 E 13th St to ZIP 45202 and Census Tract 11. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- 2020 Census: 1,088 residents and 668 housing units. These are the city figures as quoted by Wikipedia, because the city site refused connections. Re-check them. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Pendleton_2022.pdf
- Terrain: flat ground in the city's basin. Not checked against a map source: unverified.

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Hamilton County Census Tract 11)
Tract 11 matches Pendleton closely (694 units vs the city's 668). Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061001100-census-tract-11-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25001, B25003, B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040)
- Median year built: 1938. That is the ACS floor value, so read it as "1939 or earlier".
- 558 of 694 units (80.4%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 95 units (13.7%) date from 2010 or later, 59 of them from 2020 or later. Almost nothing was built between 1940 and 2009.
- Units in structure: 52 single-family detached, 88 attached (rowhouse), 67 two-family, 174 in 3-4 unit buildings, 121 in 5-9, 73 in 10-19, and 99 in 50+. So 534 of 694 units (76.9%) sit in buildings with two or more units. The 20 "mobile home" units carry a ±29 margin and are sampling noise.
- Tenure: 364 of 596 occupied units are rented (61.1%), and 232 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 300 (50.3%), electricity 263 (44.1%), bottled gas 13. The 20 "solar" households carry a ±29 margin, so they are not reliable.
- Margins are wide at this size. For example, electricity heat is 263 ±66.

## Historic district
- The Over-the-Rhine National Register Historic District (ref. 83001985, published 17 May 1983) is "roughly bounded by Dorsey, Sycamore, Liberty, Reading, Central Pkwy, McMicken Ave., and Vine Sts." Wikipedia says the city counts Pendleton inside it. Check coverage address by address: unverified. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/362674c3-1e0a-4626-9f94-f03e6df460b5
- The St. Paul Church Historic District (Spring, 12th and Pendleton Sts.) was listed on 18 January 1974 (ref. 74001513). https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/national-register-of-historic-places/nr-districts/
- Local historic designation, which would require a Certificate of Appropriateness for exterior changes, is unverified for Pendleton. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-historic-landmarks
