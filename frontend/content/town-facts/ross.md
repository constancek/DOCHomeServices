# Ross, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## 1. Where it is
- Census-designated place (not a city or village) in **Ross Township**, Butler County, OH. 2020 population 3,478. ZIP 45061. Laid out in 1817 as Venus (later "Venice"), renamed Ross in 1834. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross,_Ohio
- US 27 forms the CDP's eastern edge; SR 126 and SR 128 run through the center as Hamilton Cleves Rd. **The Great Miami River forms the southeastern edge.** (same source)
- Township office: 4055 Hamilton Cleves Rd. The township is mostly rural but growing (8,736 residents in 2020). https://rosstwp.org/zoning-and-planning-information ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Township,_Butler_County,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 3980 Hamilton Cleves Rd and 4250 Layhigh Rd fall in Ross CDP (16000US3968602), tract 108. 3371 Hamilton Cleves Rd (Ross High School) is in Ross Township but outside the CDP. Census Geocoder via census.mjs.

## 3. Permits
- Ross Township has no building department of its own. Zoning is enforced under the Butler County Rural Zoning Code, and the **Butler County Building & Zoning Department** "handles all aspects zoning for Ross Township". https://rosstwp.org/zoning-and-planning-information
- The county department (130 High St, Hamilton) issues building permits in the township and lists HVAC permits under a BLD-HC code in its monthly permits-issued reports, organized by township. https://www.bcohio.gov/JULY%202023.pdf (403 to direct fetch; from search snippet). County residential procedures: https://cms5.revize.com/revize/butleroh/Document_Center/Development/Building%20&%20Zoning/Procedures%20&%20Applications/RESIDENTIAL%20PROCEDURES%202024.pdf

## 7. Errors in locations.ts

- "Rural properties may rely on wells and septic" applies to the outer township; the CDP core was sewered under the 2014 project (EPA link above). Wells: unverified.
