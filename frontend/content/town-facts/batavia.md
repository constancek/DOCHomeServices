# Batavia, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'batavia'): ZIP 45103, Clermont County seat on the East Fork of the Little Miami River, historic village core plus "surrounding newer development", older homes with aging plumbing, low ground near the river. Mostly right; see the end.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** (1842), surrounded by Batavia Township; **Clermont County seat** since 1824 (moved from New Richmond). 2020 population 1,972. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavia,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 101 E Main St, 200 E Main St and 235 S Market St, Batavia 45103 = Batavia village (16000US3904150), tract 411.01. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45103 is far bigger than the village (Batavia Township, parts of Amelia and rural Clermont). Use village figures for "Batavia".

## 3. Permits
- **Clermont County Building Inspection** (Permit Central, 2275 Bauer Road, Batavia) issues permits in the village; its reports have a "Batavia Village" section. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/building-activity-reports
- **HVAC confirmed:** 11 "Residential / HVAC Replacement" permits in Batavia Village in 2025, and one at 320 Riverside Dr in April 2026. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2025/JANUARY%20DECEMBER%202025%20PERMITS%20DETAILED.pdf ; https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2026/PERMITS%20DETAILED%20APRIL%202026.pdf
- Fee schedule covers mechanical (furnace and condensing unit replacement, with electrical inspection) and electrical permits; 2019 fees, don't quote. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FeeSchedule/permit-central-fee-schedule-03-28-19.pdf
- Village zoning is separate (513.732.2020 per county zoning page). https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/zoning

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **East Fork flooding: partly verified.** No NWS gauge at Batavia. The nearest downstream gauge, PERO1 (East Fork at Perintown): action 17 ft, minor 19, moderate 22, major 24; record 23.84 ft (March 10, 1964). Its impact notes name roads, not Batavia streets. Harsha Dam (BHDO1) is upstream and controls the flow. https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/PERO1 (data via https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/PERO1)
- The 1964 flood near Batavia exceeded 1913 on the East Fork (Midwest Regional Climate Center). https://mrcc.purdue.edu/1913Flood/communities/lovelandMilford
- Which village streets flood, and parcel flood zones: unverified; https://msc.fema.gov
- **Historic district: none found.** The National Register list has no Batavia village district (the courthouse is a separate article, listing status unverified). Local design review: unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Clermont_County,_Ohio
