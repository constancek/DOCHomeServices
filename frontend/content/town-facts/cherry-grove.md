# Cherry Grove (Anderson Township, OH 45255): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives the ZIP as 45230. Cherry Grove's ZIP is 45255. Four Beechmont Ave addresses in the 8000-8600 blocks all geocode to 45255. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Grove,_Ohio ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mid-century and newer single-family homes" is loose. The median build year is 1971 and 78.1% of units date from 1960-79. The Census counts 0.0% from 2000 or later, so "newer" overstates it. See the housing section.
- "Anderson Township community" is correct. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Grove,_Ohio
- The claims about worn supply lines and burst pipes being the most common emergencies are unverified.

## Where it is
- Cherry Grove is a census-designated place in Anderson Township, Hamilton County. It has no government of its own. Area 1.13 sq mi, all land, elevation 886 ft, ZIP 45255. 2020 Census population 4,419. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Grove,_Ohio
- 8400, 8500 and 8600 Beechmont Ave geocode to Cherry Grove CDP, tract 251.02, Anderson Township. 8000 Beechmont Ave and 1000 Nordyke Rd fall in Forestville CDP, so Forestville borders it to the west. Other neighbours were not checked (unverified). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45255 crosses the county line. 500 Ohio Pike, 45255, geocodes to Union Township in Clermont County, outside the CDP, so a 45255 address isn't necessarily in Cherry Grove or Hamilton County. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits (from _shared.md, plus township check)
- Building permits come from Hamilton County Planning + Development, Division of Buildings + Inspections, which covers the unincorporated townships. Electrical permits and inspections go through Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). The county says HVAC installations are among its most common inspections. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/planning_and_development/buildings_and_inspections/index.php ; https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/planning_and_development/buildings_and_inspections/inspections.php
- The county's residential permit form has a mechanical section that asks for furnace input/output and cooling capacity. Whether a like-for-like furnace or AC swap in Anderson Township needs its own mechanical permit from the county is unverified. Don't state it. https://cagis.hamilton-co.org/opal2010/forms/residential_bldg1.pdf
- Plumbing permits: Hamilton County Public Health (from _shared.md). https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- Anderson Township issues zoning certificates for exterior improvements such as additions, sheds and decks. HVAC equipment is not on its list, so whether a condenser needs one is unverified. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/Portals/andersontownship/Documents/Forms/Residential%20Zoning%20Certificate%20Final.pdf
- Fees and timings: unverified, don't use.

## Utilities
- Electric: Duke Energy Ohio delivers power and bills customers. Under Anderson Township's opt-out aggregation, renewed in March 2026, owners are enrolled automatically unless they opt out. New residents are not enrolled automatically. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/news-events/news/2026/03/10/electricity-aggregation-program-renewed-again ; https://andersontownshipoh.gov/community/electric-aggregation-program
