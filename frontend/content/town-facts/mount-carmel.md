# Mount Carmel, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'mount-carmel'): ZIP 45244, Clermont County, Union Township community "near the I-275 corridor" with "mid-century and newer homes". Mid-century is confirmed (median 1973). The I-275 placement is unverified.

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated census-designated place (CDP) in Union Township, Clermont County. 2020 census population 4,828. Summerside borders it to the north. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Carmel,_Ohio (cites Census) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summerside,_Ohio
- Union Township has no incorporated municipalities; 2020 population 49,639. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Township,_Clermont_County,_Ohio
- Geocoded inside the CDP: 4300 and 4271 Mount Carmel Tobasco Rd, both ZIP 45244, tract 414.03 (Census geocoder via census.mjs).

## 3. Permits
- **Clermont County Building Inspection (Permit Central)**, 2275 Bauer Rd, Batavia, regulates the residential code in all unincorporated areas; Union Township is unincorporated. Its checklist covers building, mechanical and electrical review, and asks for a township zoning permit or approval letter at application. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/helpful-links ; https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FormsHandouts/1%20Permit%20Checklist.pdf ; https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FormsHandouts/General/5-work-exempt-from-approval.pdf
