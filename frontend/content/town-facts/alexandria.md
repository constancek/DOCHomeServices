# Alexandria, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'alexandria'):**
- "The Campbell County seat" is incomplete. Alexandria is one of two county seats, with Newport (dual seats since 2010). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Kentucky
- "Aging plumbing" in a "historic core": the city stock is young. Median year built is 1994; 35.1% of units were built 2000 or later and only 11.1% predate 1960 (section 2). The historic downtown exists, but it is a small share. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2100802/
- "Well or septic issue": unverified. No source checked on wells, septic or sewer coverage.
- ZIP 41001 reaches well beyond the city. 909 Camel Crossing, "Alexandria 41001", geocodes to Claryville CDP, not Alexandria city. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Every day, weekends included" are company claims; check against stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city near the center of Campbell County, about 14 miles south of Cincinnati. 2020 population 10,341; 6.96 sq mi. ZIP 41001. Rolling hills; elevation 820 ft; not on the Ohio River. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Kentucky

## 3. Permits
- The state inspector list names Campbell County (Planning, Zoning and Building Inspection, 1098 Monmouth St, Suite 343, Newport) as the local building and local HVAC office for Alexandria. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- The city itself takes **electrical permit** applications online; site plans, grading and variances go to Campbell County Planning & Zoning. https://alexandriaky.gov/forms/
- Plumbing inspections: state plumbing inspector. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: don't use.

## 5. Utilities
- Electric: **Duke Energy Kentucky** lists Alexandria among communities served (tariff effective Dec 2, 2019). https://psc.ky.gov/tariffs/Electric/Duke%20Energy%20Kentucky/Cancelled%20Tariff%20Pages/2020/Index/01-01.pdf
- **Owen Electric Cooperative** also has certified territory in and around Alexandria: it applied to bid on the city's electric franchise in 2021, and a 2023 case concerned a new home on Madonna Drive, Alexandria, inside Owen's territory (moved to Duke by agreement). So an Alexandria address can be on either utility; check per address. https://psc.ky.gov/pscecf/2021-00133/psc@owenelectric.com/03252021022255/Application_Alexandria_Franchise.pdf ; https://psc.ky.gov/pscecf/2023-00020/psc@owenelectric.com/02142023033156/2023-00020_Amended_Application.pdf
