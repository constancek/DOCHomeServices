## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, in the Mill Creek valley off I-75. Residential addresses (Winton Rd, E. Epworth Ave, Derby Ave, McMakin Ave) are ZIP 45232. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Grove_Village,_Cincinnati ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Called Winton Place until early 2007, when residents voted to rename it. Incorporated in 1882 as a canal and railroad town, annexed by Cincinnati in November 1903. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Grove_Village,_Cincinnati (to locate the source; confirm with the city before using dates)
- 2020 Census: 1,916 residents, 984 housing units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Grove_Village,_Cincinnati (the city's 2020 PDF would not load)

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 73, Hamilton County)
The geocoder puts every address tested (4300, 4600, 4700, 4800 Winton Rd; 700 E. Epworth Ave; 768 Derby Ave; 759 McMakin Ave; the cemetery) in tract 73, block groups 1 and 2. Tract 73 has 1,996 residents and 970 units, close to the neighbourhood's 2020 count, so it is a good proxy. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061007300-census-tract-73-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1938. https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25035&geo_ids=14000US39061007300
- Year built, 970 units: 519 (53.5%) built 1939 or earlier, 327 (33.7%) built 1940-1979, 124 (12.8%) built 1980 or later. None since 2010. https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25034&geo_ids=14000US39061007300
- Units in structure: 641 single-family detached (66.1%), 23 attached, 78 two-family, 33 in 3-4 unit buildings, 175 in 5-49 unit buildings, 20 mobile homes. https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25024&geo_ids=14000US39061007300
- Tenure: 528 of 881 occupied units owner-occupied (59.9%), 353 rented (40.1%). https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25003&geo_ids=14000US39061007300
- Heating fuel: 567 utility gas (64.4%), 238 electricity (27.0%), 9 fuel oil, 61 no fuel. https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B25040&geo_ids=14000US39061007300
