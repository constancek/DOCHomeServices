# St. Bernard, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'st-bernard'):**
- "is its own city": the municipality now calls itself the **Village of St. Bernard** on its own permit pages, and the Census lists it as "St. Bernard village". It was a city from 1912, so use "village" (or just "St. Bernard"). https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/236/Zoning-Permits ; https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/193/About-Us
- "completely surrounded by Cincinnati": almost. Elmwood Place, also a Cincinnati enclave, touches St. Bernard along a small stretch. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmwood_Place,_Ohio ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Bernard,_Ohio
- "dense grid of early-1900s homes" / "century-old housing" overstates the age: 32.6% of homes predate 1940 and the median home was built in 1955 (section 2).
- "corroded galvanized lines and aging cast-iron drains" and "small valley lots": unverified, no source.
- Built up around P&G Ivorydale: confirmed (village history page). https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/193/About-Us

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated municipality in Hamilton County, an enclave inside Cincinnati; laid out 1851, incorporated as a village 1878; about 1.54 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Bernard,_Ohio
- ZIP 45217 (Census geocoder match for 110 Washington Ave, the village offices). https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/236/Zoning-Permits
- Shares the St. Bernard-Elmwood Place school district with Elmwood Place. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Bernard,_Ohio

## 3. Permits
- **From January 1, 2026, building permits and inspections for the Village of St. Bernard are issued by the Hamilton County Building Department** (applications through the county's CAGIS ezTRak portal). Before that the village issued its own. https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/236/Zoning-Permits
- The village still issues **zoning certificates** (new buildings, additions, decks, fences, accessory structures); the county won't issue a building permit for that work without one. The page lists "Furnace or Air Conditioning (Residential)" and "Plumbing and Electrical" in the county group that needs no village zoning certificate (table is flattened on the page, so the column reading is inferred). Same source.
- Permits issued before 2026: village records. Same source.

## 4. Electric utility
- **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers power and bills (aggregation changes only the generation supplier). https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/ohio-customer-choice/aggregation

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Historic:** one National Register listing found, the Nurre-Royston House (4330 Errun Lane, listed 2009). No local historic district or review board found; unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurre-Royston_House
- **Flood:** St. Bernard is among the Mill Creek communities in FEMA's 2004 flood-elevation rule for Hamilton County, and the village history describes low, swampy Mill Creek floodplain. Which addresses sit in a current flood zone: unverified; check https://msc.fema.gov . https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2004-01-06/pdf/04-157.pdf ; https://www.stbernard-oh.gov/193/About-Us
- **Mix of housing:** 17.6% of units are in two-unit buildings and 21.1% in 5+ unit buildings, so many jobs are in doubles and apartments, not detached houses (section 2).
