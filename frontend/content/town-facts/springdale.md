# Springdale, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'springdale'):**
- "Mid-century neighborhoods" / "mid-century homes" is loose. The median home was built in 1971 and 50.7% were built 1960-79; only 12.4% date from 1940-59 (section 2). "1960s-70s homes" is accurate. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3974104/
- "Supply lines corrode and water heaters fail" at this age: unverified, no source.
- The entry treats Springdale as a homeowner town. 40.9% of occupied units are rented and 23.3% of units are in buildings of 5 or more (505 in 50+ unit buildings). Same source.
- ZIP 45246 is much bigger than the city (11,306 housing units vs 4,891). Use city figures, not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45246/
- "We respond every day, Sundays too" and "Help usually arrives the same day" are company claims; check against stats.md.

## 3. Permits (City of Springdale)
Note: springdaleohio.gov returns 403 to automated requests; the bullets below come from search-result text of the city pages, not a full page read.
