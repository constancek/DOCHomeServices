# East Walnut Hills (Cincinnati, OH 45206): verified local facts

Note: cincinnati-oh.gov would not load (Oct 2026). City facts come from search text of city pages.

## Check in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` lists Mount Adams as adjacent. Unverified, and probably wrong: Walnut Hills and Eden Park sit between them. Evanston (O'Bryonville) is named as a neighbour on Wikipedia. Hyde Park and East End borders are also unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati
- The copy calls the houses "early-1900s". That only partly fits. Median year built is 1938, and about half the housing units are mid-century or later apartment and condo buildings (see Housing).
- ZIP: core addresses (Madison Rd at Woodburn Ave) are 45206. Whether the eastern edge near O'Bryonville uses 45208 is unverified.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, on the east side. It was founded in 1867 as the Village of Woodburn and annexed by 1873. Hamilton County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati
- 2020 Census: 4,103 residents (3,794 in 2010) and 2,715 housing units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati (citing https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/EastWalnutHills_2020.pdf, which could not be opened)
- For Census data the city uses tract 42, tract 20 and block group 2 of tract 41. This is from search text of the city profile and is unverified. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/EastWalnutHills_2020.pdf
- Main corridors: Madison Road and Woodburn Avenue, meeting at DeSales Corner (St. Francis de Sales Church). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Hamilton County tracts 20 and 42)
A proxy; it leaves out the tract 41 part. Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061002000-census-tract-20-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061004200-census-tract-42-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040, B25003)
- Median year built: 1938 (tract 20) and 1952 (tract 42).
- Built 1939 or earlier: 1,165 of 2,364 units (49.3%). Built 2000 or later: 189 units (8.0%).
- Units in structure: 375 single-family detached (15.9%), 269 single-family attached, 90 two-family, 303 in 3-4 unit buildings, 443 in 20-49 unit buildings and 522 in buildings of 50 or more units (22.1%).
- Tenure: 928 of 2,180 occupied units are rented (42.6%).
- Heating fuel: 1,263 utility gas (57.9%) and 809 electricity (37.1%). Electric heat varies a lot by tract: 555 of 992 homes (55.9%) in tract 20, against 254 of 1,188 (21.4%) in tract 42.

## Historic districts
- East Walnut Hills Historic District is a local district with city conservation guidelines. A city presentation lists it as designated in 1988, covering more than 160 homes built 1890-1925 (search text, unverified). https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-conservation-guidelines/east-walnut-hills-historic-district/
- Woodburn Avenue NBD Historic District is also a local district around DeSales Corner. Recommended by the Planning Commission in 2005; adoption date unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-conservation-guidelines/woodburn-avenue-nbd-historic-district/
- In local districts, exterior changes need a Certificate of Appropriateness, even where no building permit is needed. Interior work is exempt unless it changes the exterior. Whether outdoor mechanical units count as minor (staff-reviewed) or major (Board-reviewed) is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/historic-conservation/certificate-of-appropriateness/
- Madison and Woodburn Historic District went on the National Register on 30 June 1983 (#83001983) with 19 contributing buildings. National Register listing alone does not trigger city review. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_and_Woodburn_Historic_District ; https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/83001983

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG, 1991-2020 normals)
- July: normal high 86F, normal low 65-66F. https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf
