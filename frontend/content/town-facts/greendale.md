# Greendale, IN: verified local facts

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Greendale "a Dearborn County city adjoining Lawrenceburg, climbing the hillside above the Ohio River, with a mix of older and mid-century homes". The Census data backs "older and mid-century" (median year built 1958; 54.8% built before 1960). Hillside: elevation 650 ft vs 515 ft at Aurora's riverfront suggests higher ground, but no source describes the terrain; unverified. The claim that the grade "adds demand on basement drains in heavy rain" is unsourced; don't use.

## 1. Where it is
- City in Lawrenceburg Township, Dearborn County, Indiana. ZIP 47025 (shared with Lawrenceburg). 2020 population 4,602. Elevation 650 ft. Laid out 1852. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greendale,_Indiana
- Census geocoder: 500 Ridge Ave and 1001 Ridge Ave match Greendale city (place 16000US1829448), tracts 18029080302 and 18029080400. 500 Main St, Greendale matched Lawrenceburg city instead, so the 47025 line is irregular. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=500%20Ridge%20Ave%2C%20Greendale%2C%20IN%2047025&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&format=json

## 4. Permits
- Improvement Location Permit: City of Greendale (Code §155.095), 812-537-2125. The form asks for mechanical and electrical contractors and says the certificate of occupancy "will be issued by the Building Inspector of Dearborn County, Indiana". https://cityofgreendale.net/_files/ugd/d98a34_822e0c50110a48d7ad17ad68d6b9dc24.pdf
- So building permits most likely go through the Dearborn County Building Department (165 Mary St, Lawrenceburg; Building Commissioner Bill Shelton). https://www.dearborncounty.org/department/index.php?structureid=15 . Not stated outright: **unverified**. A 2013 Lawrenceburg contract list mentions an interlocal with Greendale for building inspector services, which muddies it. HVAC/mechanical permit office: unverified.

## 5. Utility
- Electric: City of Greendale Electric Department (municipal; four substations), wholesale from IMPA, of which Greendale is a founding member. Water also city-run; sewage goes to the South Dearborn Regional Sewer District. Utility office 812-537-2125. https://www.cityofgreendale.net/services/utility-department
- Not Duke Energy. Gas provider: unverified.

## 6. Flood
- Greendale's Improvement Location Permit has boxes for Base Flood Elevation, FIRM map and "DNR-Flood Plain", so some parcels are in a mapped flood area. Same PDF as above.
- A 2024 Green Umbrella event, "Living with a Levee: Lawrenceburg and Greendale Levee Systems", says Greendale has levee protection; details unverified. https://greenumbrella.org/event/living-with-a-levee-lawrenceburg-and-greendale-levee-systems/

## 7. Historic
- Greendale Historic District, National Register, December 3, 2019 (#100004720), roughly Gages Lane, Ridge Ave, Tanners Creek, Probasco St and Nowlin Ave. National Register only; a local district or review board: none found, unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Dearborn_County,_Indiana
