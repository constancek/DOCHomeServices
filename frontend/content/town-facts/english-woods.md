# English Woods (Cincinnati, OH 45225): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` and the service parts files describe "older homes", "older hilltop homes", "aging galvanized supply lines" and "a quiet, largely residential area". That is wrong. CMHA owns the neighbourhood, and after the 2005 demolition most of it is vacant land. What housing remains is two CMHA rental properties (see below). There are almost no privately owned houses: ACS counts 0 owner-occupied units in the block group. https://www.wcpo.com/longform/our-forgotten-neighborhoods-english-woods ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610086013/
- "Has seen redevelopment in recent years" is only partly true. CMHA started renovating Marquette Manor in January 2025. No developer, financing or construction start was found for the vacant land. https://cintimha.com/marquette-manor-receiving-a-makeover-with-a-re-imagining-affordable-housing-renovation
- The "mix of older and newer housing" and "redeveloped homes" claims in the parts files are unverified.
- The `county` field reads "Cincinnati Neighborhoods". The county is Hamilton County.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45225. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Woods,_Cincinnati
- It sits on a hilltop just west of the Mill Creek, beside Westwood Northern Blvd. Its neighbours are North Fairmount, South Fairmount, South Cumminsville, East Westwood and Millvale. https://www.wcpo.com/longform/our-forgotten-neighborhoods-english-woods
- CMHA opened the public housing in 1942. About 700 two-story brick townhomes were demolished in 2005. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Woods,_Cincinnati ; https://www.wcpo.com/longform/our-forgotten-neighborhoods-english-woods
- 2020 Census: 361 residents and 242 housing units (down from 5,334 residents in 1990). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Woods,_Cincinnati

## Housing that exists now
- **Marquette Manor**, 1999 Sutter Ave: a 15-story CMHA tower with about 140 one-bedroom apartments. A RAD gut renovation of every unit started on 16 Jan 2025, with a 16-month construction period. https://cintimha.com/marquette-manor-receiving-a-makeover-with-a-re-imagining-affordable-housing-renovation ; https://cintimha.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/february-renew-restore-revitalize-news-1.pdf
- **Sutter View**, 1990 Sutter Ave / 2015 Knob Ct: CMHA family townhomes and apartments, 119 units in 23 two-story buildings (2016 OHFA proposal), which the city listing says "underwent substantial rehabilitation". The rehab year is unverified. https://apartments.cincinnati-oh.gov/listings/2015-knob-court-cincinnati-ohio-45225 ; https://pcapidev.ohiohome.org/ppd/proposals/2016/Preservation-HUDRentalSubsidy/Sutter%20View%20Rehabilitation%2016-0025.pdf
- The Census geocoder places 1700, 1800, 1900, 1990 and 1999 Sutter Ave and 2015 Knob Ct in Census Tract 86.01, Block Group 3. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year)
Block Group 3 of Tract 86.01 holds 340 units, more than the neighbourhood's 242 in 2020, so it reaches past English Woods. Margins of error are wide. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610086013/
- Median year built: 1981. The ACS year-built answers do not match the known 1960 build date of Sutter View, so treat the era split as rough.
- Units in structure: 107 in buildings of 50+ (Marquette Manor), 105 in 5-9, 78 in 3-4, 50 single-family attached and 0 detached.
- Tenure: all 313 occupied units are rented.
- Heating fuel: 186 electricity (59.4%), 127 utility gas (40.6%).
- The whole of Tract 86.01 (mostly North Fairmount) is older and more varied: median year built 1944, 568 of 1,172 units (48.5%) from 1939 or earlier, 739 detached houses, 64.8% rented, 36.4% electric heat. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061008601/

## Permits
- The City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building and mechanical permits for addresses in the city (reused from sedamsville.md, because cincinnati-oh.gov refused the connection). https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG; reused from sedamsville.md)
- July normal high 86F, low 65-66F (1991-2020). https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf
