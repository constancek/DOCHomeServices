# Walnut Hills (Cincinnati, OH 45206): verified local facts

## Check against the existing site copy
- `locations.ts` calls Walnut Hills "a historic hilltop neighborhood" with 19th-century homes and apartment buildings around Peebles Corner. That matches the sources below. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati
- It also says the housing is "much of it now split into units". The Census data supports "mostly multi-unit": 61% of units are in buildings of 5 or more, and 26% are in buildings of 50 or more (see Housing). Whether older single homes were *converted* is unverified. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061003700-census-tract-37-hamilton-oh/
- ZIP 45206 is right for most of the neighbourhood. Whether any edge streets use 45219 is unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati
- The `adjacent` list (East Walnut Hills, Mount Auburn, Avondale) was not checked against a city map. Unverified.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, a hilltop on the near east side, in Hamilton County. Annexed by the city in September 1869. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati
- The business centre is Peebles' Corner at E. McMillan Street and Gilbert Avenue. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Hills,_Cincinnati ; https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/85002835
- The city's 2020 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation builds Walnut Hills from census tracts 19, 36, 37 and 267, with 6,344 residents. I read this through a search index because the city site refused connections, so treat the 6,344 figure as unverified until someone opens the PDF. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/WalnutHills_2020.pdf

## Other local factors
- National Register districts: Gilbert-Sinton Historic District (listed 6 Oct 1983, about 13.2 acres around Morris, Gilbert and Sinton Avenues). Gilbert Row (rowhouses on Gilbert Ave, listed 1982). Peeble's Corner Historic District (about 80 buildings around E. McMillan and Gilbert, published 14 Nov 1985). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert%E2%80%93Sinton_Historic_District ; https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/85002835 ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/national-register-of-historic-places/nr-districts/

- Terrain: hilltop, so no Ohio River flood plain claim applies. FEMA flood zones were not checked. Unverified.
