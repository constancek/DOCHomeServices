# Millville, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## 1. Where it is
- **Village** in Butler County, OH, in Hanover and Ross townships, just west of Hamilton along US 27 between Cincinnati and Oxford. 0.58 sq mi. 2020 population 634. Founded 1815; a grist mill opened about 1805. ZIP 45013. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millville,_Ohio
- SR 748 (Millville-Shandon Rd) ends at SR 129 near the village. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_Route_748
- Village hall listed at 2860 Ross Hanover Rd (directory source, unverified). https://www.citydirectory.us/village-millville-ohio.html
- Geocoded: 2860 and 2900 Ross Hanover Rd fall in Millville village (16000US3950540), tract 108, Ross Township portion. Addresses on Millville Ave and Millville Oxford Rd geocode to Hanover Township, outside the village. Census Geocoder via census.mjs.
