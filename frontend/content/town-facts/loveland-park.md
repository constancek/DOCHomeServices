# Loveland Park, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## What the site already says
- locations.ts calls Loveland Park "a Warren County community just north of Loveland near the Little Miami River, with established neighborhoods of mid-century homes", and says "low ground near the river adds a backup risk in heavy rain".
- **Errors:** (1) It is not Warren County only: the CDP straddles **Deerfield Township, Warren County** and **Symmes Township, Hamilton County** (Loveland Castle on Shore Rd is on the Hamilton County side). (2) "Mid-century homes" is half right: 37.1% were built before 1960, but 53.5% date from 1980 or later and the median year built is 1990. (3) The river backup claim has no source: **unverified**, don't use.

## 1. Where it is
- Census-designated place (not a city) in Symmes Township (Hamilton County) and Deerfield Township (Warren County). Named after the city of Loveland. 2020 population 1,737; 721 housing units. Elevation 728 ft. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loveland_Park,_Ohio
- History: in the 1920s the Cincinnati Enquirer gave a 20-by-100-ft wooded lot along the Little Miami River with a one-year subscription; Loveland Castle was built on two of these lots. Residents formed the Loveland Park Association Inc. (a homeowners' association) in 1928. (same source)
- Census geocoder: 12025 Shore Rd, Loveland 45140 (Loveland Castle) is in Loveland Park CDP, Symmes Township, Hamilton County, tract 243.03. Coordinate checks at 39.290-39.305 N, 84.262 W fall in Loveland Park CDP, **Deerfield Township, Warren County**, tract 320.10, so most of the CDP's area is on the Warren side. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/coordinates (run directly; the unit split by county is unverified)
- ZIP 45140 (mailing city Loveland), from the geocoded address.
