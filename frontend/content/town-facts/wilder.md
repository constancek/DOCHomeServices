- ZIP is given as 41076 only. Wilder uses 41071 and 41076, and the city building itself is at 520 Licking Pike, Wilder, KY 41071. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilder,_Kentucky ; https://wilderky.gov/
- "Mid-century houses" / "mid-century homes" is wrong. Median year built is 1992; 69.4% of units date from 1980-99 and only 5.5% from 1940-59 (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2183172/

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in Campbell County, KY. 2020 population 3,176; about 3.75 sq mi. ZIPs 41071 and 41076. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilder,_Kentucky
- Neighbors: Highland Heights to the east and Cold Spring to the southeast (from those cities' pages). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highland_Heights,_Kentucky ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Spring,_Kentucky
- Some "Cold Spring, KY 41076" mailing addresses sit inside Wilder city limits (4 Plum St, Cold Spring geocodes to Wilder city). Check the place, not the mailing city. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## 3. Permits
- The state inspector list names Campbell County (Planning, Zoning and Building Inspection, 1098 Monmouth St, Suite 343, Newport) as the local building and local HVAC office for Wilder. It also lists a separate inspector for "City of Wilder - Single Family Dwellings" with a campbellcountyky.gov address, so single-family work appears to go through the county office too (exact split unverified). https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing inspections: state plumbing inspector (same list; from _shared.md pattern).
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: not researched; don't use.

## 4. Terrain and flood
- Wilder lies on the Licking River side of Campbell County; KY 9 (Licking Pike) runs through it. The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet has closed KY 9 between Aspen Ct. in Wilder and W. 12th St. in Newport for floodgate work (local news, undated; article fetch failed, so **unverified**). https://linknky.com/?p=47836
- A large landslide has closed Licking Pike in Wilder in the past (local news, undated). https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/campbell-county/wilder/residential-areas-see-uptick-in-traffic-during-licking-pike-closure
- Northern Kentucky hillsides are prone to slides (clay soils over Kope Formation shale), regional, not Wilder specific. https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1032/pdf/Kentucky.pdf
- Wilder participates in the NFIP; first flood map 1974, current effective map 08/10/2021. Which streets sit in the floodplain: unverified; check https://msc.fema.gov. https://www.fema.gov/cis/KY.html
- Historic districts: none found. St. John the Baptist Church is an individual NRHP listing (1980). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Campbell_County,_Kentucky
