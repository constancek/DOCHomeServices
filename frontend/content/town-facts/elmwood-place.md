## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, laid out 1875, incorporated 1890. About 0.32 sq mi, all land. Nearly surrounded by Cincinnati; small portion borders St. Bernard. Originally settled chiefly by German Catholics. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmwood_Place,_Ohio
- Population 2,087 (2020 census). ZIP 45216, which is much larger than the village (it also covers Cincinnati neighborhoods), so use village figures, not ZIP figures. Same source.
- Village hall: 6118 Vine Street. https://www.elmwoodplace-oh.gov/Content/ApplicationsForms/Building-Permit-Application.pdf (site refused connections; address confirmed in the Federal Register, section 6)
