# Milford, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'milford'): ZIP 45150, Clermont County, historic city on the Little Miami River, "preserved old Main Street district of 19th-century buildings", low ground near the river with flood and backup risk. The flood risk is confirmed below. A formal historic district is not confirmed (see section 6).

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** (village from 1836, city since 1982). Mostly in Clermont County, with a small piece in Hamilton County. Sits on the Little Miami River where the East Fork joins. 2020 census population 6,582; land area about 3.7 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milford,_Ohio and https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3950176/
- ZIP 45150 is much bigger than the city (14,581 housing units vs 3,490) and takes in Miami Township communities such as Mulberry, Mount Repose and Day Heights. Use city figures for "Milford", not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45150-45150/

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Milford city place 16000US3950176)
Source: tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3950176/
- Median year built: **1968** (ZIP 45150 overall: 1981).
- Of 3,490 units: **685 (19.6%) built 1939 or earlier**, 653 (18.7%) in the 1950s, 406 in the 1960s, 622 in the 1970s. **2,434 (69.7%) were built before 1980.** Only 454 (13.0%) were built 2000 or later, none 2020 or later.
- Home types: 1,485 (42.6%) single-family detached, 259 attached, 1,127 (32.3%) in buildings of 10 or more units (548 in 50+ unit buildings), 87 mobile homes.
- Tenure: 1,655 owner-occupied of 3,339 occupied units (**49.6%**); about half of households rent.
- Heating fuel (occupied units): utility gas 1,575 (47.2%), **electricity 1,389 (41.6%)**, fuel oil 125 (3.7%), propane 121 (3.6%), other 111. ZIP 45150 electricity share: 43.3%.
- Inference, not a statistic: a high electric-heat share plus many apartments suggests many heat pumps or electric furnaces in multi-unit buildings. ACS does not split heat pumps from electric resistance heat.

## 3. Permits (City of Milford)
- The city contracts with **National Inspection Corporation (NIC)** to administer building and electrical permits. Applications and plans go to the City Administrative Office; the zoning manager reviews them before NIC approves. https://www.milfordohio.org/departments/building_department.php and https://www.milfordohio.org/departments/permit_applications.php
- **Plumbing permits** inside Milford are issued by **Clermont County Public Health** (Board of Health), which asks for a copy of the Milford building permit with the application. https://clermonthealthohio.gov/services/permits_and_inspections/plumbing/residential_plumbing_permits.php
- **Unverified:** whether mechanical/HVAC permits also go through NIC (the city page names only building and electrical). Also unverified: processing times, fee schedule and plan-set counts (city pages conflict). Don't state any of these. Clermont County Permit Central lists Milford as a separate jurisdiction, so the county building office is not the answer for city addresses. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/helpful-links
- Note: city pages were read through search-result excerpts; the site blocked direct fetching.

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Flooding, verified (NWS gauge MLGO1, Little Miami River at Milford).** Action stage 13 ft, minor flood 17 ft, moderate 22 ft, major 27 ft. NWS impact notes: at 21 ft, flooding in low areas between Main Street and the river; at 27 ft (about the FEMA 1% flood), Locust, Cash, Elm, Water and Polk streets go under water along with parts of US 50. Flood of record 30.5 ft (March 1913); 27.3 ft in January 1959; 21.81 ft July 2001; 21.69 ft April 1998; 21.28 ft March 2008. https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/MLGO1 (data via https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/MLGO1)
- 1913 crest at Milford is still the flood of record (Midwest Regional Climate Center). https://mrcc.purdue.edu/1913Flood/communities/lovelandMilford
- **Historic district: unverified.** Main Street is old and widely called historic, but I found no National Register district or city design-review rule for it. Don't claim one. (A "North Milford Village Historic District" page in results is Milford, Michigan.)
- FEMA flood zone for specific parcels: unverified; check FEMA's Flood Map Service Center by address. https://msc.fema.gov
