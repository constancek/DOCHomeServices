## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County (incorporated April 5, 1940; council-manager government since 1955). About 3.5 sq mi, all land. ZIPs 45237 and 45213. Population 3,840 (2020 census). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amberley,_Ohio
- Village offices: 7149 Ridge Road, Cincinnati, OH 45237. https://www.amberleyvillage.org/Documents/Departments/Zoning/final_zoning_approval_-2025_updated_4-14-25a.pdf
- ZIP 45237 is much bigger and different (10,809 units, 47.7% owner-occupied, 30.9% electric heat). Use village figures, not ZIP figures (census.mjs, 86000US45237).

## 3. Permits
- **Zoning approval:** Amberley Village reviews projects covered by its zoning code; the owner submits the zoning approval form, and "a building permit may be issued" if plans meet the code. Requests go to the Village Manager / Zoning Administrator at 7149 Ridge Road. https://www.amberleyvillage.org/departments/zoning/index.php and https://www.amberleyvillage.org/Documents/Departments/Zoning/final_zoning_approval_-2025_updated_4-14-25a.pdf
- Homes in the **Rollman Estates or Rollman Reserve** HOAs must attach HOA approval to a village zoning request. Same PDF.
- Contractors "may be required to register with Amberley Village Income Tax Department." Same PDF.
- **Unverified:** which office issues the building or mechanical (HVAC) permit itself, and whether a furnace or AC swap needs village zoning approval. The village page names no issuing office and never mentions HVAC. Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections covers townships and "six contract jurisdictions" it does not name. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- Plumbing and electrical permit offices: unverified for Amberley (the _shared.md county pattern of Hamilton County Public Health / IBI is for unincorporated areas only).
