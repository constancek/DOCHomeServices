# Goshen, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'goshen'): ZIP 45122, "township community in northern Clermont County", suburban and rural homes, rural properties "may rely on wells and septic". See the end.

## 1. Where it is
- **Goshen** is an unincorporated **census-designated place** (2020 population 715) in central **Goshen Township**, centered on State Route 28 where it meets SR 132 and SR 48. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goshen,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 6757 Goshen Rd (township offices), 6707 Goshen Rd and 1800 State Route 28, Goshen 45122 = Goshen CDP (16000US3930996), tract 402.03, Goshen Township (06000US3902531010). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45122 and "Goshen" in everyday use mean the whole township (6,686 units), not just the 339-unit CDP. Both blocks below.
