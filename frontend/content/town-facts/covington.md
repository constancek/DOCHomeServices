# Covington, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'covington'): ZIP 41011, across the Ohio River from downtown Cincinnati, MainStrasse Village and Licking Riverside, 19th-century rowhouses and brick homes, low ground near the Ohio and Licking, original cast-iron and galvanized pipe. The ACS data below supports "19th-century housing". The cast-iron and galvanized pipe claim is not verified here.

## 1. Where it is
- Covington is an incorporated city in Kenton County, Kentucky, at the meeting of the Ohio and Licking rivers. The PDS office address is "Covington KY 41011". https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf
- ZIP 41011 is the main Covington ZIP. 41014 and 41015 also carry Covington addresses (unverified, don't use in copy).

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Covington city, place 16000US2117848)
Source: Census tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2117848-covington-ky/
- Median year built: **1947** (Kenton County: 1976, ACS 2024 1-year).
- Of 21,048 housing units, **9,268 (44.0%) were built in 1939 or earlier**. 12,681 (60.2%) were built before 1960. Only 1,720 (8.2%) were built 2010 or later.
- Home types: 11,319 (53.8%) single-family detached, 861 attached. 3,591 (17.1%) are in 2-4 unit buildings, and 5,135 (24.4%) are in buildings of 5 or more units.
- Occupied units: 18,265. Owner-occupied 9,464 (51.8%), renter-occupied 8,801 (48.2%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 10,838 (59.3%), **electricity 6,879 (37.7%)**, propane 201 (1.1%), fuel oil 41 (0.2%).
- ZCTA 41011 alone (same source and tables, censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41011-41011/): 14,566 units, 5,263 (36.1%) built 1939 or earlier, 43.5% single-family detached, 53.9% renter-occupied, 41.4% heat with electricity.

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits for Covington are issued by **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state inspector list names PDS as the local building and HVAC inspector for Covington. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf
- PDS fee schedule (effective July 1, 2024): residential HVAC $105 first system, $50 each additional. Do not use the fee in copy. https://pdskc.govbuilt.com/media/Downloadable%20Forms/Fees/Building%20-%20HVAC/HVAC.pdf
- Plumbing inspections in Kenton County are done by the state (Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction). Same source as above.
- Electrical: the state list says to contact the city or county office where work is done. Who issues electrical permits in Covington is unverified. Same source.
- Kentucky requires a permit for replacing a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/

## 4. Historic districts
- Covington has seven Historic Preservation Overlay (HPO) zones, set by ordinance. A Certificate of Appropriateness is required before any exterior alteration in an HPO, through the city's Historic Preservation Office. https://thecovky.gov/historic-preservation/
- Whether outdoor condensers or other mechanical equipment need a certificate: unverified (the city page doesn't say).
- Zone names reported by a local news source: central business district, Licking Riverside, MainStrasse Village, Mutter Gottes, Pike Street, Old Seminary Square (only six; unverified against the current code). https://linknky.com/?p=15626

## 5. Flood plain
- SD1 runs 15 flood pump stations on the Ohio and Licking rivers. Ten are in Covington, mostly built in the 1950s by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers after the 1937 flood. Pumps start when the Ohio reaches 44 feet (normal level about 25.1 feet), and outfall pipes are gated shut at high water. https://sd1.org/445/Flood-Levee-Protection-System
- The city installs removable floodgates in the floodwall when the river rises. https://www.covingtonky.gov/news/2019/02/11/piece-by-piece-a-floodgate-takes-shape
- Basement sewer backups during high water: not stated by SD1 (unverified as a source fact).

## 7. Climate
- Official Cincinnati station is CVG in Boone County, roughly 10 miles southwest of Covington (distance approximate). NWS lists the CVG July normal high as 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- Average 22 days a year at 90°F or above, calculated from 1991-2020 CVG daily data (not a published normal). https://data.rcc-acis.org
