# Corryville (Cincinnati, OH 45219): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls Corryville "a dense mix of early-1900s homes, apartments, and student rentals". Only part of that holds. ACS data (below) shows about a third of units predate 1940, but 40% were built in 2000 or later, and the median year built is 1985 (tract 33) and 1998 (tract 32). The largest group is buildings of 50 or more units. Describe it as old houses alongside large newer apartment blocks. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061003300-census-tract-33-hamilton-oh/
- The plumbing FAQ says "on call 7 days a week". Check against `stats.md`; it is not a town fact.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, immediately east of the University of Cincinnati, with Clifton to the northwest, Avondale to the north and east, Walnut Hills to the southeast and Mount Auburn to the south. ZIP 45219. Annexed by the city in 1870. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corryville,_Cincinnati (locator only)
- Other ZIPs at the edges (45229 near Avondale) are unverified.
- Short Vine (Vine Street) is the business district. Vine Street and Jefferson Avenue were realigned in the 1960s-70s to bypass it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine_Street,_Cincinnati (locator only)
- The city's Census profiles define Corryville as Census Tracts 32 and 33. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Corryville_2020.pdf
- 2020 Census: 4,373 residents and 2,362 housing units (up from 3,327 residents in 2010). Taken from Wikipedia's summary of the city profile; the city PDF would not load to check, so treat as unverified until confirmed. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Corryville_2020.pdf

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Tracts 32 and 33, Hamilton County)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061003200-census-tract-32-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061003300-census-tract-33-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040)
- Units: 809 (tract 32) + 1,454 (tract 33) = 2,263.
- Median year built: 1998 (tract 32), 1985 (tract 33).
- Built 1939 or earlier: 756 of 2,263 (33.4%). Built 2000 or later: 910 (40.2%). Built 1940-1999: 597 (26.4%).
- Units in structure: 236 single-family detached (10.4%), 63 single-family attached, 107 two-family, 451 in 3-4 unit buildings, 238 in 5-9, 103 in 10-19, 258 in 20-49, and 807 (35.7%) in buildings of 50 or more units. No mobile homes.
- Tenure: 1,824 of 1,968 occupied units are rented (92.7%); 144 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 1,132 electricity (57.5%), 748 utility gas (38.0%), 73 propane, 15 no fuel.

## Permits
- The City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues building, mechanical and plumbing permits for Cincinnati addresses. A permit is required to install or replace an air conditioner, and for all new and replacement plumbing, including fixture replacement. Unpermitted work carries an investigation fee equal to the permit fee. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/inspections/plumbing-inspections/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
