## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in southeastern Hamilton County; settled 1792 as Mercersburg, incorporated as Newtown in 1901. Surrounded by Anderson Township (separated by forming a paper township in the 1960s). About 2.2 sq mi. ZIP 45244. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtown,_Ohio
- Sits along the Little Miami River; Plainville lies across the river. The Little Miami Scenic Trail runs through the village. https://newtownohio.gov/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plainville,_Ohio
- 2020 census: 2,702 people, 1,207 housing units. (The ACS unit count below is higher; it may cover a wider geography or carry a large margin of error. Use percentages, not the count.) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtown,_Ohio
