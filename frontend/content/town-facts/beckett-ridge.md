# Beckett Ridge, OH: verified local facts (shared by every service)

## 1. Where it is
- Census-designated place in **West Chester Township** (unincorporated), Butler County, OH. ZIP 45069. A planned community begun in the 1970s on former Beckett family farmland, developed by Gary L. Schottenstein; at the time the largest planned unit development in Ohio. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_Ridge,_Ohio
- Built around the Beckett Ridge golf course, opened 1975 (Jack Kidwell design). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_Ridge,_Ohio ; https://www.journal-news.com/news/beckett-ridge-golf-club-new-owner-plans-improvements-additions/5wtRMFw209jlKUY1iKOsZL
- Geocoded: 5400 and 6055 Beckett Ridge Blvd and 8000 Beckett Center Dr fall in Beckett Ridge CDP (16000US3904840), tracts 111.28 and 111.29. Census Geocoder via census.mjs.

## 7. Errors in locations.ts
- "Built around a golf course from the 1980s on" is wrong on the start date: the community began in the 1970s and the course opened in 1975 (Wikipedia above). 17.1% of homes are 1960-79; the bulk (59.5%) is 1980-99, median 1990. Better: "begun in the 1970s, mostly built in the 1980s and 90s".
