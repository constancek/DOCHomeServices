# Owensville, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'owensville'): ZIP 45160, "small village in eastern Clermont County", historic core, rural homes on wells and septic. Small village: right. The rest is weak or unverified; see the end.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in **Stonelick Township**, platted in 1836 as Boston; the Owensville post office dates from 1832. 2020 population 786. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owensville,_Ohio
- Village offices: Owensville Municipal Building, 115 West Main Street. https://owensvilleoh.gov/?p=3079
- Geocoded: 200 N Broadway St and 100 N Broadway St, Owensville 45160 = Owensville village (16000US3959220), tract 408, Stonelick Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45160 and the surrounding rural roads reach well beyond the 786-person village.

## 3. Permits
- **Clermont County Building Inspection** issues permits in the village; its reports have an "Owensville Village" section. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2026/PERMITS%20DETAILED%20MAY%202026.pdf
- **HVAC confirmed:** 3 "Residential / HVAC Replacement" permits in Owensville Village in 2025; 38 in surrounding Stonelick Township. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2025/JANUARY%20DECEMBER%202025%20PERMITS%20DETAILED.pdf
- Fee schedule covers mechanical (furnace and condensing unit replacement, with electrical inspection) and electrical permits; 2019 fees, don't quote. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FeeSchedule/permit-central-fee-schedule-03-28-19.pdf
- The village runs its own zoning (ordinance dated March 1, 2016; zoning contact 513.732.5350 per county page). https://owensvilleoh.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/3-1-16-Village-of-Owensville-Zoning-final2R-1.pdf ; https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/zoning
- A rescinded state rule once certified a village building department; it is not active. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/ohio/Ohio-Admin-Code-4101-2-72-77
- **Plumbing:** Clermont County Public Health, county-wide. https://clermonthealthohio.gov/services/permits_and_inspections/plumbing/residential_plumbing_permits.php
