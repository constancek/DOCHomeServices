## 4. Terrain
- Homes on Fort Henry Drive reported a sliding hillside (leaning trees, cracked road, shifting deck and shed). The city attorney said hill slides are common across Northern Kentucky because of hills and clay soil, and that city and SD1 tests found new construction was not the cause. Article undated. https://www.wcpo.com/money/consumer/dont-waste-your-money/n-ky-homeowners-worry-their-street-is-silding
- March 2025: Kentucky Transportation Cabinet closed part of Kyles Lane (between about 800 and 845 Kyles Lane) for landslide repair. The article names Kenton County, not a city; whether that stretch is inside Fort Wright is **unverified** (409 Kyles Ln geocodes to Fort Wright). https://linknky.com/news/2025/03/25/kyles-lane-closure-landslide-repair/
- Kentucky Geological Survey: heavy or prolonged rain and vegetation clearing are common landslide triggers. https://www.uky.edu/KGS/education/factsheet/landslide_factsheet.pdf
- No river flood-plain issue found for homes.
