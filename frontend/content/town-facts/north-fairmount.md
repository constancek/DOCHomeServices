## Where it is
- North Fairmount is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45225, on the west side above the Mill Creek valley. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Fairmount,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- The 2020 Census counted 1,590 residents, down from 1,812 in 2010. The city's statistical approximation uses Census Tract 86.01, Block Groups 1 and 2. Block Group 3 of the same tract is counted as English Woods. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/NorthFairmount_2020.1.pdf ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/EnglishWoods_2020.pdf (both seen via search snippets only, because the city site refused connections)
- The Census geocoder places these addresses in Tract 86.01: 1600 and 1800 Baltimore Ave and 1700 Carll St (Block Group 2), and 2500 Beekman St (Block Group 1). 1900 Sutter Ave falls in Block Group 3 (English Woods). 2600-2668 Baltimore Ave are outside the tract. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- History: factories grew along the Mill Creek at the foot of the hill. French and German immigrants settled first, then Italians around 1900. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Fairmount,_Cincinnati (cites the 1943 WPA guide)
