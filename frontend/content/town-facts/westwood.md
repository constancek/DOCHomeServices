## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood on the west side, in Hamilton County. Village incorporated 1868, annexed by Cincinnati 1896. It is the city's largest neighbourhood by area and population. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westwood,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- 2020 Census: 33,774 residents and 16,456 housing units (city's Statistical Neighborhood Approximation; city PDF could not be reached, figures via Wikipedia's citation). https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Westwood_2020.1.pdf
- City SNA 2020 boundary: 3,732 acres. Census tracts whose internal points fall inside it: 100.02, 100.03, 100.04, 101, 102.01, 102.02 and 109. Tract 102.01 crosses the city line (4000 Werk Rd geocodes outside Cincinnati). https://data.cincinnati-oh.gov/d/i9zh-juvu ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Main corridors: Harrison Ave, Montana Ave, Westwood Northern Blvd, Werk Rd, Queen City Ave, Ferguson Rd (all geocoded to Westwood tracts). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
