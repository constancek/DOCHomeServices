## Where it is
- Oakley is one of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, on the east side, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45209. It became a village in 1898, and Cincinnati annexed it in 1913 after a 1911 vote. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakley,_Cincinnati (used to locate the source)
- Madison Road is the main corridor, and Oakley Square is the business district along it. The former Milacron plant site in northern Oakley (1907-2002) is now Oakley Station. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakley,_Cincinnati
- City planning builds Oakley from 2020 tracts 52, 53.01 and 53.02, plus part of tract 276 (block group 1). Population in the 2020 Census was 11,761. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Oakley_2020.pdf (PDF would not load; figures from a search snippet, unverified)

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, tracts 52 + 53.01 + 53.02 combined, partial tract 276 excluded)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005200-census-tract-52-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005301-census-tract-5301-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005302-census-tract-5302-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040, B25077)
- Total housing units: 6,123. Of these, 2,923 (47.7%) were built in 1939 or earlier and 768 (12.5%) in 2000 or later.
- Median year built: 1938 in tract 52, 1960 in tract 53.01, 1955 in tract 53.02. In tract 52, 71.3% of homes (1,369 of 1,919) predate 1940.
- Units in structure: 2,611 single-family detached (42.6%), 398 attached, 635 two-family, 637 in 3-4 unit buildings, and 747 in buildings of 50 or more units (507 of those are in tract 53.01). There are no mobile homes.
- Tenure: 3,028 of 5,777 occupied units are rented (52.4%).
- Heating fuel: 3,541 utility gas (61.3%), 2,108 electricity (36.5%), 51 bottled gas. Electric heat ranges from 23.4% in tract 52 to 51.3% in tract 53.01.
- ZIP 45209 (wider than Oakley, ACS 2020-2024): 6,572 units, median year built 1949, 44.1% pre-1940, 39.1% electric heat. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45209-45209/

## Flooding (local news; use at most twice in any page)
- On 17 July 2026 a flash flood hit Oakley. Duck Creek overflowed, the Duck Creek flood gate in Oakley and Madisonville did not close, and firefighters rescued people from a car on Madison Road. MSD logged more than 900 reports of sewer backups and basement flooding citywide. https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2026-07-20/rain-rescues-sewer-backups-cincinnati-duck-creek-flooding
