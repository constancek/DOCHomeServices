# Hartwell (Cincinnati, OH 45216): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- ZIP: `locations.ts` gives 45215. The Census geocoder puts Hartwell addresses in 45216 (200 Parkway Ave, 8000 and 8400 Anthony Wayne Ave, 25 E Galbraith Rd all return "CINCINNATI, OH, 45216"). 45215 is the ZIP for neighbouring Wyoming (300 Burns Ave returns Wyoming, 45215). Some sources list Hartwell under both ZIPs, so use 45216 as the primary. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=200+Parkway+Ave,+Cincinnati,+OH&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&layers=all&format=json
- "Streetcar suburb": unverified. Hartwell was platted in 1868 and named after John W. Hartwell, vice president of the Cincinnati, Hamilton and Dayton Railway, when its station was there. That makes it a railroad suburb. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartwell,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- "Victorian and early-1900s homes" and "century-old homes" overstate the housing age. Median year built is 1949, and only 33.1% of units predate 1940 (see below). "Tree-lined streets" is unverified.
- `county: 'Cincinnati Neighborhoods'` is a grouping label. The county is Hamilton.
- The FAQ says "any day of the week". Check it against the brief's no-"7-day" rule.

## Where it is
- Hartwell is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, at the city's northern edge. It incorporated as a village in 1876, and Cincinnati annexed it in 1912. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartwell,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- The Census geocoder places 200 Parkway Ave, 8000 and 8400 Anthony Wayne Ave, 8100 and 8300 Vine St and 25 E Galbraith Rd inside the City of Cincinnati, in Census Tract 60. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress?address=8400+Anthony+Wayne+Ave,+Cincinnati,+OH&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&layers=all&format=json
- Boundaries per Wikipedia: Caldwell Park and the Ronald Reagan Cross County Highway to the south, I-75 to the east, and Millsdale St and Compton Rd to the north. 150 Caldwell Dr geocodes to Tract 222, not Tract 60. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartwell,_Cincinnati
- Neighbours: Wyoming is next door (verified by geocoder above). The rest of the neighbour list is unverified.
- 2020 Census: 5,806 residents and 2,891 housing units. This comes via Wikipedia and is unverified because the city's neighbourhood PDF would not load. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartwell,_Cincinnati
