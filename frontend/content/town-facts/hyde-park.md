# Hyde Park (Cincinnati, OH 45208): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls the homes "early-1900s" Tudors, colonials and brick foursquares. The era is right (median year built 1938-1948 by tract, see below), but the specific house styles are unverified. No government source for them was found.
- `locations.ts` lists Oakley, Mount Lookout and East Walnut Hills as neighbours. All three are confirmed. Evanston, O'Bryonville and the City of Norwood also border it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_Park,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- `locations.ts` mentions "mature trees over old sewer laterals" (unverified) and its FAQ says "7 days a week", which breaks the brief's no-"7-day" rule.

## Where it is
- Hyde Park is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County. The city's statistical approximation uses Census Tracts 49, 50 and 51. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/HydePark_2020.pdf
- The 2020 Census counted 14,193 residents: 6,644 in Tract 49, 5,029 in Tract 50 and 2,520 in Tract 51. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/HydePark_2020.pdf
- ZIP 45208 is larger than the neighbourhood. ACS 2024 5-year gives the ZIP 17,943 residents and 9,027 housing units, so don't use ZIP figures as Hyde Park figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45208-45208/
- History: the village incorporated in 1896. Cincinnati annexed it in 1903 (west) and 1909 (east). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_Park,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- Terrain: the neighbourhood sits on a plateau above the Ohio and Miami valleys, with Hyde Park Square as its business district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_Park,_Cincinnati ; https://planning.org/greatplaces/neighborhoods/2010/hydepark.htm . Wikipedia's claim that the Ohio River bounds it is unverified and probably wrong (Columbia-Tusculum and the East End lie between).

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)

## Other local factors
- Observatory Historic District: National Register listing 78002080 (1978), on Observatory Place and Observatory Avenue around the Cincinnati Observatory, with houses built from 1873 to the early 1900s. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/78002080 . National Register listing alone doesn't trigger design review. Local designation status is unverified.
- No local historic district for Hyde Park Square was found (unverified).
- Flood: FEMA flood zones were not checked. Because the neighbourhood is on a plateau, river flooding is unlikely to be a factor, but this is unverified.
