## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **charter city** in Hamilton County (village 1916, city 1941, charter approved November 2007; mayor). About 1.83 sq mi, nearly all land. About 10 miles north of downtown Cincinnati. College Hill (a Cincinnati neighborhood) borders it to the south, Mount Healthy to the north. 2020 population 9,663. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_College_Hill,_Ohio
- ZIPs 45224, 45239, 45231 are all much bigger than the city; use city figures. Census geocoder places 1646 W Galbraith Rd, 6800 Simpson Ave and 1500 W Galbraith Rd in place 16000US3956322 (tract 218.01). Run via census.mjs.
