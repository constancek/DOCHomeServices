# Park Hills, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'park-hills'): ZIP 41011, hillside city beside Devou Park above Covington, "early-1900s homes" on steep streets, heavy rain on basement drains.
**Errors / unverified in locations.ts:** "early-1900s homes" is overstated. The area was developed from 1926 and incorporated 1927; median year built is 1953 and only 31.4% of units predate 1940. Better: 1920s-1950s homes. The entry also misses that 30.1% of units are in 5+ unit buildings. The basement-drain claim has no source. Every-day wording conflicts with the brief's hours ban.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated city in Kenton County, Kentucky, beside Devou Park; developed 1926, incorporated 1927; 2020 population 3,162; 0.78 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Hills,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- Geocoded 1106 Amsterdam Rd and 1000 Old State Rd, Park Hills, KY 41011 to place "Park Hills city" (16000US2159255), tract 649. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## 4. Historic district
- **National Register:** Park Hills Historic District, listed 2008-03-13 (ref 07001252); "much of the city" is in it. Bounded by Devou Park on the north and northwest, Sleepy Hollow Road on the west; filed under "Historic Residential Suburbs in the United States, 1830-1960". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Kenton_County,_Kentucky ; https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/2b52a751-3b53-4b21-8ae6-78030039a4dd
- National Register listing alone does not restrict private work. A Park Hills local historic overlay or design review: none found, **unverified**.

## 5. Terrain
