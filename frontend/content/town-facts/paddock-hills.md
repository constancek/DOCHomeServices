# Paddock Hills (Cincinnati, OH 45229): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` (and the plumbing parts files) call the homes "early- and mid-1900s". Roughly right, but the block group that holds Paddock Hills has a median year built of 1951 and 40% of its units date from 1960-1979 (see below). "Galvanized supply lines", "nearly every home runs its gas water heater off the basement floor" and hard water "common on the north side" are all unverified. ACS shows electricity, not gas, heats about half the occupied units in the block group.
- "Near the Norwood Lateral" and "tucked between Paddock Road and the Norwood Lateral" are unverified. Paddock Road itself is confirmed (4200 and 4400 Paddock Rd geocode to this tract). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- `water-heater-repair-parts/part2.ts` says "7 days a week", which the brief bans.
- No neighbour list exists in `locations.ts` for this town. Bordering neighbourhoods (Bond Hill, North Avondale, Roselawn) are unverified, so don't name them.

## Where it is
- Paddock Hills is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, ZIP 45229. The 2020 Census counted 1,038 residents and 547 housing units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddock_Hills,_Cincinnati (to locate the source; the city's 2020 Census PDF at cincinnati-oh.gov timed out)
- Census geocoder: 4400 Paddock Hills Ln, 1266 Avon Dr, 4200 Paddock Rd and 4350 Reading Rd all fall in Census Tract 65.02, Block Group 1 (GEOID 390610065021). 4549 Paddock Rd falls in Block Group 3. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Avon Fields Golf Course is in or beside the neighbourhood (unverified, from a listing site only).
- The neighbourhood is known for Dutch Colonial Revival, Tudor Revival and bungalow houses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddock_Hills,_Cincinnati . A local history site says most historic homes were built 1910-1935 and the area was annexed in 1903 (unverified; the site would not load).

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year)
Block Group 1 of Tract 65.02 is the closest match (1,143 residents, 723 units, against 547 units in the 2020 count), so it likely also takes in apartments along Reading Road. Block-group margins are wide. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/15000US390610065021-bg-1-tract-6502-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25001, B25003, B25024, B25034, B25035, B25040)
- Median year built: 1951 (MOE plus or minus 18 years).
- By era: 1939 or earlier 302 (41.8%); 1940-1959 130 (18.0%); 1960-1979 291 (40.2%); none from 1980 on.
- Units in structure: 205 single-family detached (28.4%), 10 two-family, 17 in 3-4 unit buildings, 237 in 5-49 unit buildings, 254 in buildings of 50 or more.
- Tenure: 462 of 667 occupied units rented (69.3%), 205 owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: electricity 342 (51.3%), utility gas 325 (48.7%).

Whole Tract 65.02 for context (3,678 residents, 1,781 units): median year built 1938; 1,138 units (63.9%) built 1939 or earlier; 870 single-family detached (48.8%); 646 of 1,610 occupied units rented (40.1%); gas 1,015 (63.0%), electricity 562 (34.9%). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006502-census-tract-6502-hamilton-oh/
