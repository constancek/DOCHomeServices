# Kenwood (Sycamore Township, OH 45236 / 45242): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives Kenwood one ZIP, 45236. The CDP also takes in 45242: 8800 Kenwood Rd geocodes to Kenwood CDP with ZIP 45242. Stated ZIP should be "45236 and parts of 45242". https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mid-century homes alongside newer, larger houses" doesn't hold up. The median build year is 1967, 46.7% of units date from 1960-79 and only 3.8% from 2000 or later. Nor is it all houses: 22.9% of units sit in buildings of 5+ units (410 in buildings of 50+), and 38.3% of households rent. Copy should mention 1960s-70s homes, condos and apartments, and say little about "newer" homes. See Housing.
- "Upscale" is unverified. No income figure was checked.
- Nearby streets aren't all Kenwood. 4300 Kugler Mill Rd and 4500 E Galbraith Rd geocode to Rossmoyne CDP, and 7800 E Galbraith Rd to Concorde Hills CDP. All three are in Sycamore Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Where it is
- Kenwood is a census-designated place in unincorporated Sycamore Township, Hamilton County. Its 2020 Census population was 7,570. Wikipedia gives its area as both 2.42 and 2.3 sq mi, so treat the area as unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenwood,_Ohio
- 7875 Montgomery Rd (Kenwood Towne Centre area), 7300 Kenwood Rd and 8100 Kenwood Rd all geocode to Kenwood CDP, Sycamore Township, tract 240.01, ZIP 45236. 8800 Kenwood Rd falls in tract 235.22, ZIP 45242. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits
- **Zoning certificate first (Sycamore Township):** The township issues zoning certificates through the Cloudpermit portal, and each project element needs its own application. Final inspections are booked through Cloudpermit with 24 hours' notice. The township has warned of fake invoices sent under its name. This is from search snippets only, because the township site returned 403 this session. https://sycamoretownship.org/departments/planning-and-zoning/permitting-inspections/ ; https://sycamoretownship.org/faq-items/do-i-need-to-apply-for-a-building-permit-with-hamilton-county-also/
- **Building permits:** Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections issues them after the township zoning certificate. Small residential sheds (200 sq ft or less) and fences are exempt from the county permit (snippet only). https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi (from _shared.md, plus the township FAQ above)
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). From _shared.md. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- **HVAC/mechanical permits:** unverified. It's also unverified whether replacing an AC or furnace needs a township zoning certificate. Only third-party permit sites were found, and they weren't used.

## Utilities
- Duke Energy Ohio delivers power and sends the bill. Through Sycamore Township's electric aggregation, Dynegy supplies power by default, and residents can opt out. Rates change, so don't quote them. https://sycamoretownship.org/information/aggregation-information/
