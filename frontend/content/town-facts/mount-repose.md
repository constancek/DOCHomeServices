# Mount Repose, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'mount-repose'):**
- "community in the Milford area" is loose: it is an unincorporated CDP in Miami Township, about 4 miles northeast of Milford on SR 28, not part of the city. Permits go to the county, not Milford (section 3). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Repose,_Ohio
- "mid-century and newer homes" is roughly right (median 1978; 40.4% built 1960-79), but 44.7% were built 1980 or later and 10.9% of units are in 5+ unit buildings (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3953032/
- ZIP 45150 is shared with Milford, Mulberry and Day Heights; use CDP figures, not ZIP figures. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45150-45150/
- "We respond every day, weekends included" is a company claim; check stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated **census-designated place** in Miami Township, northwestern Clermont County. 2.05 sq mi, all land; population 4,648 (2020). ZIP 45150. Mulberry borders it to the west, Day Heights to the south. SR 28 runs through it (Goshen about 4 mi NE, Milford about 4 mi SW). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Repose,_Ohio
- Began with a country store in the 1820s; post office 1867-1907. Same source.

## 3. Permits (unincorporated Miami Township, Clermont County)
- **Clermont County Permit Central / Building Inspection Department**, 2275 Bauer Road, issues building, electrical and mechanical permits on one application. "Heating Systems" and "Cooling Systems" are on its list of projects that need a permit; electrical and mechanical work gets rough and final inspections. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/frequently-asked-questions ; http://building.clermontcountyohio.gov/Inspections.aspx
- County monthly reports list residential "HVAC Replacement" permits by township. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2026/PERMITS%20DETAILED%20APRIL%202026.pdf
- **Zoning first:** a Miami Township zoning certificate is often needed to get the county building permit; the county checklist asks for township zoning approval at application for residential work. Which HVAC jobs need a township zoning certificate: unverified. https://www.miamitwpoh.gov/PDF/CD/ZoningFAQ-062024.pdf ; http://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FormsHandouts/1%20Permit%20Checklist.pdf

## 4. Utilities
- Electric: **Duke Energy Ohio** delivers power; Miami Township runs an opt-out electric aggregation program (supplier only, Duke still maintains lines). Rates change; don't quote. https://www.miamitwpoh.gov/PDF/PopDocs/050620-Aggregation.pdf . Co-op service at any address: unverified.
- Water: Greater Cincinnati Water Works lists Miami Township (Clermont) as outside its service area. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/water/about/outside-our-service-area . Which utility serves an address, and well/septic use: unverified.
