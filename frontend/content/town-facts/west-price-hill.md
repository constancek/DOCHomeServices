# West Price Hill (Cincinnati, OH 45205 / 45238): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives the ZIP as 45238 only. The eastern half (Glenway Ave in the 3900-4300 blocks, Sunset Ave, Rosemont Ave, Manss Ave) geocodes to 45205. West of about the 4700 block of Glenway, and along Rapid Run Rd and Fehr Rd, it's 45238. Use "45205 and 45238". Source: US Census Geocoder, https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- The `adjacent` list includes Lower Price Hill. That's unverified and probably wrong. Lower Price Hill sits in the river valley below East Price Hill, and East Price Hill sits between it and West Price Hill. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati (to locate the source)
- The site gives Covedale its own page. Inside city limits, tract 107 belongs to the city's 2010 West Price Hill statistical neighbourhood. Whether Covedale is part of West Price Hill is unverified. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/Census/2010/West-Price-Hill.pdf

## Where it is
- West Price Hill is one of Cincinnati's city neighbourhoods, on the west side. It was part of Delhi Township until the city annexed it in 1902. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_Hill,_Cincinnati
- Hamilton County.
- 2020 Census: 19,960 residents, 8,962 housing units, 8,108 households. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/WestPriceHill_2020.pdf (via Wikipedia; city site unreachable this session)
- The terrain is hilltop and hillside above the Ohio valley. USGS says landslides in thin colluvium are "widespread on steeper slopes" of the valley walls around Cincinnati. https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b2059A. Slope or landslide data for individual West Price Hill streets is unverified.

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Hamilton County tracts 97, 98, 99.01, 99.02, 107 combined)
These five tracts geocode to West Price Hill addresses and appear on the city's 2010 tract list. They hold 7,720 of the city's 8,962 units, so treat them as a close proxy, not an exact match. Tract 100.05 (partly in) is excluded. Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061009700-census-tract-97-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061009800-census-tract-98-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061009901-census-tract-9901-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061009902-census-tract-9902-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061010700-census-tract-107-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built by tract: 1944 (97), 1964 (98), 1951 (99.01), 1951 (99.02), 1938 (107).
- Year built (7,720 units): 2,825 (36.6%) in 1939 or earlier, 965 in the 1940s, 1,650 in the 1950s and 1,102 in the 1960s. That makes 3,717 units (48.1%) from 1940-1969. Only 302 units (3.9%) date from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 4,647 single-family detached (60.2%), 196 attached, 713 two-family, 678 in 3-4 unit buildings, 176 in 5-9, 678 in 10-19, 162 in 20-49 and 459 in buildings of 50 or more.
- Tenure: of 7,067 occupied units, 3,517 are owner-occupied (49.8%) and 3,550 are rented (50.2%).
- Heating fuel: 4,912 utility gas (69.5%), 1,956 electricity (27.7%), 90 propane.

## Permits (City of Cincinnati, Department of Buildings & Inspections)
- Plumbing, electrical and HVAC work each need their own permit. Replacing a built-in plumbing fixture or appliance also needs one. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/
- Installing or replacing air conditioning or fuel-burning equipment needs a mechanical permit. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
- The city contracts electrical permit review and inspection to Inspections Bureau, Inc. (IBI). https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections
- Fees, timings and phone numbers are unverified (the city site couldn't be reached this session).
