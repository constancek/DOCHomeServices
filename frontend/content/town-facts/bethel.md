**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'bethel'): ZIP 45106, "historic village in southeastern Clermont County", 19th-century core, rural homes that "may run on wells and septic". Village and age partly right; see the end.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in **Tate Township**. Platted 1798 as Plainfield, replatted 1802 as Bethel. 2020 population 2,620. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethel,_Ohio
- Village offices: Municipal Building, 120 North Main Street. https://bethel-oh.gov/
- Geocoded: 120 N Main St and 200 W Plane St, Bethel 45106 = Bethel village (16000US3906068), tract 418, Tate Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 45106 covers rural Tate Township beyond the village; use village figures for "Bethel".
