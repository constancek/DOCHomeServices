# Kennedy Heights (Cincinnati, OH 45213): verified local facts (service-neutral)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says "large early-1900s homes". Only 46.4% of units in Tract 58 predate 1940; 47.8% were built 1940-1979, and 21.5% of units sit in buildings of 5 or more. "Large early-1900s homes" overstates both age and size. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005800/
- `locations.ts` calls it "a Victorian-era resort community". Lewis Kennedy filed subdivision plans in 1885 and the neighbourhood had one resort, the 50-room Yononte Inn (closed 1907, burned 1909). It was a subdivision with a resort hotel, not a resort community. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Heights,_Cincinnati
- `commonIntro` says "historic homes", which suggests a designation. No local or National Register historic district was found (see below).
- The FAQ says "every day, including Saturdays and Sundays" and relevance says "We respond every day", close to the brief's no-"7-day" rule.
- The entry has no `adjacent` list. Madisonville's and Pleasant Ridge's entries list Kennedy Heights as adjacent. Wikipedia names Pleasant Ridge and Silverton as neighbours; the Census geocoder puts 3950 Woodford Rd in Silverton village, so Silverton borders it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Heights,_Cincinnati ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress
- "Known for its arts center": the Kennedy Heights Arts Center was not checked (unverified). Trivia either way.

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, Hamilton County, ZIP 45213. Once part of Columbia Township; organised as a village in 1896 after an Ohio Supreme Court decision; annexed by Cincinnati on 24 July 1914. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Heights,_Cincinnati
- 2020: 5,166 residents, 2,716 housing units (Wikipedia, citing city/Census figures). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Heights,_Cincinnati
- Census geocoder: 6300 Kennedy Ave, 3800 Davenant Ave, 6546 and 6620 Montgomery Rd, and 3600 Woodford Rd all fall in Tract 58, City of Cincinnati. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress
- Tract 58 has 2,528 units against the neighbourhood's 2,716, so the city's approximation may include part of another tract (unverified). ZIP 45213 also covers Pleasant Ridge and Silverton; don't use ZIP figures as Kennedy Heights figures.
