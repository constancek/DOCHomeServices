# Greenhills, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'greenhills'):**
- "close to a century of plumbing wear" / "1930s-era homes" as the norm: only 18.7% of homes were built 1939 or earlier; 52.1% date from 1940-59 and the village median is 1955 (section 2). The federal build ran 1935-1938, with more housing added to 1950. https://greenhillsohio.gov/history/
- "original cottages and townhomes": the village and Wikipedia describe the originals as International-style townhomes (row units) and apartments, plus postwar single-family houses for veterans. "Cottages" is unverified. https://greenhillsohio.gov/history/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhills,_Ohio
- "now a National Historic Landmark district" is right (January 11, 2017), but the landmark covers about three quarters of the village, not all of it (section 4).
- "Original supply lines and drains": unverified. Don't use.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in northern Hamilton County, ZIP 45218. Village hall: 11000 Winton Road. 2020 census population 3,741; about 1.24 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhills,_Ohio and https://greenhillsohio.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/needpermit.pdf
- One of three New Deal Greenbelt towns built by the federal Resettlement Administration; superblocks A-F built and owned by the federal government 1935-1938; the government sold off the property in 1950. https://greenhillsohio.gov/history/
- The village says it has about 1,660 homes. https://greenhillsohio.gov/history/
- ZIP 45218 is nearly the same area as the village (1,555 housing units vs 1,490), so ZIP figures can stand in. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45218/
- Neighbors (Forest Park, Springfield Township, Winton Woods park): unverified, not checked.

## 3. Permits (Village of Greenhills)
- A **Village of Greenhills Building Permit** is required for "new or replacement heating, ventilation, and air conditioning equipment or ducts," and for installing, altering, repairing or replacing any electrical, gas, mechanical or plumbing system. Every building permit includes a zoning review. Work should not start until the permit is issued. https://greenhillsohio.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/needpermit.pdf
- The village issues **building and electrical permits**. https://greenhillsohio.gov/?p=86
- **Plumbing permits**: Hamilton County environmental health, plumbing division (Hamilton County Public Health). https://greenhillsohio.gov/?p=86
- The village uses the Hamilton County building fee schedule; plans are reviewed by a state-certified examiner and inspected by a state-licensed inspector. Fee amounts and turnaround: unverified, don't state. https://www.greenhillsohio.us/index.php/departments/building-zoning

## 4. Historic district rules
- Greenhills Historic District: on the National Register; designated a **National Historic Landmark January 11, 2017**. The NHL boundary covers about three quarters of the village (all but the northeast section past Farragut and Ingram roads) and adds veterans' houses on Damon Road, Gambier Circle and the south side of Ingram. https://greenhillsohio.gov/history/ and https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/88003066
- A separate **local historic district** (zoning code chapter 1143, H Historic Overlay) has design guidelines. Exterior alterations need a **Certificate of Appropriateness** from the village before work starts; the focus is what is visible from the street; in-kind repairs are exempt. https://greenhillsohio.gov/?p=138 and https://www.zoneomics.com/code/greenhills-OH/chapter_18
