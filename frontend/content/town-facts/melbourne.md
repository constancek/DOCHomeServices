# Melbourne, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'melbourne'):**
- "Small village" is wrong. Melbourne is a home rule-class **city** (Kentucky has no villages). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne,_Kentucky
- "Along the Ohio River east of Newport" is loose: it is upriver (southeast) on the south bank, with Silver Grove between it and Newport-area cities to the west. Same source.
- "Older homes" is supported: median year built 1958, 30.5% built 1939 or earlier (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2151438/
- "Low riverside ground" and "high water brings a real backup risk": not confirmed for Melbourne homes by any source found (unverified). The city is in the NFIP with flood maps since 1974 (section 4), but no Melbourne flood reports were found.
- "Every day, weekends included" are company claims; check against stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in northeastern Campbell County on the south bank of the Ohio River. 2020 population 458; 0.83 sq mi. ZIP 41059. Silver Grove to the west; Coldstream, Ohio, across the river. KY 8 (Mary Ingles Hwy) follows the river; downtown Cincinnati is about 11 miles northwest. St. Anne Convent and St. Anne Woods and Wetlands are in the city. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne,_Kentucky
- City hall: 502 Garfield Ave (directory listings, not the city's own site). https://web.sos.ky.gov/land/cities.aspx?CTR=261

## 3. Permits
- The state inspector list names Campbell County (Planning, Zoning and Building Inspection, 1098 Monmouth St, Suite 343, Newport) as the local building and local HVAC office for Melbourne. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing inspections: state plumbing inspector. Same list.
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: don't use.

## 4. Flood, terrain, historic
- NFIP participating community; first flood hazard map 02/01/1974, first FIRM 03/28/1980, current effective map 03/03/2014. Which homes are in the floodplain: unverified; check https://msc.fema.gov. https://www.fema.gov/cis/KY.html
- Stretches of KY 8 flood when the Ohio is high (2026 Cincinnati forecast; Melbourne not named, so **unverified** here). https://www.yahoo.com/news/ohio-river-flooding-levels-expected-170640043.html
- No historic district found. Trutschell House and Tiemeyer House are individual NRHP listings (1983). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Campbell_County,_Kentucky
- Regional hillside slide risk, not city specific. https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1032/pdf/Kentucky.pdf
