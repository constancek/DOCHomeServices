## 3. Permits (the city issues its own; not Butler County)
- **City of Fairfield Building & Zoning Division**, 5350 Pleasant Ave. Residential applications go there. https://fairfield-city.org/167/Building-Permits
- **HVAC permits** are required for "New/ replacement electric or gas heating and air conditioning equipment" and "Alteration or addition of duct work". Work must be done by a heating and air conditioning contractor registered in Fairfield (gas lines excepted). (same source)
- **Electrical permits** are required for new or replacement building service and for "Reconnecting of heating and air conditioning appliances when replaced". Electrical contractors must be registered in Fairfield. (same source)
- Homeowners may do the work on their own single-family home if they pull the permit and schedule inspections. (same source)
- The city application asks for BTU output, fuel type and number of units on HVAC permits. https://www.fairfield-city.org/DocumentCenter/View/4862/PERMIT-APPLICATION-2019-WEB
- Plumbing permits: not covered on that page, **unverified**. Fees and timings: unverified.

## 6. Other local notes
- **Flood:** the Great Miami River overflows into low-lying areas, usually mid-winter to mid-spring. Backwater reaches about half a mile up Pleasant Run Creek; the worst flooding is in that reach and along the river's east bank. The city designates floodway and flood fringe areas. https://fairfield-city.org/541/Flooding
- The city has bought out flood-prone homes along Pleasant Run (31 in earlier phases, 17 more targeted by 2013) after the 2003 Father's Day flood. https://fairfield-city.org/580/Pleasant-Run-Creek-Riparian-Corridor ; https://www.journal-news.com/news/fairfields-eroding-creek-banks-pose-threat-to-roads-and-utilities/EDVQ2EJMW5AFTKGMW7GNQRDLME
