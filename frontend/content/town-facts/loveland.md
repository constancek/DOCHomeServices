# Loveland, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'loveland'):**
- `county: 'Clermont County, OH'` is incomplete. The city spans Hamilton, Clermont and Warren counties; most residents live in the Hamilton County part, while City Hall is in Clermont. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loveland,_Ohio (geocoder: 120 W Loveland Ave is county 025 Clermont; 1 Tiger Trail and 400 Loveland Madeira Rd are county 061 Hamilton)
- ZIP given as 45140 only; a few annexed businesses use 45249. Same Wikipedia source.
- "mix of historic and newer homes" leans old. Median year built is 1985; only 5.4% predate 1940 and 58.6% were built 1980 or later (section 2). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3945108/
- "Low riverside ground adds a flood and backup risk" needs care: downtown floods historically, but a dike was built in 1962-63 after the 1959 flood, and most homes sit on the hills (section 6).
- "We respond every day" is a company claim; check stats.md.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city**, about 5.07 sq mi; population 13,307 (2020). Downtown sits in a small valley on both sides of the Little Miami River, which is the Hamilton/Clermont line; most residential areas are on the surrounding hills. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loveland,_Ohio
- A Loveland mailing address does not mean the home is inside city limits (much of 45140 is Miami Township, Symmes Township etc.). https://lovelandoh.gov/148/Buliding-Zoning ; geocoder: 6300 Branch Hill Guinea Pike, Loveland 45140 is Miami Township, not the city.

## 3. Permits (City of Loveland, all three counties)
- **Building & Zoning**, City Hall, 120 W. Loveland Ave., issues residential building/zoning permits. The residential application has a county box (Hamilton, Clermont, Warren) and an HVAC section (furnace new/replacement, air conditioner, fuel type, new ductwork). https://www.lovelandoh.gov/DocumentCenter/View/965/Residential-Building-Permit-Application
- Apply in person or online (Accela). City adopts the Residential Code of Ohio. https://lovelandoh.gov/148/Buliding-Zoning
- **Electrical:** city contracts with Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). **Plumbing:** city contracts with Hamilton County Public Health "for all plumbing permits". Same source. Whether that covers Clermont and Warren addresses: unverified (page doesn't say).
- Fees, timings: unverified. Don't quote.

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Local historic district:** Loveland is a Certified Local Government with a Historic Preservation and Planning District around historic downtown. Only projects that need a building permit are reviewed by the Historic Preservation and Planning Commission. https://www.lovelandoh.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1753/HPPC-Fact-Sheet---Update-1-16-21 ; design guidelines dated January 25, 2021: https://www.lovelandoh.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1703 . District boundaries and whether an HVAC permit triggers review: unverified. Never claim a placement rule.
- Shield's Crossing (William Johnston House) is individually on the National Register. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loveland,_Ohio
- **Flooding:** downtown has been flood-prone; the March 1913 flood was the worst and washed out the bridge; a dike was built 1962-63 after the 1959 flood. Same source. No NWS flood-stage gauge at Loveland found (USGS 03245000 exists, no NWS thresholds). https://reference.geoconnex.us/collections/gages/items/1015836?f=html . Parcel flood zones: the city permit form asks "flood zone"; check https://msc.fema.gov
