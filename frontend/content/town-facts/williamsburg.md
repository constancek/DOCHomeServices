## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in **Williamsburg Township**, founded 1796 by General William Lytle. 2020 population 2,570. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamsburg,_Ohio
- On the East Fork: USGS gauge 03246500 is "East Fork Little Miami River at Williamsburg OH" (drainage area 237 sq mi). https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/03246500/
- Village offices: 107 West Main Street. https://williamsburgohio.org
- Geocoded: 107 W Main St and 200 E Main St, Williamsburg 45176 = Williamsburg village (16000US3985288), tract 409 (block groups 3 and 4), Williamsburg Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
