# Burlington, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'burlington'): ZIP 41005, Boone County seat, "historic core around the old courthouse has aging pipes", "fast-growing newer subdivisions", "rural homes". The county seat and the historic district are confirmed. The housing data below does not back "aging pipes" or "rural homes" for the CDP as a whole (see section 6).

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP), county seat of Boone County. 2020 census population 17,318 (2010: 15,926, up 8.7%). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- ZIP 41005 covers more than the CDP (about 24,192 people, third-party figure, unverified). Geocoded addresses at 2950 Washington St and 6028 Camp Ernst Rd both fall inside the CDP. https://www.zip-codes.com/zip-code/41005/zip-code-41005.asp ; Census geocoder via census.mjs

## 3. Permits
- Burlington is unincorporated, so building and HVAC permits go through the **Boone County Building Department**, 2950 Washington St, Burlington (state listing names Boone County as local building and HVAC inspector). https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Boone%20County.pdf
- Permits are filed through the county's online portal. Fees on the county HVAC page: don't use in copy. https://boonecountyky.org/departments/building_inspection/hvac.php
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector, office days at 2950 Washington St. Electrical: state listing says contact the County Judge Executive's office; local electrical inspector **unverified**. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Boone%20County.pdf

- **Historic district, verified:** Burlington Historic District, National Register 1979, a 74-acre grid covering the original town; boundary change 2005. https://npgallery.nps.gov/pdfhost/docs/NRHP/Text/79000961.pdf ; https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/AssetDetail/f462a6d5-3464-4265-90e7-625e0ce4bcbb
