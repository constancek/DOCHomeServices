# Northbrook (Colerain Township, OH 45251 / 45231): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives only ZIP 45251. The CDP also takes in 45231. 9100 Colerain Ave geocodes to Northbrook CDP in 45251, and 9500 Pippin Rd geocodes to Northbrook CDP in 45231. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mostly mid-century houses" is loose. The median build year is 1970, and 49.2% of units date from 1960-79, against 25.9% from 1940-59. "1960s and 1970s homes" fits better. See Housing.
- "Made up largely of single-family homes" is roughly right (71.2% detached), but 17.7% of units are in buildings of 5 or more. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3956280/
- "On the northwest side" only works if it means north-west of Cincinnati. The CDP lies around Colerain Ave and Pippin Rd, on the township's east side, beside Springfield Township (New Burlington CDP). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- The claims about corrosion leaks and failing water heaters being the most common problems are unverified.

## Where it is
- Northbrook is a census-designated place in Colerain Township, Hamilton County, and is unincorporated. It covers 1.93 sq mi, all land, at about 817 ft elevation. 2020 Census population 10,912. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northbrook,_Ohio
- Neighbours confirmed by geocoder: 2800 Springdale Rd is in Northgate CDP (Colerain Twp), and 9900 Hamilton Ave is in New Burlington CDP (Springfield Twp). Other borders are unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits
- **Zoning first:** Colerain Township Planning & Zoning (4200 Springdale Rd) issues zoning certificates. Hamilton County won't accept a building permit application until the township approves zoning. Most projects need a certificate. Whether like-for-like HVAC or water heater swaps need one is unverified. https://www.colerain.org/197/Development ; https://www.colerain.org/DocumentCenter/View/4891/Zoning-FAQ
- **Building permits:** Hamilton County Planning + Development, Division of Buildings + Inspections, for the unincorporated townships, Colerain included. The county page returned 403 this session, so this is confirmed by search snippet only. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/buildings_and_inspections
- **HVAC/mechanical:** One third-party guide says the county division issues mechanical permits, including for like-for-like furnace and AC swaps. It isn't an official source, so this is unverified. https://permitmint.com/permits/ohio/hamilton-county-oh/hvac/
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). From _shared.md. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- Fees and timings are unverified. The township's zoning documents give both 5 and 10 business days.

## Utilities
- Duke Energy delivers power and handles outages. Since 2012 the township's electric aggregation has used Dynegy (Vistra), with an opt-out. The listed rate ran through the June 2026 meter read, so current terms are unverified. https://www.colerain.org/216/Electric-Aggregation
- Gas aggregation runs through AEP Energy (term through October 2026). https://www.colerain.org/234/Natural-Gas-Aggregation
