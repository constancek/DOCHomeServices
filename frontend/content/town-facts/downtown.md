# Downtown Cincinnati (Central Business District, OH 45202): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` lists only Over-the-Rhine and West End as adjacent. The city's boundaries also put Pendleton (north), Queensgate (west, across I-75) and Mount Adams (east, across Eggleston Ave) next to Downtown. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Cincinnati (it cites the city's Downtown 2020 Statistical Neighborhood Approximation PDF)
- ZIP 45202 is much bigger than Downtown. It has 17,869 residents and 11,940 housing units (ACS 2020-2024) and also takes in OTR, Pendleton and Mount Adams. Don't use ZIP-level numbers as if they were Downtown numbers. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45202-45202/

## Where it is
- A City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County. Its boundaries are Central Parkway to the north, Plum Street to the northwest, I-75 to the west and Eggleston Avenue to the east, with the Ohio River to the south. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Cincinnati
- The 2020 Census counted 5,835 residents (city statistical approximation). https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Downtown_2020.pdf (the city server refused connections, so I took the figure from the search snippet and Wikipedia. Treat it as unverified until someone opens the PDF.)
- The Census geocoder puts Fountain Square (520 Vine) and 600 Broadway in Tract 7, and City Hall (801 Plum) and Great American Ball Park in Tract 265. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/address

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Tracts 7 + 265, Hamilton County)
These two tracts are a close proxy for Downtown, not an exact match. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061000700-census-tract-7-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061026500-census-tract-265-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1953 in Tract 7 and 1977 in Tract 265.
- Of 4,821 units, 2,089 (43.3%) were built in 1939 or earlier, and 1,454 (30.2%) were built in 2010 or later. Very little was built in between.
- Of 4,821 units, 3,453 (71.6%) are in buildings of 50 or more units. Only 70 are single-family detached.
- Tenure: 3,739 of 4,284 occupied units (87.3%) are rented.
- Heating fuel: 2,981 of 4,284 occupied units (69.6%) heat with electricity and 1,111 with utility gas. Tract 7 is 65.4% electric and Tract 265 is 73.0%.
- Margins of error are large at tract level.

## Permits
- Work at Downtown addresses is permitted by the City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections. A mechanical permit is needed to install or replace HVAC equipment. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections (carried over from the Sedamsville facts file because the city site would not load. Fees and timings are unverified.)

## Utility
- Electric: Duke Energy Ohio, which serves Cincinnati and southwest Ohio. https://news.duke-energy.com/releases/duke-energy-restores-more-than-171-000-outages-across-greater-cincinnati-repairs-continue-as-company-works-to-determine-restoration-times
- District cooling: CoolCo holds a city franchise for chilled water in the CBD and serves offices, hotels and government buildings, including the convention center. Source is trade press, so treat it as unverified: https://irei.com/news/harrison-street-acquires-coolco/
- Unverified: whether any Downtown residential buildings are on CoolCo.

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG 1991-2020 normals; CVG is in Boone County KY, about 13 miles from Downtown)
- January normal high 39.6F, normal low 23.2F. https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jan.pdf
- July normal high 86.0F, normal low 65.9F. https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf
- On average 21 days a year reach 90F or higher (1981-2010). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
- Unverified: any urban heat island figure for Downtown. None was found from NWS.

## Other local factors
- Historic districts: Ninth Street Historic District (Plum to Vine, NR listed 1980), West Fourth Street (NR 1976) and East Fourth Street (NR 1988). National Register listing alone does not trigger city design review. Local designation is unverified. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/national-register-of-historic-places/nr-districts/ ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/local-historic-landmarks
- Flood protection: the city's Local Flood Protection Project (Mill Creek Barrier Dam, a 1.5-mile floodwall along Mehring Way to Linn St, and 14 floodgates) closes at 52 ft river stage. It sits on the west edge of Downtown. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/stormwater/flood-management/
- Unverified: FEMA flood zone status of individual riverfront (The Banks) lots.
