# Mount Lookout (Cincinnati, OH 45226): verified local facts (shared by all services)

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls Mount Lookout a "hilltop neighborhood". Wikipedia says only that it overlooks the Ohio River valley, and Mount Lookout Square itself sits low, in the drainage path along Delta and Linwood Avenues (see Flooding below). Use "hillside" or "hilly" rather than "hilltop" for the whole neighborhood. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Lookout,_Cincinnati
- The "early-1900s homes" wording holds up: the median year built is 1938 (Census, below).

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 city neighborhoods, in Hamilton County, northwest of Linwood. It was first a village called Delta, annexed by the city in 1870, and named for the Cincinnati Observatory, which moved there in 1873. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Lookout,_Cincinnati
- ZIP 45226 (per `locations.ts`). 2020 Census population: 5,173. The city's Statistical Neighborhood Approximation uses Census Tract 48 plus part of Tract 47.03. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/resources/census/2020 ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/MtLookout_2020.pdf (that city PDF could not be opened during research, so the tract split comes from a search summary and is unverified)

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 48, the core of the neighborhood)
Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061004800-census-tract-48-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040)
- Median year built: 1938. 740 of 1,454 units (50.9%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 1950-1969 adds 367 units (25.2%). Only 84 units (5.8%) date from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 1,140 single-family detached (78.4%), 15 attached, 21 two-family, 99 in 3-4 unit buildings, 174 in 5-9 unit buildings.
- Tenure: 1,038 of 1,401 occupied units owner-occupied (74.1%), 363 rented.
- Heating fuel: 1,024 utility gas (73.1%), 330 electricity (23.6%), 35 propane, 12 other.
- Median owner-occupied home value: $682,500.

## Permits
- Mount Lookout is inside the City of Cincinnati, so the City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues mechanical, plumbing and electrical permits. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
- Fees, timings and phone numbers are unverified. Don't quote them.

## Utility
- Electric: Duke Energy Ohio (serves all of the City of Cincinnati). https://www.duke-energy.com/home
- Sewer: Metropolitan Sewer District of Greater Cincinnati (MSD); the area is on a combined sewer system (Fox19, below).

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)
- July normal high 86F, normal low 65-66F (1991-2020 normals). https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf ; https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- On average 21 days a year reach 90F or higher (1981-2010 normals). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s

## Flooding and sewer backups (changes plumbing, basement and electrical jobs)
- Flash flooding hit Mount Lookout on 17 July 2026 and again around 21 July. Mount Lookout Square sits in a low-lying drainage path along Delta and Linwood Avenues, open to both surface runoff and combined-sewer surcharges. https://hoodline.com/2026/08/mount-lookout-tattoo-shop-reopens-after-flooding-hit-twice-in-july/ (news source; the page did not load during research, so the details come from a search summary and the dates are unverified)
- Fox19 (13 Oct 2026) names Mount Lookout with Hyde Park and Oakley as the East Side neighborhoods hit hardest by the July 2026 flooding and sewer backups. MSD approved 1,200 properties for cleaning, at an estimated cost of more than $5 million. The city's plan has no timeline or budget yet. https://www.fox19.com/2026/10/13/we-need-lot-more-answers-officials-present-plan-address-cincinnati-flooding/
- An earlier storm on 30 June (2020, during COVID) flooded at least one Mount Lookout home. MSD classed it as "overland flooding" rather than a sewer backup and logged 244 backups citywide that day. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/mount-lookout/msd-denies-responsibility-for-flooding-that-damaged-mount-lookout-womans-home

## Other local factors
- Development review: after a Hyde Park demolition outcry (article undated), the city required City Planning Commission approval for home demolitions in Hyde Park and Mount Lookout for 90 days, and it is studying a Neighborhood Conservation Overlay. It is not in force (unverified current status). This affects demolitions, not HVAC or plumbing work. https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/hyde-park/after-outcry-over-hyde-park-demolition-city-planners-begin-preservation-study
- No local historic district was confirmed for Mount Lookout. The Observatory Historic District (National Register, 1978) is listed by Wikipedia as Hyde Park. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observatory_Historic_District
