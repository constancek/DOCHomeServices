# Florence, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'florence'): ZIP 41042, "Boone County’s largest city", mid-century neighborhoods plus newer subdivisions around the Florence Mall area. The ACS data below backs the mid-century part strongly. "Fastest-growing in Northern Kentucky" is unverified; leave it out.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated home rule-class city with a mayor-council government, in Boone County. 2020 census population: 31,946. Mailing ZIPs 41042 and 41022. About 12 miles from downtown Cincinnati. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- ZIP 41042 covers much more than the city (about 23,700 housing units vs 13,877 in the city), so ZIP-level data mixes in newer unincorporated Boone County subdivisions. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41042-41042/
- "Largest city in Boone County": unverified (likely, not confirmed from a primary source).

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, City of Florence, place 2128900)
Source: Census tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2128900-florence-ky/ (pulled from the Census Reporter API, release acs2024_5yr)
- Median year built: **1972** (ZIP 41042: 1988; Boone County: 1996).
- Of 13,877 units: 1970-79: 2,812 (20.3%); 1960-69: 2,153 (15.5%); 1950-59: 1,899 (13.7%); 1940-49: 821; 1939 or earlier: 1,568 (11.3%); 1980-89: 1,383; 1990-99: 1,779; 2000-09: 1,004; 2010 or later: 458. **66.7% built before 1980**; only 23.4% built 1990 or later.
- The 1939-or-earlier count looks high for Florence; margin of error not checked, so don't quote it alone.
- Home types: 7,481 single-family detached (53.9%), 592 attached (4.3%), 5,579 in 2+ unit buildings (40.2%), 225 mobile homes (1.6%). Lots of apartments and condos.
- Tenure: 6,302 owner-occupied of 12,906 occupied (48.8%); renters 51.2%.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 6,305 (48.9%), **electricity 6,264 (48.5%)**, propane 205 (1.6%). ACS does not split heat pumps from electric furnaces or baseboard.
- ZIP 41042 for comparison: electricity 11,967 of 22,807 (52.5%), gas 45.1%; 47.4% of units built 1990 or later. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41042-41042/

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits for the City of Florence go through the **Boone County Building Department** (state listing: "Boone County (Includes cities of Florence, Union, Walton)"), 2950 Washington St, Burlington, KY 41005. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Boone%20County.pdf (updated 7/30/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspectors, office at the Boone County Health Center, 7505 Burlington Pike, Florence. Same source.
- Electrical: the state listing only says to contact the County Judge Executive’s office or the city office where work is done. Which office issues electrical permits in Florence is **unverified**.
- County HVAC fee figures ($105 residential) come from a search snippet of https://boonecountyky.org/departments/building_inspection/hvac.php; site blocked direct fetch, so **unverified**. Don't use fees in copy anyway.

## 4. Electric utility
- Florence is served by **both Duke Energy Kentucky and Owen Electric Cooperative**; both bid on and received non-exclusive city franchises in late 2025. Which utility serves a given street is **unverified**. https://psc.ky.gov/Case/ViewCaseFilings/2025-00334 and https://psc.ky.gov/Case/ViewCaseFilings/2025-00336 and https://psc.ky.gov/tariffs/Electric/Owen%20Electric%20Cooperative,%20Inc/Franchise%20Agreements/City%20of%20Florence/2025-12-16.pdf
- Say "Duke Energy Kentucky or Owen Electric, depending on the street", never one alone.

## 5. Climate
- Cincinnati’s official weather station is at CVG, also in Boone County (exact distance from Florence unverified).
- CVG July average high 85.7°F and about 22 days a year at 90°F or above, calculated from 1991-2020 CVG daily data (NOAA RCC-ACIS, https://data.rcc-acis.org, station CVG); not published normals (same figures as hebron.md). NWS Wilmington lists the CVG July normal high as 86.0°F. https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
