# Groesbeck (Colerain Township, OH): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says Groesbeck is "made up largely of mid-century single-family homes". That's only partly right. The median build year is 1970, and 59.0% of units date from 1940-79. But 16.3% were built in 2000 or later, 23.1% are in buildings with 5+ units, 225 are mobile homes and 33.9% are rented. Single-family detached homes make up 67.7%. See the housing block. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3932536/
- ZIP 45239 is right for 3100 W Galbraith Rd. Whether parts of the CDP use 45251 is unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- The claims that corrosion leaks and failing water heaters are "the emergencies we handle most" are unverified. The `relevance` text also has an em dash.

## Where it is
- Unincorporated census-designated place in Colerain Township, Hamilton County. It's 2.93 sq mi, and the 2020 Census population was 7,365. It was founded as West Union and renamed in 1857 after William S. Groesbeck. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groesbeck,_Ohio
- 3100 W Galbraith Rd geocodes to Groesbeck CDP, tract 207.05, Colerain Township, ZIP 45239. Nearby addresses fall in neighbouring CDPs: 9200 Colerain Ave is in Northbrook CDP, 8100 Colerain Ave and 3000 Banning Rd are in White Oak CDP, 8800 Pippin Rd is in Skyline Acres CDP and 3400 Springdale Rd is in Northgate CDP. Whether each one borders Groesbeck is unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ (via census.mjs)

## Permits
- **Zoning first:** Colerain Township Planning & Zoning (4200 Springdale Rd) issues zoning certificates, and they're required for most projects, including remodels and additions. Hamilton County won't accept a building application until the township has approved zoning. Applications go through OpenGov. Whether an AC or furnace swap needs a zoning certificate is unverified. https://www.colerain.org/DocumentCenter/View/4885/Zoning-Certificates ; https://www.colerain.org/197/Development
- **Building permits:** Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections issues building permits in the unincorporated townships. This comes from _shared.md and a search snippet, because the county site returned 403. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health covers the county "excluding the City of Cincinnati". Colerain isn't named on the page. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/ (from _shared.md)
- **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). From _shared.md, not re-checked for Colerain.
- **HVAC/mechanical:** unverified. Only a third-party permit site says the county issues them, so don't use it.

## Terrain, flood, historic
