# North Avondale (Cincinnati, OH 45229): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` and the `-parts` copy put North Avondale's homes "near Rockdale Avenue". Rockdale Ave geocodes to tracts 66, 68 and 69, which are Avondale, not North Avondale. Use Reading Road, Rose Hill Ave or Clinton Springs Ave instead. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006600-census-tract-66-hamilton-oh/
- "Grand early-1900s homes" is only partly right: 64.8% of units predate 1940 and 35% are in buildings of 5 or more units (see housing). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006502-census-tract-6502-hamilton-oh/
- The entry has no neighbour list. Confirmed neighbours: Avondale and Evanston. Paddock Hills shares tract 65.02 (Paddock Hills Ln geocodes there). Others are unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Cincinnati ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Cincinnati

## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, Hamilton County, ZIP 45229 (shared with Avondale and Paddock Hills). Home to Xavier University (partly) and Avon Woods Preserve. 2020 Census: 3,405 residents, 1,735 housing units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Avondale,_Cincinnati
- Census geocoder: 3920 Rose Hill Ave, 820 Clinton Springs Ave, 3815 and 4300 Reading Rd sit in tract 65.02; 4000 Reading Rd, 940 Lenox Pl, 3900 Winding Way and Xavier (3800 Victory Pkwy) in tract 65.01. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, tracts 65.01 and 65.02)
Sources: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006501-census-tract-6501-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061006502-census-tract-6502-hamilton-oh/ (tables B25034, B25035, B25024, B25003, B25040, B26001). Tract 65.02 includes Paddock Hills, so treat it as a close proxy. Tract 65.01 has 1,599 people in group quarters (Xavier dorms), so use housing-unit figures, not population.
- 2,344 housing units, 2,151 occupied.
- Median year built: 1938 in both tracts.
- Era: 1,519 (64.8%) built 1939 or earlier, 299 (12.8%) 1940-59, 463 (19.8%) 1960-79, 24 (1.0%) 1980-99, 39 (1.7%) 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 1,052 single-family detached (44.9%), 10 attached, 276 two-family (11.8%), 35 in 3-4 unit buildings, 620 in 5-49 unit buildings, 351 in 50+ unit buildings (15.0%).
- Tenure: 1,338 owner-occupied (62.2%), 813 rented (37.8%).
- Heating fuel: utility gas 1,267 (58.9%), electricity 662 (30.8%), other fuel 201, no fuel 21. Electric share: 18.5% in 65.01, 34.9% in 65.02.

## Historic district
- City Council approved a North Avondale local historic district 8-1 in August 2026. It covers part of the neighbourhood (370 properties, 33 non-contributing). Exterior alterations and new construction on contributing buildings need Historic Conservation Office design review. Styles include Queen Anne, Shingle, Richardsonian Romanesque, Prairie and Craftsman. https://www.wvxu.org/politics/2026-08-04/north-avondale-local-historic-district-cincinnati
