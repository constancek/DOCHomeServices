# Addyston, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'addyston'):**
- "Century-old worker homes" overstates the age. Only 28.9% of homes predate 1940 and the median home was built in 1965 (section 2). The pre-1930 company houses are real but concentrated in the historic district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_of_Addyston_Historic_District
- "Far west side" reads as a Cincinnati neighborhood. Addyston is a separate incorporated village in Miami Township, not part of the city. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addyston,_Ohio
- Housing is mostly rented: 61.1% of occupied units are rentals and 27.0% of units sit in buildings of 5+ units (section 2). Copy that assumes owner-occupied single-family homes misses that.
- "High water pushing backups into basements" and "closely spaced" homes: unverified, no source found.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** (1891) in Miami Township, Hamilton County, on the Ohio River west of Cincinnati. ZIP 45001. About 0.90 sq mi (0.85 sq mi land). US 50 runs through it for about two miles. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addyston,_Ohio
- Population 927 (2020 census). Same source.
- Founded as a company town around Addyston Pipe and Steel (1889); the company closed in 1950. Same source.
- Law enforcement moved to the Hamilton County Sheriff in November 2024 (village council vote). Same source.

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Village of Addyston Historic District**, National Register, listed September 13, 1991: 384 buildings, nearly all of the village, mostly small vernacular houses built before 1930 (Main, Sekitan, Church, First, Second Streets). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_of_Addyston_Historic_District
- That is a National Register listing; no local design review found. Any rule on outdoor equipment placement: unverified. Same source.
- **Ohio River flooding.** NWS gauge Ohio River at Cincinnati: action 40 ft, minor flood 52 ft, moderate 56 ft, major 65 ft. NWS impact at 70 ft: "Downstream of Cincinnati, most property between Route 50 and the river is flooded." Record crest 80 ft, January 26, 1937. https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/CCNO1 (data behind https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1)
- Recent crests at that gauge: 60.91 ft (April 7, 2025), 55.46 ft (February 19, 2025), 56.86 ft (March 4, 2021). Same source. Which Addyston streets flood at which stage: unverified.
- The 1937 flood devastated the village. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addyston,_Ohio
- Addyston participates in the National Flood Insurance Program; flood map effective date 02/16/12 (from a FEMA community status listing, seen in search results only: unverified). https://www.fema.gov/cis/OH.pdf . Check individual parcels at https://msc.fema.gov
