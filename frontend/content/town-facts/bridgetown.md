## Where it is
- Bridgetown CDP: unincorporated Green Township, Hamilton County, Ohio. It is not a city or village and has no building department of its own. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Area 4.31 sq mi, all land. 2020 Census population 14,731. State Route 264 (Bridgetown Road) runs through the middle. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgetown,_Ohio
- Geocoded addresses: 5500 Bridgetown Rd (tract 210.01, 45248), 3900 Race Rd (tract 210.01, 45211), 5900 Harrison Ave (tract 206.04, 45248), all in Bridgetown CDP, Green Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits (unincorporated Green Township)
- Building permits: Hamilton County Planning + Development, Buildings + Inspections, which issues building permits in the unincorporated townships. Applications go through the ezTrak portal (fully digital since 18 February 2025). The county page returned 403 this session, so this is confirmed by search snippet only. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/planning_and_development/buildings_and_inspections/index.php ; https://eztrak.cagis.org/
- Electrical permits and inspections: Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI) (from _shared.md and the county page snippet). https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/buildings_and_inspections
- Plumbing permits: Hamilton County Public Health, which covers the county outside the City of Cincinnati (from _shared.md, covedale.md). https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- HVAC/mechanical permits: probably Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections, but only third-party permit guides say so. Unverified, don't name an office for HVAC in copy.
- Zoning: Green Township zones only the north-east part of the township. Hamilton County zones the rest, and a zoning certificate usually has to come before a building permit. Whether Bridgetown sits inside the north-east zoning area is unverified. https://www.greentwp.org/northeast-green-township-zoning-code ; https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/government/departments/planning_and_development/development_process.php
