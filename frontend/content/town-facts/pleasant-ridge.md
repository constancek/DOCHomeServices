## Where it is
- Pleasant Ridge is one of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County on the city's northeast side, centred on Montgomery Road and Ridge Avenue. It incorporated as a village in 1891 and Cincinnati annexed it in 1912. The 2020 Census counted 8,895 residents and 4,636 housing units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleasant_Ridge,_Cincinnati (city PDF not reachable, so treat these as unverified against city sources)
- The Census geocoder places 6000, 6100 and 6400-6600 Montgomery Rd, 5600-6200 Ridge Ave, 3100 Woodford Rd, 6200 Grand Vista Ave and 2900 Losantiville Ave in ZIP 45213, in Tracts 57.01, 57.02 and 58. 5800 Montgomery Rd returns ZIP 45212. 6500 Ridge Ave falls in Tract 233, which is outside the neighbourhood. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/onelineaddress
- Tract 57.01 lies entirely inside Cincinnati. Only 69.8% of Tract 57.02 and 82.7% of Tract 58 are inside the city, so they also take in parts of neighbouring jurisdictions. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005702-census-tract-5702-hamilton-oh/ ; https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061005800-census-tract-58-hamilton-oh/
