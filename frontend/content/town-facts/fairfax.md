## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, incorporated 1955 to head off annexation by Cincinnati; left Columbia Township in 2010 by forming a paper township. About 0.76-0.78 sq mi. 2020 population 1,768. ZIP 45227. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfax,_Ohio
- Bordered by Cincinnati on the north, west and south (Madisonville, Hyde Park, Mount Lookout, Linwood), Mariemont to the east and south, and Columbia Township. Same source.
- Village offices: 5903 Hawthorne Ave. https://www.fairfaxoh.com/building-department/

## 3. Permits (Village of Fairfax)
- **Zoning Certificate first:** for any project, whether or not a county permit is needed, apply first to the village **Zoning Officer** for a Zoning Certificate. https://www.fairfaxoh.com/building-department/ ; https://www.fairfaxoh.com/download/building_department/Zoning-Application.pdf
- **Building permits and inspections:** the village contracts with the **Hamilton County Building Department** for residential and commercial permits and inspections, except fences and sheds of a certain size. https://www.fairfaxoh.com/building-inspector/ ; https://www.fairfaxoh.com/building-department/

## 6. Local factors that change the job
- **Little Duck Creek** runs through the village, into Duck Creek and then the Little Miami River. It splits the village into Lower and Upper Fairfax. **Lower Fairfax** (low-lying, northeast residential area) has often flooded in heavy rain, with multiple deaths; the village bought and demolished homes there with federal funds. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfax,_Ohio
- **July 17, 2026 flash flood:** several inches of rain in minutes overwhelmed sewers and Duck Creek; basements flooded; more than two dozen homes reported damage; a Warren Avenue resident died (neighbors said he was working on a sump pump; cause under coroner investigation, unverified). Village said the culvert worked as designed. 2001 flood killed two residents in their basement. https://www.fox19.com/2026/07/21/fairfax-village-board-addresses-deadly-flash-flooding-says-infrastructure-worked-designed/ ; https://www.fox19.com/2026/07/20/coroner-ids-fairfax-man-who-died-after-flash-flooding/ ; https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/father-and-daughter-die-in-flash-flood-25-years-ago-in-fairfax-home
- **Architectural Review Districts:** Red Bank Road and Wooster Pike; properties there get extra zoning review and may need Planning Commission review. Whether outdoor equipment counts: unverified. https://www.fairfaxoh.com/building-department/
- No historic district found. FEMA flood zone for any parcel: check https://msc.fema.gov (unverified per address).
