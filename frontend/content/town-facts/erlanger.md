# Erlanger, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'erlanger'): ZIP 41018, Kenton County city near the airport, "settled mid-century neighborhoods" with older and newer homes. The ACS data below supports "mid-century" (48.5% of units built 1950-1979).
**Errors in locations.ts:** `adjacent` lists only Florence. Erlanger's Kenton County neighbors such as Elsmere (which shares ZIP 41018), Crescent Springs, Lakeside Park, Villa Hills and Edgewood are missing; whether Erlanger actually borders Florence is unverified. The entry also says "7 days a week", which the brief bans in copy.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated city in Kenton County, Kentucky. 2020 census population 19,611; 8.4 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlanger,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- ACS 2020-2024 population estimate: 19,875. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2125300-erlanger-ky/
- ZIP 41018 covers more than the city (12,121 housing units vs 8,180 in the city) and also carries Elsmere addresses, so ZIP data mixes in Elsmere. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41018-41018/
- The Cherry Hill neighborhood is reported as partly in Boone County: **unverified**. If true, those addresses would fall under Boone County offices, not PDS. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlanger,_Kentucky

## 2. Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Erlanger city, place 16000US2125300)
Source: Census tables B25035, B25034, B25024, B25003, B25040 via https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2125300-erlanger-ky/ (Census Reporter API, release acs2024_5yr)
- Median year built: **1976** (ZIP 41018: 1974; Kenton County: 1974, same release).
- Of 8,180 units: 1970-79: 1,650 (20.2%); 1960-69: 1,160 (14.2%); 1950-59: 1,154 (14.1%); 1940-49: 132; 1939 or earlier: 676 (8.3%); 1980-89: 715; 1990-99: 1,226 (15.0%); 2000-09: 923; 2010-19: 413; 2020 or later: 131. **58.3% built before 1980**; 32.9% built 1990 or later.
- Home types: 5,261 single-family detached (64.3%), 402 attached (4.9%), 2,283 in 2+ unit buildings (27.9%, of which 982 in 10-19 unit buildings), 222 mobile homes (2.7%).
- Tenure: 5,398 owner-occupied of 7,792 occupied (69.3%); renters 30.7%.
- Heating fuel: utility gas 4,393 (56.4%), **electricity 3,062 (39.3%)**, propane 111 (1.4%), fuel oil 88 (1.1%), no fuel 96. ACS does not split heat pumps from electric furnaces or baseboard.
- ZIP 41018 for comparison: electricity 4,935 of 11,441 (43.1%), gas 51.1%; 60.6% built before 1980. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41018-41018/

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits for Erlanger are handled by **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Erlanger by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector, office at the Boone County Health Center, 7505 Burlington Pike, Florence. Same source.
- Electrical: the state list only says to contact the County Judge Executive's office or the city office where work is done. Who issues electrical permits in Erlanger is **unverified**. Same source.
- Kentucky requires a permit for replacing a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
