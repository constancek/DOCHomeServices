## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city in Campbell County, KY. 2020 population 6,662; 2.62 sq mi. ZIPs 41076 and 41099. Fort Thomas to the north, Wilder to the west, Cold Spring to the south. Northern Kentucky University has been here since 1971. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highland_Heights,_Kentucky

## 3. Permits
- The state inspector list names Campbell County (Planning, Zoning and Building Inspection, 1098 Monmouth St, Suite 343, Newport) as the local building and local HVAC office for Highland Heights. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Campbell%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- The city's own site says Campbell County Planning and Zoning issues permits for the city (search summary only; hhky.com did not load, so treat as supporting, not primary). https://hhky.com/?p=8846

## 4. Terrain, flood, historic
- No river frontage (all land; landlocked between Fort Thomas, Wilder and Cold Spring). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highland_Heights,_Kentucky
- NFIP participating community (first FIRM 03/03/2014). https://www.fema.gov/cis/KY.html
- Regional hillside slide risk (clay soils over Kope shale), not city specific. https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1032/pdf/Kentucky.pdf
