## Where it is
- Northside is a City of Cincinnati neighbourhood in Hamilton County, ZIP 45223. It was first called Cumminsville and was annexed by the city in 1873. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northside,_Cincinnati (to locate the source; verify the annexation date with the city before using it)
- It borders Clifton, Mount Airy, Spring Grove Village, College Hill and Westwood. Hamilton Avenue and Spring Grove Avenue are the main roads. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northside,_Cincinnati
- 2020 Census population: 8,096. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northside,_Cincinnati (the city's 2020 PDF would not load)

## Permits
- City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections issues Northside permits. All new and replacement plumbing needs a permit and inspections, and Ohio-licensed plumbers must do the work. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/plumbing-inspections/
- A B&I mechanical permit covers installing or replacing an air conditioner or furnace. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/
