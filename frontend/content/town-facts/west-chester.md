# West Chester, OH: verified local facts (AC repair pilot)

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated **township** (not a city), one of 13 townships in Butler County, run by a 3-member board of trustees. Called Union Township until voters approved the rename on March 7, 2000 (in effect June 28, 2000). 35.6 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Chester_Township,_Butler_County,_Ohio
- About 18 miles north of Cincinnati, in the southeast corner of Butler County. Borders Liberty Twp (N), Mason and Deerfield Twp (E, Warren Co.), Sharonville (SE), Springdale (SW), Fairfield (W) and Fairfield Twp (NW). (same source)
- ZIPs: mainly 45069. The southeast corner uses 45241 (Sharonville PO), and edges use 45014 (Fairfield) and 45011/45015 (Hamilton). (same source)
- I-75 exits 19, 21 and 22 serve the township. Landmarks: The Square at Union Centre (library, Verdin bell tower), Voice of America MetroPark (former 625-acre Bethany Relay Station), Keehner Park. (same source)
- Population 66,973 (ACS 2024 1-yr); median household income $102,099; median owner-occupied home value $372,300. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/06000US3901783150-west-chester-township-butler-county-oh/

## 3. What that means for AC (reasonable pro claims, tied to data above)
- Most homes are 25–55 years old. Any original condenser is long gone, so many are on their second or third outdoor unit, often still running on the original ductwork and line set.
- Systems put in before 2010 often use R-22. US production and import of R-22 ended January 1, 2020, so a leak repair costs more and replacement is often the better call. https://www.epa.gov/ods-phaseout/homeowners-and-consumers
- 42.3% of homes heat with electricity. That points to a large heat-pump population, where one outdoor unit does both heating and cooling. Note: the Census does not separate heat pumps from electric resistance heat, so do not quote a heat-pump percentage.
- About 1 in 5 homes are attached or in 5+ unit buildings. Expect condos and townhomes with tight closet air handlers and condensers grouped in shared spaces.

## 4. Permits
- Two steps: (1) the West Chester Township Community Development Dept. issues a zoning certificate, (513) 777-4214. (2) **Butler County Building & Zoning Department** (130 High St, Hamilton; (513) 887-3205) issues the building permit and enforces building/electrical codes. Turnaround 3–7 working days. https://cms5.revize.com/revize/butleroh/Document_Center/Development/Building%20&%20Zoning/Procedures%20&%20Applications/RESIDENTIAL%20PROCEDURES%202024.pdf and https://www.westchesteroh.org/government/community-development/planning-zoning/permit-applications-plan-submissions
- **Unverified:** whether a like-for-like AC swap needs a county permit. The county procedures mention a Mechanical System Description Form for remodels and alterations but do not cover equipment replacement. Confirm with the county before publishing. (Both sites returned 403/404 to direct fetch, so the details come from search snippets.)

## 5. Utility
- **Duke Energy Ohio** owns and maintains the electric system (township FAQ). The township runs an opt-out electric/gas aggregation program; new residents are not auto-enrolled. https://www.westchesteroh.org/government/general-government/frequently-asked-questions
- **Rebates: unverified.** I found no current Duke Energy Ohio page offering a residential AC or heat-pump rebate. Aggregator sites conflict. Do not mention a rebate.

## 6. Climate
- Normal July high at Cincinnati (CVG) is 86°F, normal low about 66°F (NWS ILN). https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climate_summary/ClimateReport_June2021.pdf
- Cincinnati averages **21 days a year at 90°F or above** (NWS, 1981–2010 normals; I could not find a 1991–2020 figure). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
- Nothing town-specific verified. West Chester is upland suburban, not river valley. Mill Creek starts in West Chester and runs south along I-75 (radio news source, flood risk is downstream in Evendale/Sharonville). https://700wlw.iheart.com/content/2020-05-19-major-flooding-today-along-the-mill-creek/
