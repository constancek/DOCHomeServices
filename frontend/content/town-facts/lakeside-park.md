# Lakeside Park, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'lakeside-park'): ZIP 41017, "small, settled Kenton County city", "largely mid-century single-family homes on quiet streets".
**Errors in locations.ts:** "mid-century" is roughly right (median 1964) but leaves out that 17.5% of units were built in 1939 or earlier, the oldest stock of the five ZIP 41017 cities checked, and that 14.9% sit in 5+ unit buildings. No `adjacent` list (Fort Mitchell, Crestview Hills, Edgewood and Erlanger are nearby; borders **unverified**). "Every day, including Saturdays and Sundays" is an hours claim the brief keeps out of body copy.

## 1. Where it is
- City in Kenton County on the Dixie Highway (US 25/42), about five miles south of Covington. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/88000146 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeside_Park,_Kentucky
- Census geocoder places 9 Buttermilk Pike (city building) and 50 Arcadia Ave in Lakeside Park city (place 2143606), tract 646. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 41017 is shared with Villa Hills, Crescent Springs, Crestview Hills, Edgewood and Fort Mitchell. Same geocoder results.

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits: **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Lakeside Park by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector, office at the Boone County Health Center, 7505 Burlington Pike, Florence. Same source (state plumbing inspections, from _shared.md).
- Electrical: the state list only says to contact the County Judge Executive's office or the city office where work is done. Ludlow's site describes PDS as a one-stop shop that includes electrical permits, but who issues electrical permits in Lakeside Park is **unverified**. https://www.ludlow.org/government/departments/code-enforcement.aspx
- Kentucky requires a permit for replacing a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
- Fees and timings: **unverified**, don't use.

## 5. Historic district
- Dixie Highway Historic District: five large post-WWI houses at 2696, 2698, 2700, 2708 and 2712 Dixie Highway, period of significance 1900-1924, listed on the National Register 1988-02-19 (ref 88000146). https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/88000146
