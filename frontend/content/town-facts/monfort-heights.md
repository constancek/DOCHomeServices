# Monfort Heights (Green Township, OH 45247 / 45211 / 45239): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` calls the housing "largely mid-century single-family homes" and says "most homes date to the mid-1900s". The Census says otherwise: median year built 1975, only 23.9% from 1940-59, and 23.4% built in 2000 or later. Single-family detached is 71.4%, and 15.7% of units are in buildings of 5+ units. "Mid-century" is wrong for the CDP as a whole. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3951212/
- `zip: '45247'` is only part of the picture. The Census Geocoder matches addresses inside the CDP to three ZIPs: 5200 North Bend Rd and 3900 West Fork Rd are 45247, 3500 West Fork Rd is 45211, and 5400 Edalbert Dr is 45239. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- The claims about sewer laterals backing up and corroded supply lines are unverified (no local source).

## Where it is
- Census-designated place in unincorporated Green Township, Hamilton County. It runs along North Bend Road and centres on the North Bend Rd / West Fork Rd intersection, about 9 miles north-west of downtown Cincinnati. 2020 Census population 12,070. Area 5.93 sq mi. Elevation 925 ft. Source: Wikipedia citing Census DP1 and USGS GNIS. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monfort_Heights,_Ohio
- Geocoded points fall in tracts 208.02, 208.11 and 208.12, all in Monfort Heights CDP, Green Township. 5900 Lawrence Rd is in Bridgetown CDP instead. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Neighbouring places: unverified (none checked by geocoding apart from Bridgetown).
