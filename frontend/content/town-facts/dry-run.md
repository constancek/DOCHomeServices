# Dry Run (Anderson Township, OH 45244): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` describes "mid-century and newer single-family homes". That is wrong for most of Dry Run. The median build year is 1986, only 8.3% of homes date from before 1960, and 63.2% were built in 1980 or later (see Housing). Call it a 1970s-2000s suburb. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3922674/
- "Older houses... see corrosion leaks" has no source. Almost no housing here dates from before 1940 (0.2%). Treat it as unverified. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3922674/
- ZIP 45244 is confirmed for the Clough Pike addresses. Whether parts of the CDP use 45255 is unverified. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Where it is
- Dry Run is a census-designated place in unincorporated Anderson Township, Hamilton County. It covers 4.8 sq mi, all land, and had 7,672 residents in the 2020 Census. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_Run,_Ohio
- 7800, 8000 and 8400 Clough Pike geocode to Dry Run CDP, Anderson Township, ZIP 45244, tract 251.01. 7400 Clough Pike falls in Turpin Hills CDP instead. 7600 Beechmont Ave, 1000 Nordyke Rd and 1100 Eight Mile Rd fall in Forestville CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits
- **Zoning certificate:** Anderson Township Planning & Zoning Department, Anderson Center, 7850 Five Mile Road. For most improvements it needs two copies of a site plan showing setbacks. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/Portals/andersontownship/Documents/Forms/2022%20Forms/Q%20_%20A%20Planning%20_%20Zoning%202022.pdf
- **Building permits:** The township says that, except for fences and small detached structures, "most improvements also require building permits from the Hamilton County Planning & Development". Same source as above. The county office (Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections) is from _shared.md. Its site returned 403 this session. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **HVAC/mechanical permits:** the county probably issues them, but the only source found is a third-party permit site, so this is unverified. Whether a like-for-like AC or furnace swap also needs a township zoning certificate is unverified.
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health. **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). Both are from _shared.md. https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- Fees and timings are unverified for the county. The township says most residential zoning certificates are issued within two business days (source above), but don't use timings in copy.

## Terrain, flood, historic
- The township calls its terrain hilly with poorly draining soils, which push heavy runoff into Clough Creek, its largest watershed. Whether Dry Run drains to Clough Creek is unverified. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/news-events/news/2020/04/20/diversion-project-to-help-decrease-clough-creek-flooding ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clough_Creek
- Little Miami River floodplain buyouts after the 1997 flood were in Old Fort off Debolt Road, not in Dry Run. FEMA flood zones for Dry Run were not checked, so this is unverified. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/news-events/news/2026/04/02/old-fort-little-miami-river-open-space-planning
- No historic district was found for Dry Run (unverified).
