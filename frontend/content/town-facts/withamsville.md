# Withamsville, OH: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'withamsville'): ZIP 45245, Clermont County, Union Township community on the Ohio Pike corridor in western Clermont County, suburban homes and commercial strips, "mid-century and newer homes". Mostly right; see corrections at the end.

## 1. Where it is
- Unincorporated **census-designated place**, not a village or city. Wikipedia puts it in **Union and Pierce townships**; every address geocoded here fell in Union Township. 2020 population 7,357; 3.13 sq mi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withamsville,_Ohio
- Geocoded: 803 Ohio Pike, Cincinnati 45245 = Withamsville CDP (16000US3986254), tract 412.01, Union Township. 4400 Glen Este-Withamsville Rd (45245) and 530 Ohio Pike (45255) are Union Township but outside the CDP. Mail says "Cincinnati, OH"; ZIPs 45245 and 45255 both reach the area and both are larger than the CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Mostly 1940s-90s housing (see block).

## 3. Permits (unincorporated Union Township)
- **Clermont County Building Inspection** (Permit Central, Batavia) enforces the Ohio building and residential codes in all unincorporated areas of the county. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/building-activity-reports
- **HVAC confirmed:** the county's 2025 permit report lists 308 "Residential / HVAC Replacement" permits in Union Township (township-wide, not CDP-only). https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/BuildingActivityReports/2025/JANUARY%20DECEMBER%202025%20PERMITS%20DETAILED.pdf
- County fee schedule covers mechanical permits for new or replacement heating and cooling, "replacement of furnace and/or condensing unit (includes elect. inspection)", and electrical permits. Fees are a 2019 schedule: don't quote. https://permit.clermontcountyohio.gov/media/FeeSchedule/permit-central-fee-schedule-03-28-19.pdf
- Unpermitted work carries a penalty fee, which the chief building official can waive for emergencies "such as a replacement furnace in winter". Same source.
- **Plumbing:** Clermont County Public Health regulates the Ohio Plumbing Code county-wide. https://clermonthealthohio.gov/services/permits_and_inspections/plumbing/residential_plumbing_permits.php
- Unverified: processing times, current fees. Don't state.
