- The `adjacent` list includes Lower Price Hill as a neighbour. I could not confirm this. City maps show East Price Hill directly north/upslope: https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Sedamsville_2020.1.pdf

## Where it is
- Sedamsville is one of Cincinnati's 52 city neighbourhoods, on the Ohio River on the far west side. Col. Cornelius Sedam settled it in 1795, it incorporated as a village in Storrs Township in 1835, and the city annexed it in 1870. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedamsville,_Cincinnati (to locate the source) ; https://www.cincinnatiport.org/sedamsville-block-by-block/
- River Road (US-50) is the main corridor. Its widening in the 1940s destroyed most of the old business district between Steiner and Sedam Streets. Other streets: Steiner, Delhi and Fairbanks Avenues, Sedam and Edwin Streets, Mt. Hope Ave. https://www.cincinnatiport.org/sedamsville-block-by-block/
- 2020 Census: 1,256 residents and 638 housing units. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Sedamsville_2020.1.pdf
- The terrain is narrow river flats along River Road with steep hillside rising toward Price Hill. USGS says landslides in thin colluvium are "widespread on steeper slopes" of the Ohio valley walls around Cincinnati. https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b2059A

## Housing stock (ACS 2020-2024 5-year, Census Tract 103, Hamilton County)
Tract 103 covers Sedamsville plus the eastern edge of Riverside. Treat it as a close proxy, not an exact match. Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061010300-census-tract-103-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1938. Of 610 units, 444 (72.8%) were built in 1939 or earlier. Only 23 units (3.8%) date from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 242 single-family detached, 90 two-family, 78 in 3-4 unit buildings, 135 in buildings of 10-49 units, and 44 mobile homes.
- Tenure: 328 of 504 occupied units are rented (65.1%) and 176 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 323 utility gas, 149 electricity (29.6%), 32 propane.
- Type: "modest worker cottages with Italianate influences", 19th and early 20th century. The Port is rehabbing 18 single-family homes from 2025 to 2026 and started a critical home-repair programme in early 2026. https://www.cincinnatiport.org/sedamsville-block-by-block/

## What that means for AC (reasonable inferences from the data above)
- About 73% of homes predate 1940, so central air was retrofitted (attic or closet ductwork, or chases cut through plaster) or never installed. Window units and ductless mini-splits are the realistic options in many of them.
- Two-family and small multi-unit buildings (168 units) often need a separate system or condenser for each unit. With a 65% rental rate, the landlord, not the occupant, often approves repairs.
- Old service panels in pre-1940 homes may need an electrical upgrade before a new condenser or heat pump goes in. This is a general inference, not Sedamsville-specific data.

## Permits
- A City of Cincinnati Department of Buildings & Inspections mechanical permit is needed to install or replace an air conditioner. Unpermitted work costs an investigation fee equal to the permit fee. https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/old-apply-for-a-building-permit/permit-guide/residential-permit-guide/ ; https://cincinnati-oh.gov/buildings/building-construction-inspections/mechanical-inspections
- Fees and phone numbers are unverified because the two city pages list different numbers.

## Utility
- The electric utility is Duke Energy Ohio (all of the City of Cincinnati).
- Duke rebates are unverified for Ohio. Duke's HVAC replacement rebate page could not be confirmed as covering Ohio, so don't quote amounts. https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/home-energy-improvement/hvac-replacement
- The federal 25C credit expired 31 Dec 2025, according to a third-party site. Confirm on irs.gov before using it.

## Climate (NWS Wilmington, CVG)
- July normal high 86F, normal low 65-66F (1991-2020 normals). https://www.weather.gov/media/iln/climo_webpage/CVG_Jul.pdf ; https://www.weather.gov/iln/climate_normals_cvg
- On average 21 days a year reach 90F or higher (1981-2010 normals). https://www.weather.gov/iln/90s
- No verified humidity figure was found. Avoid "river humidity" claims unless they're framed generally.

## Other local factors
- Two National Register districts: Sedamsville River Road Historic District (2008; 2449-2734 River Rd, 309-317 Mt. Hope Ave, 604 Mt. Echo Park Dr) and Sedamsville Village Historic District (2012). National Register listing does not by itself trigger city design review. Local designation status was not confirmed. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/08000975 ; https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/planning/historic-conservation/national-register-of-historic-places/nr-districts/
- The 1937 Ohio River flood hit the neighbourhood hard. https://www.cincinnatiport.org/sedamsville-block-by-block/ FEMA flood zone status for individual River Road lots was not checked, so there's no verified claim about condenser elevation.
