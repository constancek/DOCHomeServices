# Forestville (Anderson Township, OH 45230 / 45255): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives one ZIP, 45230. The CDP covers parts of both 45230 and 45255: 7850 Five Mile Rd and 1080 Nimitzview Dr geocode to 45230, while 7500 Forest Rd and 8000 Beechmont Ave geocode to 45255. All four fall inside Forestville CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- "Mid-century and newer homes" is off. The median build year is 1981, and 44.2% of units date from 1980-99. Only 16.3% are from 1940-59. See Housing. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3927776/
- The copy treats Forestville as all houses. In fact 28.4% of units sit in buildings of 5 or more units (760 in buildings of 50 or more), and 35.2% are rented. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3927776/
- That water heaters, supply lines and sump pumps are the most common failures there is unverified.

## Where it is
- Forestville is a census-designated place in unincorporated Anderson Township, Hamilton County, Ohio. It is not a city or village. Area 3.7 sq mi. 2020 Census population 10,615. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestville,_Ohio (to locate the source)
- Census tracts 250.01, 251.02 and 251.03. Anderson Center (township offices, 7850 Five Mile Rd) is inside the CDP. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Permits
- Building permits come from Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections. Plumbing permits come from Hamilton County Public Health. Electrical permits go through Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). From _shared.md. The county page returned 403 again this session. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi ; https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- The office that issues HVAC/mechanical permits in Anderson Township is unverified. Only a third-party permit site says the county handles it, and that isn't an official source. https://permitmint.com/permits/ohio/hamilton-county-oh/hvac/ (not official, don't cite in copy)
- Anderson Township does its own zoning. A township zoning certificate is needed for fences, sheds, decks, pools, driveways, solar panels and additions, new or replacement. HVAC equipment is not on the township's list, so whether a condenser or generator needs one is unverified. https://andersontownshipoh.gov/Portals/andersontownship/Documents/Forms/Residential%20Zoning%20Certificate%20Final.pdf

## Other
