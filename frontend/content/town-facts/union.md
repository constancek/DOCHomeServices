# Union, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**What the site already says** (locations.ts slug 'union'): ZIP 41091, Boone County, "one of the fastest-growing cities in Boone County", "largely newer subdivisions built from the 1990s onward". The housing data strongly backs the newer-subdivision claim. "Fastest-growing" is unverified; use the census counts instead.

## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated home rule-class city in Boone County. 2020 census population 7,416 (2010: 5,379; 1970: 233). Rural until residential growth in the 1990s and 2000s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union,_Kentucky (cites Census)
- The city calls itself the second largest city in Boone County. https://cityofunionky.org/union-is-the-place-to-be-in-boone-county
- ZIP 41091 is much bigger than the city (about 24,820 people, third-party figure). Use city figures for "Union". https://www.zip-codes.com/zip-code/41091/zip-code-41091.asp
- Geocoded inside the city: 1843 Mount Zion Rd, 10165 Old Union Rd, 2000 Hathaway Rd (Census geocoder via census.mjs).
