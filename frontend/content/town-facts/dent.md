# Dent (Green Township, OH 45247/45248): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` says "mid-century to newer" and "established neighborhoods of mid-century and newer homes". The housing is mostly newer: median build year 1995, 42.9% built 2000 or later, and only 11.1% built before 1960. See the housing section. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3921742/
- `locations.ts` gives only ZIP 45247. Dent covers 45247 and 45248. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dent,_Ohio (to locate the source)
- The entry doesn't mention that 25.9% of units are in buildings of 5 or more units, or that 40.9% of homes heat with electricity. Both change the job (condo/apartment access, heat pumps). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3921742/
- The water heater and supply line claims are unverified as local fact (no source).

## Where it is
- Dent is a census-designated place in unincorporated Green Township, Hamilton County. It has no city government. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dent,_Ohio ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- 6300 and 6600 Harrison Ave (ZIP 45247) geocode to Dent CDP, Green Township, tract 206.04. 5800 and 6000 Harrison Ave geocode to Bridgetown CDP (ZIP 45248), so Dent's eastern edge on Harrison is somewhere between 6000 and 6300. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- 2020 population 12,301. Area 5.9 to 6.25 sq mi (Wikipedia gives both figures). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dent,_Ohio
- The Green Township administration office is at 6303 Harrison Ave, Cincinnati 45247, inside Dent. https://greentwp.org/zoning-application

## Terrain and flood
- The name comes from the "valley topography". A UC geologist says the area drains to Taylor Creek and the valley is deeper than its neighbours because of where the creek meets the Great Miami near Miamitown. https://www.uc.edu/news/articles/2024/04/uc-geologist-explains-origins-of-ohio-town-called-dent.html
- FEMA flood zones for Dent weren't checked: unverified. MSD's Muddy Creek Road buyout (Glenway to Westbourne) is most likely outside Dent (not geocoded; unverified), so don't use it here. https://wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/green-township/msd-seeks-to-demolish-35-flood-prone-properties-along-muddy-creek-road
