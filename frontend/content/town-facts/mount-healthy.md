## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County (village until 1951). Founded 1817 as Mount Pleasant; renamed Mount Healthy in 1850 after a cholera epidemic. About 1.4-1.5 sq mi, all land (Wikipedia gives both figures). ZIP 45231. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Healthy,_Ohio
- Bordering communities: unverified (not checked against a map). Mount Healthy Heights is a separate CDP in Colerain Township, not part of the city. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Healthy_Heights,_Ohio
- Geocoded addresses: 7700 Perry St (city building), 1411 Compton Rd, 7615 Harrison Ave all fall in place 16000US3952752 (tracts 217.01, 217.02). Census geocoder via census.mjs.
