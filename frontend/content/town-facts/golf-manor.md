## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **village** in Hamilton County, incorporated February 3, 1947; before that it was part of Columbia Township. Developers laid out the lots in the mid-1920s. https://www.golfmanoroh.gov/our-village/village-history/
- About 0.58 sq mi, all land. 2020 population 3,814. ZIP 45237 (the ZIP also covers Cincinnati neighborhoods, so use village figures, not ZIP figures). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golf_Manor,_Ohio
- Census geocoder confirms 6450 Wiehe Rd and 2200 Losantiville Ave fall in Golf Manor village (tract 234). Run via `census.mjs --geocode`.

## 3. Permits
- **Village Zoning Certificate first:** "A Zoning Certificate must be issued by the Village for all fence, replacement fence, or building projects." After that, Hamilton County issues the building permit. https://www.golfmanoroh.gov/?p=270
- **Building permits:** "Building department services, including building permits, are issued by Hamilton County." The village contracts with Hamilton County for plan review, building permits, electrical permits and inspections. Same source.
- **Plumbing:** the Hamilton County Board of Health (Hamilton County Public Health) does plumbing inspections under contract. Same source.
- **HVAC:** the village page lists "heating and cooling additions or replacements" as work that needs a permit. It doesn't name the office that issues HVAC/mechanical permits. Hamilton County is likely, but **unverified**. Same source.
- Unverified: fees, timings, and whether a like-for-like swap needs the village zoning certificate.
