## Where it is
- One of Cincinnati's 52 neighbourhoods, in Hamilton County, on a narrow strip along the Ohio River between Sedamsville and Sayler Park. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside,_Cincinnati
- It incorporated as a village on 20 August 1867, mostly from Delhi Township land, and Cincinnati annexed it in 1896. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside,_Cincinnati
- US-50 (River Road) runs the full length, beside the main rail line into the city from the west. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside,_Cincinnati
- 2020 Census: 1,257 residents, 672 housing units, 506 households. The city's 2020 profile maps the neighbourhood to Census Tract 104. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Riverside_2020.1.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside,_Cincinnati
- 354 residents live outside households (903 of 1,257 in households); the group quarters were not identified. https://cincinnati-oh.gov/sites/planning/assets/2020%20CENSUS/Riverside_2020.1.pdf

Small-area estimates carry wide margins of error. Source: https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061010400-census-tract-104-hamilton-oh/
- Median year built: 1971. Of 550 units, 142 (25.8%) date from 1939 or earlier, 116 (21.1%) from 1940-1969, 283 (51.5%) from 1970-1999 and 9 (1.6%) from 2000 or later.
- Units in structure: 216 single-family detached, 11 attached, 38 two-family, 26 in 3-4 unit buildings, 60 in 5-9, 113 in 10-19, 35 in 20-49 and 51 in 50+. 199 units (36.2%) are in buildings of 10+.
- Tenure: 326 of 533 occupied units are rented (61.2%) and 207 are owner-occupied.
- Heating fuel: 255 utility gas (47.8%), 224 electricity (42.0%), 32 fuel oil or kerosene (6.0%), 22 propane (4.1%).
- Compared with Sedamsville's tract 103 (median 1938, 29.6% electric heat), Riverside's housing is newer, more apartment-heavy and more electric. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/14000US39061010300-census-tract-103-hamilton-oh/

## Flooding and terrain
- Ohio River at Cincinnati gauge: flood stage 52 ft, moderate 56 ft, major 65 ft. At 50-52 ft NWS says Riverside Park "off River Road" floods. Whether that park is inside Riverside's boundary is unverified. https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1
- At 70 ft (just above the FEMA 1% flood), NWS says "downstream of Cincinnati, most property between Route 50 and the river is flooded". https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1
- Record crest 80 ft (Jan 1937). Recent crests: 60.91 ft (7 April 2025) and 55.46 ft (19 Feb 2025). https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/CCNO1
- FEMA flood zone status for individual Riverside lots was not checked. Unverified.
- USGS says landslides in thin colluvium are "widespread on steeper slopes" of the Ohio valley around Cincinnati, which includes the hillside above River Road. https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b2059A
- No National Register or local historic district in Riverside was found. Unverified, not ruled out.
