## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated city in Kenton County, Kentucky, on the Ohio River west of Covington. Geocoded 51 Elm St and 227 Elm St, Ludlow, KY 41016 to place "Ludlow city" (16000US2148378), tract 669. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 41016 is shared with Bromley (Bromley addresses geocode to 41016 too). Same source.

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits: **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Ludlow by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- Plumbing: state DHBC inspector. Same source; from _shared.md.
- Electrical: state list says contact the County Judge Executive or the city office. Who issues electrical permits in Ludlow: **unverified**. Same source.
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/

## 4. Historic district (local AND National Register)
- **Local:** Ludlow has a Historic Preservation Overlay. Exterior alterations, additions, new construction or demolition in the Ludlow Historic District need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Urban Design Review Board (UDRB) or the Historic Preservation Officer before work starts. Painting and ordinary repairs are not "alterations". https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/ludlow/latest/ludlow_ky/0-0-0-7966 (site returned 403; text from search excerpt) ; https://www.ludlow.org/Portals/ludlow/Documents/Ordinance%20%202021-2%20Zoning%20Ordinance%20Amendments.pdf
- Guidelines reportedly apply only to street-facing facades, not sides that don't face a street: **unverified** (PDF redirected, seen only in a search excerpt). Whether a condenser needs a certificate: **unverified**. https://www.ludlow.org/Portals/ludlow/Documents/Ord.%202021-1%20Exhibit%20A%20-%20Revised%20Historic%20Guidelines.pdf
- **National Register:** Central Ludlow Historic District, listed 1984-11-28 (ref 84000526), roughly Glenwood, Church, Adela and Carneal Sts, 367 contributing buildings. https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/64000238_text ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_Kenton_County,_Kentucky

## 5. Flood
- NWS Cincinnati gauge: flood stage 52 ft; record crest 80.0 ft on 1937-01-26. https://www.weather.gov/iln/1937ohioriverflood
- April 2025 (Ohio at 60.7 ft per NWS): "many of the low-lying portions of the city's north end" partly under water; football stadium and Memorial Park under water; KY-8 between Ludlow and Bromley closed. No Ludlow basement count given. https://linknky.com/news/2025/04/07/region-hit-with-historic-flooding-what-to-know-how-to-help/
- 2018 high water: 140 homes and businesses affected across Bromley and Ludlow combined. https://local12.com/news/local/cleaning-up-after-high-flood-water-hits-nky-communities
- Floodwall: **unverified**.
