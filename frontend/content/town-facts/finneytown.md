# Finneytown (Springfield Township, OH 45231 / 45224): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- `locations.ts` gives ZIP 45231 only. The CDP spans 45224 and 45231. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneytown,_Ohio
- "Mid-century single-family homes" holds up: median build year 1959, 52.5% built 1940-59, 88.9% single-family detached (see housing). https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3927104/
- "Decades-old pipe" and "prone to corrosion leaks and failing water heaters" are unverified. No source checked supports them.
- Not every 45231 address on the main roads is in Finneytown. 1100 Compton Rd geocodes to Pleasant Hills CDP (tract 221.01), not Finneytown. Check each address before calling it Finneytown. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

## Where it is
- Census-designated place (unincorporated) in Springfield Township, Hamilton County, just north of Cincinnati. Area 3.98 sq mi, all land; elevation 840 ft; 2020 Census population 12,399. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneytown,_Ohio
- It has no government of its own. The only legal body with the name is the Finneytown Local School District. Locals put its centre at Winton and Galbraith Roads. https://finneytown.org/about/history-of-finneytown/
- 8916 Fontainebleau Ter and 8400 Winton Rd geocode to Finneytown CDP, tract 221.02. 1101 W Galbraith Rd geocodes to Finneytown CDP, tract 111. All three are in Springfield Township. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Township offices: 9150 Winton Road, Cincinnati, OH 45231. https://www.springfieldtwp.org/

## Permits
- **Building permits:** issued by Hamilton County, not the township. The township page says to "apply for a building permit, which are issued by the Hamilton County Building Department" and links to Hamilton County Buildings and Inspections. https://www.springfieldtwp.org/144/Building-Permits
- **Furnace or air conditioning:** the township's "Who to Call" cheat sheet lists "Furnace or Air Conditioning" under Hamilton County. It doesn't name the county office or use the word "mechanical", and it calls itself non-exhaustive. Safe wording: "Hamilton County handles furnace and AC permits, per the township." The exact county office is unverified. https://www.springfieldtwp.org/144/Building-Permits
- **Plumbing and electrical:** the same cheat sheet lists them under Hamilton County. From _shared.md: plumbing goes through Hamilton County Public Health, and electrical through Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI). https://www.springfieldtwp.org/144/Building-Permits ; https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- **Zoning:** "Springfield Township is responsible for the issuance of zoning permits." Whether a condenser or other HVAC equipment needs a zoning permit is unverified. https://www.springfieldtwp.org/144/Building-Permits ; https://www.springfieldtwp.org/141/Planning-and-Zoning
- Fees and timings: unverified.

## Utilities
- **Electric:** Duke Energy Ohio delivers power (from _shared.md, Hamilton County). The township runs an electric aggregation: an April 2025 notice set the rate at $0.0945/kWh through broker Energy Alliances. The current supplier and rate are unverified, so don't quote them. https://springfieldtwp.org/CivicAlerts.asp?AID=480
- **Gas aggregation:** AEP Energy, November 2025 to October 2026, per a township notice. Not useful for copy. https://springfieldtwp.org/CivicAlerts.asp?AID=486
- **Sewer:** the township's links point to MSD of Greater Cincinnati. Whether MSD serves every Finneytown address is unverified. https://www.springfieldtwp.org/
