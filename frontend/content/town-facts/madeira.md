## 1. Where it is
- Incorporated **city** in Hamilton County (village 1910, city August 1959). Nearly doubled in area in 1970 by annexing South Kenwood from Columbia Township. About 3.4 sq mi; 2020 census population 9,487. ZIP 45243. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira,_Ohio
- ZIP 45243 is bigger than the city (6,398 housing units vs 3,620). Use city figures for "Madeira". https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US45243-45243/

## 3. Permits (City of Madeira)
- The **City of Madeira Building Department** (7141 Miami Avenue) issues a combined Building and Zoning Permit. The form has a "Heating/Air: New / Replacement, furnace / air / both" box, so furnace and AC replacements go on the city permit. Form also asks whether the property is in a floodplain. https://cms2.revize.com/revize/madeiraoh/Building%20and%20Zoning/Permit%20Applications/MBD01_Bldg_Rev11.pdf
- City page (search excerpt; site blocked direct fetch): all building permits go through the city "except for electrical and plumbing"; exterior HVAC is listed as needing a permit. https://www.madeiracity.com/government/departments/building___zoning/index.php
- Madeira is not one of Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections' contract jurisdictions. https://hamilton.hosted.civiclive.com/residents/residential_development/building_and_development_process
- **Unverified:** who issues electrical and plumbing permits in Madeira (likely Hamilton County Public Health for plumbing, not confirmed). Also unverified: fees, processing times. Don't state them.
- Construction hours on the city form: Mon-Fri 7am-7pm, Sat 8am-4pm, no Sundays or listed holidays (Sec. 132.20). (same form URL)
