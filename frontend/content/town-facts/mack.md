# Mack (Green Township, Hamilton County, OH 45248): verified local facts

## Fix in the existing site copy first
- The `mack` entry in `locations.ts` already says `state: 'OH'` and `county: 'Hamilton County, OH'`. That part is correct, and nothing in the file lists Mack as Kentucky. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- It calls the homes "mid-century and newer". Most are newer: the median year built is 1981, 42.7% date from 1980-99, and only 21.8% are from before 1960 (see Housing). Lead with 1980s-90s homes, not mid-century pipe. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US3946088/
- It lists ZIP 45248 only. Wikipedia gives 45211 and 45248, but every address I geocoded was in 45248, so 45211 is unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mack,_Ohio
- The claims about water heaters, supply lines and "corrosion leaks" are not sourced (unverified).

## Where it is
- Mack CDP is unincorporated, in Hamilton County. Wikipedia says it spans Green and Miami townships. Every address I checked fell in Green Township, so the Miami Township part is unverified at the address level. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mack,_Ohio ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- 6300 and 6800 Bridgetown Rd and 3700 Mack Ave all geocode to Mack CDP, Green Township, tract 211.01, ZIP 45248. Bridgetown Rd (OH 264) is the main road. https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- Neighbours: 5900 Bridgetown Rd is in Bridgetown CDP, east of Mack, and 4300 Ebenezer Rd is in Dent CDP to the north. Wikipedia also lists Covedale (south-east), Delhi Township (south) and North Bend and Miami Heights (west). https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mack,_Ohio
- 2020 population 11,088. Area 8.91 sq mi, elevation 830 ft. It was called Dry Ridge in the 1800s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mack,_Ohio

## Permits (from _shared.md, plus town checks)
- **Building permits:** Hamilton County Buildings + Inspections handles unincorporated townships. The county page returned 403 this session, so this rests on a search snippet only. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **HVAC/mechanical:** a search summary says the county also issues mechanical permits for unincorporated townships, Green Township among them. I couldn't open the county page to confirm, so this is unverified. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **Plumbing:** Hamilton County Public Health covers the county "excluding the City of Cincinnati". https://hamiltoncountyhealth.org/services/plumbing/plumbing-permits/
- **Electrical:** Inspection Bureau, Inc. (IBI), from _shared.md. https://www.hamiltoncountyohio.gov/hcbi
- **Zoning:** Green Township issues zoning certificates only under its Northeast Green Township Zoning Code. Elsewhere Hamilton County zones, and the certificate has to come before the building permit. Mack is in the south-west of the township, so county zoning is likely, but that's unverified. https://greentwp.org/zoning-application ; https://hamilton-co.org/i_want_to/pre_development_meeting
- Fees and timings are unverified. Don't use them.
