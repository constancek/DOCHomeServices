# Elsmere, KY: verified facts (service-neutral)

**Errors in locations.ts (slug 'elsmere'):**
- "along the Dixie Highway corridor": Dixie Highway is Elsmere's northern border, and Dixie Highway addresses that mail as "Elsmere, KY 41018" can sit inside **Erlanger** city limits. 3500 and 4000 Dixie Hwy, Elsmere both geocode to Erlanger city (tract 643). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsmere,_Kentucky ; https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/
- ZIP 41018 is shared with Erlanger, so ZIP data mixes the two cities. https://censusreporter.org/profiles/86000US41018-41018/
- "older and mid-century homes" is fair (median 1970), but 19.6% of units date from 2000 or later and 344 are mobile homes (section 2).
- "corrosion leaks" as a local pattern: **unverified**.

## 1. Where it is
- Home rule-class city, mayor-council, Kenton County, ZIP 41018. 2020 population 9,159; 2.64 sq mi. Settled 1885 as "South Erlanger", organized as a city in 1896. Borders Erlanger (north and east), Independence (south) and Florence in Boone County (west). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsmere,_Kentucky

## 3. Permits
- Building and HVAC permits: **Planning and Development Services of Kenton County (PDS)**, 1840 Simon Kenton Way #3400, Covington. The state list names PDS as local building and HVAC inspector and lists Elsmere by name. https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/Kenton%20County.pdf (updated 10/20/2026)
- An address with an Elsmere mailing address may legally be in Erlanger; Erlanger is also under PDS on the same list, so the permit office is the same. Same source.
- Plumbing: state DHBC plumbing inspector (Boone County Health Center, Florence). Same source.
- Electrical: local office **unverified** (state list says contact the county or the city). Same source.
- Kentucky requires a permit to replace a furnace, condensing unit or heat pump. https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/815/008/070/
