## Introduction

After dealing with an old and inefficient heating system for many years, I decided it was time for a full **furnace replacement**. I contacted **Degree of Comfort** for help upgrading my outdated **fuel oil furnace** to a modern **electric furnace and HVAC system**.

My main goal was better **indoor air quality**, improved efficiency, and a more reliable home heating and cooling solution.

## The Problem

My old **fuel oil-burning furnace** had been running for nearly 28 years. Over time, it started affecting the air quality in my home and became less reliable. I was also concerned about dust, discomfort, and rising inefficiency.

I knew I needed a full **HVAC system upgrade**, not just a repair.

## Choosing Degree of Comfort

I found **Degree of Comfort HVAC services** through recommendations and local reputation. They were well known for **furnace installation in residential homes** and full system replacements, including **ductwork, AC, and heat pump installation**.

What stood out most was their reputation for handling complete **HVAC replacement projects in a single installation process**.

## The Installation Experience

From the beginning, everything was clearly explained. The team outlined the full scope of work, including removal of the old **fuel oil furnace, ductwork, AC, and heat pump system**, and installation of a new **electric furnace with upgraded HVAC components**.

Communication throughout the project was handled through phone and email, which made everything easy to follow.

The crew worked efficiently and professionally. They removed the old system, installed the new equipment, and ensured the ductwork was properly sealed and secured.

Even after installation, when a small adjustment was needed with a vent connection, the team returned quickly and corrected it. They also performed a full inspection of the **ductwork and HVAC sealing system** to ensure everything was properly optimized.

## Additional Upgrades & Improvements

During the project, I also upgraded my system with a **humidifier and air filtration system**, which significantly improved **indoor air quality**. This helped reduce dust, static shocks, and overall dryness in the home.

The team also made sure the new outdoor AC unit had proper spacing and ventilation, which required minor adjustments on my property.

## The Results

The difference after installation was immediate.

My home now has:

* Better **air quality and cleaner indoor environment**
* More consistent and efficient heating and cooling
* Less dust buildup
* Improved sleep and overall comfort
* Reduced maintenance concerns

The new **electric furnace system** has made a noticeable improvement in everyday comfort.
