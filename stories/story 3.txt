## Introduction

My old water heater had been in use for over 20 years, and it had started becoming unreliable. I reached out to **Degree of Comfort Plumbing Services** to look into replacing it with a more efficient solution. That’s when I learned about switching to a **tankless water heater system**.

## The Problem

The biggest issue was inconsistency. Some days we had hot water, other times it ran out quickly or took too long to heat up. The system was also clearly becoming less efficient and likely costing more to run.

At that point, I knew I needed a proper **water heater replacement**, not another repair.

## Why I chose Degree of Comfort

I first came across **Degree of Comfort Plumbing** at a local home and garden show. After speaking with their team and learning more about their **tankless water heater installation services**, I felt confident they knew what they were doing.

What stood out was how straightforward they were about the process and what needed to be done.

## The Installation Experience

From the beginning, everything was clearly explained. They walked me through the process of removing the old unit and installing the new **tankless water heater system**, including how it would improve efficiency and hot water availability.

The team handled the full job — removal of the old water heater, installation of the new system, and cleanup afterward. They worked efficiently and made sure everything was set up properly before leaving.

Communication was also solid throughout the process, mainly through email and phone, which made everything easy to follow.

There were no major issues during installation, and everything went smoothly from start to finish.

## The Results

The difference after switching to a **tankless water heater** was noticeable right away.

* We now have consistent hot water whenever we need it
* No more running out of hot water mid-use
* The system heats water more efficiently
* Overall performance feels much more reliable

It’s also reassuring knowing the system should require less maintenance in the long run and help with energy efficiency.

## Final Thoughts

Working with **Degree of Comfort Plumbing** was a smooth experience from start to finish. The installation was quick, the communication was clear, and everything was handled professionally.

I’d definitely recommend them to anyone thinking about a **water heater replacement or upgrading to a tankless water heater system**.
